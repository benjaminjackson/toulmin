**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Ending display advertising and launching reader membership in January 2027

**Request**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising on January 1, 2027, and making reader membership our main source of earned revenue.

**Why now**
Display ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 over two years, a 36% drop. If that decline continues, ads would bring in about $67,000 in 2027. We are putting staff time into revenue that keeps shrinking.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week spring pilot, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. Our only promotion was two newsletter mentions. That is about 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers and about $20,000 a year at current levels. A newsroom of similar size in [state] reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget [over X years].

**The target**
Our goal is to replace ad revenue within 12 months. Matching last year's $84,000 takes about 875 members. Matching the roughly $67,000 that ads would likely earn in 2027 takes about 700. This is a target, not a guarantee. The pilot was short and unmarketed, so we don't yet know how sign-ups grow with sustained effort or how many members will cancel. The plan below is built to answer those questions quickly.

**Plan and accountability**
- Launch campaign: [channels, timeline, budget]
- Monthly report to the board on total members, new sign-ups, cancellations and revenue
- Checkpoints: [X] members by March 31 and [Y] by June 30. If we fall more than [Z]% short in June, I will bring the board options, including bringing back sponsorships.

**Covering the gap**
Membership revenue will trail ad revenue for much of 2027. If members grow steadily to 875, membership would bring in about $55,000 next year. That is $12,000–$29,000 less than ads would have earned, plus campaign costs of $[ ]. I propose covering this through [reserves / bridge grant / other].

**Decision requested**
Approve ending display advertising effective January 1, 2027, launching the membership program, and the gap funding above, with a formal board review in July 2027.
