# Blind check: 10 pairs

Each pair has two documents written for the same request. One was written with the skill and one without. The order is random.

For each pair, read as the reader described, then write down which document is more likely to bring you to agree or act: A, B or tie. Do not open `answer-key.md` until you have marked all 10.
