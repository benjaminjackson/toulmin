Good evening, commissioners. My name is Margaret Shaw. I've lived on Alder Road for 19 years, two blocks from St. Luke's Lutheran Church. I'm a member of St. Luke's, but tonight I'm speaking for myself. I'm asking you to approve the permit for 64 affordable apartments on the church's back parking lot, with a traffic signal at Alder and 4th as a condition of approval.

The county's own housing report says we are short about 2,100 affordable homes. Behind that number are 412 households on the housing authority's waiting list, and the average wait is three years. Every one of these 64 apartments would rent to a household earning under 60 percent of the area median income. That's enough to house about one in six of the households on that list.

This project won't close a gap of 2,100 homes by itself. No single project could. But a gap like that closes one site at a time, and this is an easy site. It's 1.8 acres of pavement that sits empty six days a week.

I know the traffic concerns, because I live with them. Some mornings around eight, cars already back up past my house. The traffic study estimates 310 more car trips a day on Alder Road. It also says that, left as it is, the intersection at Alder and 4th would drop from a C to a D at rush hour. The builder has offered to pay for a signal at that intersection, and the same study says the signal keeps it at a C. That's why I'm asking you to make the signal a condition of the permit. As a condition, it's a requirement, not just an offer.

On parking, the plan has 72 spaces for 64 units. The church has also committed in writing to let neighbors use the lot on weekdays when there's space.

Construction will take about 20 months, and there will be trucks on Alder Road. I won't pretend that will be easy, and I can't promise you traffic won't get worse while it's going on. I'll be serving on the neighbor group the church has promised to set up during construction. I'm not asking my neighbors to put up with anything I won't be living with too.

So here is the choice as I see it: twenty hard months on my street, or more years of waiting for 412 households who have already waited too long. I'll take the trucks. Please approve the permit, with the signal as a condition. Thank you.
