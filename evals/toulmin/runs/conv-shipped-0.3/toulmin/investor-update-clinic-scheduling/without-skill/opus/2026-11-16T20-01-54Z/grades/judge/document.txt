**What happened**
We grew this quarter, but one loss cancelled out that growth. Our largest customer was a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500 a month, about 13% of our revenue. They moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. The 9 new clinics made up for that revenue, so we stayed flat instead of going down.

This is a risk for us. When a clinic's records vendor offers its own scheduler, a scheduling product on its own is easy to replace, especially for large multi-site groups that want everything in one system.

**What's working**
Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a 7% no-show rate. Before they turned reminders on, it was 13%. That cuts no-shows almost in half. Every no-show is an appointment slot the clinic can't bill, so this is the clearest value we deliver.

**What's next**
Next month we start selling text reminders on their own for $150 a month. This lets us sell to clinics that already use their records system's scheduler, including clinics like the chain we just lost. They get the no-show reduction without switching schedulers.
