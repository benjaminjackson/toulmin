The email is about 175 words. Some notes on what I chose:

- **The credit rule:** The email says the credit applies only when weather cuts a box by more than a third. I didn't write "you'll get credit for any loss," because that would promise more than you're offering.
- **The new field:** You didn't know if it will be producing this season, so the email only says you have it. It doesn't claim the field will protect this year's harvest.
- **The survey:** I used the actual numbers (29 of 41) instead of "most of you." Readers can see you listened, and the email doesn't suggest the other 56 people said the same thing.
- **The 16-hour days:** I kept this to one line and followed it straight away with "that's no excuse." Readers learn what was happening without it sounding like you're defending yourself.
