Subject: About last July, and what's changed

Hi,

On July 9, hail destroyed about half our crops. For six weeks, your boxes held about half of what they should have. We gave no credit, and I sent one short email. I was working 16-hour days to save what was left, but you deserved better than that, and I'm sorry.

Most of you who answered our fall survey said you'd come back if we fixed how we handle bad weeks. Here's what we've done:

- Any week that weather cuts a box by more than a third, you get credit for it.
- I'll email all members every Thursday of the season.
- You can pause up to 2 weeks and get credit for them.
- We spent $31,000 on a second field on higher ground and hail nets over the tomatoes and peppers. The greens still aren't covered, and I can't promise a season without crop loss.

A full share is still $540, the same as last year.

We have 52 shares left, and sign-up closes March 15. If you'd like one, the link is on our website.

I hope you'll give us another season.
