Dear Admissions Committee,

I am writing to recommend Tomás Reyes for the two-year agricultural technology program. I am the farm manager at Hollis Dairy, a 400-cow operation, and I have supervised Tomás for the three years he has worked here, since he was 19.

Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift on his own, five days a week. Nobody else is there at that hour, so the shift depends on him showing up and doing the work right. In three years he has missed two shifts.

He also learned our herd management software, and he now uses it to flag sick cows for our vet. Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early. Our vet estimates that saved the farm about $8,000. He learned this on the job, without formal training. Your program teaches the knowledge behind that work, and I believe he will put it to use.

There are two other things you should know. Last summer his father fell seriously ill in Mexico, and Tomás was away for five weeks. He gave me two days' notice. Before he left, he arranged for his cousin, who had worked for us before, to cover his shifts, and he came back on the day he said he would. He took care of his family without leaving us short, and I count that in his favor.

His spoken English is good. His written English is weaker, and he knows it. He is taking a night class to improve it. He once failed the written test in a dairy safety course, then passed it on his second try. He did not let that stop him, and I don't expect written work in your program to stop him either.

I will keep his job open for him part-time on weekends while he is enrolled.

I would be glad to speak with the committee. Please call me at 555-0187.

Sincerely,

Mike Hollis
