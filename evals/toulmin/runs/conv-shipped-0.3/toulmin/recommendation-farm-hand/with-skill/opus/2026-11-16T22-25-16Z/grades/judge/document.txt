To the Admissions Committee:

I am the farm manager at Hollis Dairy, a 400-cow dairy farm. I am writing to recommend Tomás Reyes for your two-year agricultural technology program. Tomás has worked for us for three years, since he was 19. He already does technical work with livestock, and he has shown he can learn new systems on the job.

Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift on his own, five days a week. He also learned our herd management software and now uses it to flag sick cows for our vet. Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early, and our vet estimates that catching them early saved us about $8,000. That is the kind of work your program trains people for, and he is already doing it.

He is dependable. In three years he has missed two shifts. The one long absence came last summer, when his father fell sick in Mexico and Tomás was away for five weeks. He told me two days before he left. In those two days he got his cousin, who used to work for us, to cover his shifts. He came back on the day he said he would. I count the way he handled that as a mark in his favor.

You should know that his written English is weaker than his spoken English. He knows it, and he is taking a night class to improve it. He once failed the written test in a dairy safety course. He did not let that stop him: he took it again and passed.

While he is in your program, I will keep his job open for him part-time on weekends, so he can keep working while he studies. I am glad to talk with anyone on the committee about him. You can reach me at 555-0187.

I recommend Tomás strongly.

Sincerely,

Mike Hollis
Farm Manager, Hollis Dairy
