Admissions Committee
Agricultural Technology Program

Dear Admissions Committee,

I am the farm manager at Hollis Dairy, a 400-cow dairy. I am writing to recommend Tomás for your two-year agricultural technology program. He has worked for us for three years, since he was 19, and I recommend him without reservation.

Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift by himself, five days a week. No one else is on the farm at that hour to check his work or cover for him. In three years he has missed two shifts.

He has also become the person who gets the most out of our herd management software. He learned the system and now uses it to flag sick cows for our vet. Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early, and our vet estimates that this saved the farm about $8,000. Your program trains people to use data to manage animal health, and Tomás is already doing that work.

You should know about one absence. Last summer his father fell ill in Mexico, and Tomás was away for five weeks. He told me two days before he left, which was short notice. Before he went, though, he arranged for his cousin, a former employee of ours, to cover his shifts. He came back on the day he said he would. I count that as evidence of his reliability.

His spoken English is stronger than his written English. He knows this and is taking a night class to improve it. He failed the written test in a dairy safety course the first time and passed it on his second attempt. I expect the written coursework to take him more effort than the hands-on work. I also expect him to put that effort in, because that is how he has handled everything we have asked of him.

We want to keep him. While he is in your program, I will keep his job open part-time on weekends.

I am glad to talk with the committee about him. You can reach me at 555-0187.

Sincerely,

Mike Hollis
Farm Manager, Hollis Dairy
