**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31, and our new Member Phone and Chat Center**

Team,

I want you to hear this directly from me. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. All 14 people who work at those branches are being offered a job in our new Member Phone and Chat Center, at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're making this change**

How our members reach us has changed. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. We haven't kept up. Members who call us today wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and it puts pressure on everyone who answers those calls.

The new center puts experienced people where members need them most. Our Maple Street and Route 9 staff know our members and our products, and that experience will make a real difference on the phone and in chat.

**What the new role looks like**

- **Pay:** The same as you earn today, or higher.
- **Hours:** The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., Monday through Friday, and shifts will rotate. This is a real change from the 9-to-5 branch schedule, and I know it will matter more to some of you than to others. [We will share how shift scheduling works by DATE.]
