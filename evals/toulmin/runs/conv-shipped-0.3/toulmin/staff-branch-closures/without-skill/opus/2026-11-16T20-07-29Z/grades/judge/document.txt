**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches, and our new member phone and chat center**

Team,

I want you to hear this from me first. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. At the same time, we're opening a new member phone and chat center. Every one of the 14 people who work at those two branches will be offered a job there at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're making this change**

Our members have changed how they reach us. Over the last three years, visits to these two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union went up 60%. Members who call us now wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. That's not the service we want to give, and it puts pressure on everyone answering those calls today.

The new center puts more of our people where our members are. We expect it to cut wait times and take some of the load off colleagues across the credit union.

**What this means for Maple Street and Route 9 staff**

- **Pay:** The same as now or higher.
- **Hours:** The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. The branches are open 9 to 5. Shifts will rotate, so some of your shifts will start earlier or end later than they do now. I know this is a real change, especially if you have family or other commitments. Please tell us what you need, and we'll work with you where we can.
- **Working from home:** After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **If you decide the role isn't right for you:** You'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

**What happens next**

Your manager will meet with each of you one-on-one by [DATE]. They'll go through your offer, answer your questions, and talk through your schedule. We'll ask for your decision by [DATE], and training begins [DATE]. If you'd like a confidential conversation, you can contact [HR CONTACT NAME, EMAIL/PHONE] at any time.

**For everyone else**

[Our other five branches aren't affected by this change.] As the center gets going, we hope you'll see fewer calls on hold and more time to spend with the members who come through your doors.

Maple Street and Route 9 have served our members well for years, and the people who work there are a big reason why. Decisions like this are never easy. I'm grateful to each of you, and I'm confident that the skill and care you bring to members will carry over to the new center.

If you have questions, my door is open.
