**Subject:** Maple Street and Route 9 branches will close March 31

Team,

I'm writing about a decision that affects all of us, and most of all the 14 colleagues at our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Both branches will close on March 31. The decision is final. Every person who works at those two branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center at the same pay or higher.

Here is why. Over the last three years, visits to those two branches fell 44%. Over the same three years, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Members who call us today wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. If we change nothing, that wait will keep growing. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we need our people where our members are.

This change protects every job and puts us where our members need us. For many of you, it also brings flexibility a branch job couldn't offer.

Here is what changes. The center is at our main office on Elm Avenue, so some of you will have a different commute. The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, which is longer than the branches' 9-to-5 hours, and shifts will rotate. I can't promise anyone 9-to-5 hours. I can promise that staff with the most years here will choose their shifts first. After 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week.

Many of you know our phone team lost about 30% of its people last year. We don't want that to happen again. That is why pay is protected, why shifts are chosen by seniority, and why the decision to join the center comes with a way out.

For our colleagues at Maple Street and Route 9:

- Written offers will go out by February 14.
- Your decision is due March 1.
- If you decline the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.
- If you take the role and decide within six months that it isn't right for you, you can still leave with that same severance.
- Anyone who wants a one-on-one with HR before deciding can have one.

Some details are still being worked out, including how the shift rotation will run. I'll share them as soon as they're settled, before anyone has to decide.

Closing a branch is hard for the people who built it and for the members who know you by name. Thank you for the work you've done there. I'm glad we're keeping every one of you.

Chief Operating Officer
</final_document>
