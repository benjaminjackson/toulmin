**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31**

To all Northfield staff,

I'm writing to share a decision that directly affects many of you. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Every one of the 14 people who work at those branches will be offered a job in a new member phone and chat center at our main office on Elm Avenue, at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're doing this.** Over the last three years, visits to those two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Members who call us today wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. Our members have changed how they reach us, and right now we aren't keeping up. The decision to close the two branches is final.

**What the new role offers.** Pay in the center starts at $1.50 an hour above current teller pay. After 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week. You'll also be helping members who are waiting for help right now.

**What it asks of you.** The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. I can't promise anyone 9-to-5 hours. Staff with the most years here will pick their shifts first. I don't have shift lengths or schedules yet. Phone and chat work is also different from branch work, and I won't pretend otherwise: our current phone team lost about 30% of its people last year. I take that number seriously.

**Your choice and your timeline.**
- Written offers go out by February 14.
- Decisions are due March 1.
- If you decide not to take the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.
- If you take the role and decide within six months that it isn't right for you, you can still leave with the same severance.

That last point matters to me. I'd rather you try the role knowing you can leave than turn it down because you're unsure.

If you'd like to talk this through privately, you can book a one-on-one meeting with HR. Bring your questions, and tell them what would make this a job you'd want to stay in.

You've built strong relationships with members at Maple Street and Route 9, and closing those branches isn't easy for any of us. I hope many of you will bring that experience to the center, where members need it.

Rachel Greene
Chief Operating Officer
Northfield Community Credit Union
