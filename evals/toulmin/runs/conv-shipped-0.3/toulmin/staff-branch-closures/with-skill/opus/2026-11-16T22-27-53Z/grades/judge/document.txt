**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches will close March 31**

Team,

I'm writing to share a decision that affects all of us, and most directly our 14 colleagues at Maple Street and Route 9. Both branches will close on March 31. Everyone who works there will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're doing this**

Members have changed how they reach us. Over the last three years, visits to these two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Members who call us now wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. That's too long, and the people answering those calls have been handling more contacts every year. This move puts 14 experienced colleagues where members are now trying to reach us.

**What the new role looks like**

- The center is at our main office on Elm Avenue.
- Pay starts $1.50 an hour above current teller pay. No one will be offered less than they earn today.
- The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. That's a longer day than the branches' 9-to-5 hours, and shifts will rotate, so some shifts will run into the evening. Staff with the most years here pick their shifts first.
- After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.

**What happens next**

- Written offers go out by February 14.
- Decisions are due March 1.
- If you decide not to take the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.
- If you take the role and decide within six months that it isn't right for you, you can still leave with that same severance. You don't have to make a final decision before you've tried the job.

**Something I know is on people's minds**

Our phone team lost about 30% of its people last year. Many of you know that, and I won't pretend otherwise. Adding 14 experienced people makes that team substantially bigger. The six-month option means no one who joins it is locked in.

This decision covers these two branches. I don't know of any plans to close others.

**If you have questions**

Anyone who wants a one-on-one meeting with HR can have one. Please ask for it.

To everyone at Maple Street and Route 9: you've served members in those communities well, and that experience is exactly what members calling us need. I hope you'll join the center.

[Your name]
Chief Operating Officer
