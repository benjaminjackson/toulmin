**The light has to burn every night. Can your family last until spring?**

On a rocky stretch of coast, a lighthouse keeper's family faces the longest season of the year. Storms roll in. Supplies run low. And every night, no matter what, the lamp at the top of the tower has to be lit. Ships are counting on it, and so is everyone inside.

**Lantern Winter** is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You don't play against each other. You win or lose together as one family. Each day you decide who tends the light, who keeps the household going, and what you can afford to give up before the thaw.

**Built at the table**

This game wasn't designed in one burst of inspiration. It came from three years of watching people play it.
