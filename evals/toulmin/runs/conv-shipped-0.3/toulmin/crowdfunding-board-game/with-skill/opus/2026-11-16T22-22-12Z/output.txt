**Lantern Winter**

Lantern Winter is a cooperative board game for one to four players. Together, you see a lighthouse keeper's family through a winter.

I'm Owen Hale. This is my first published game. I work full time as a land surveyor, and I've spent the last few years making this game as good as I can. Here's what that work looks like, and what I'm promising you.

I've taken Lantern Winter to two conventions. On the main board game ratings site, 23 people have rated it, with an average of 8.1.
