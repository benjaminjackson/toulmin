**Lantern Winter**

Lantern Winter is a cooperative board game for one to four players. Together, you see a lighthouse keeper's family through a winter.

I'm Owen Hale. This is my first published game. I work full time as a land surveyor, and I've spent the last few years making this game as good as I can. Here's what that work looks like, and what I'm promising you.

**Tested, not just designed**

Since 2023, Lantern Winter has been through 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. Every session showed me something to fix, and I fixed it. The art and the rules are now finished. The money raised here goes to manufacturing and shipping, not to finishing the game.

I've taken Lantern Winter to two conventions. On the main board game ratings site, 23 people have rated it, with an average of 8.1. Most of those ratings came from people who played at my table at those conventions. That's a small, early sample, and I'd rather tell you than have you find out. What it shows is simple: people who sat down and played it liked it.

You don't have to rely on them alone. A board game reviewer with about 90,000 YouTube subscribers has a copy, and their video comes out during launch week. You can watch it while the campaign is still running.

And 1,240 of you signed up to hear about this launch before there was anything to back. Thank you.

Lantern Winter ships in November 2027. I can't promise sooner, so I won't.

I'm committing to two things:

Being a first-time creator is a fair reason to hesitate. I can't change that, but I can make sure you're never left guessing and never stuck if things go wrong.
