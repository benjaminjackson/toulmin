**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food Recovery**

Our volunteer-run food bank serves 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we distribute about 38,000 pounds of food. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs, the foods that matter most for a healthy diet and that families on tight budgets find hardest to afford.

**The Need**

Fresh food is available to us. What we lack is a way to move it. Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or keep it cold. That food was grown a short drive from the families we serve, and we could not get it to them.

**The Project**

We are requesting $50,000 toward the purchase of a $68,000 refrigerated van. With it, our volunteers can collect produce directly from farms and transport it at safe temperatures, so that when a farm calls with extra food, we can say yes.

We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. For an organization our size, this is a serious commitment: it will bring our reserves down to $11,000. We are making it because the van closes the biggest gap between the food available in our community and the families who need it.

**Capacity**

The food bank runs entirely on volunteers, with 23 who serve regularly. Four of them have already committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so pickups will not depend on any one person. The farms that offered produce last summer give us a ready list of partners to contact first.

**Expected Results**

Based on last summer's offers, we expect the van to let us accept about 9,000 additional pounds of fresh produce in its first year. During the harvest months, that would raise our fresh food distribution by about half, from roughly 6,000 to 9,000 pounds a month. Over the full year, it would increase the fresh food we provide by about 12 percent. As we build relationships with more farms and other donors, we expect that amount to grow.

Each month we will track how many pounds of fresh food the van collects and how many we distribute, and we will report those figures to the Foundation.

**Our Partnership with the Foundation**

This would be the Foundation's second investment in our work, following its $12,000 grant for shelving in 2023. A refrigerated van would let us turn food we currently have to refuse into food on our neighbors' tables.
