**Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food**

Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We turned it all down because we had no way to pick it up and keep it cold.

**Who we are**
Millbrook Community Pantry is run entirely by volunteers. Each month we distribute about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

**Our record with the Foundation**
The Foundation granted us $12,000 for shelving in 2023. Our final report on that grant was four months late because our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period. Our current treasurer, Dave Lin, will be responsible for the final report on this grant and will submit it within 30 days of the end of the grant year. We will also send the Foundation our fresh food totals every quarter.

**The problem**
Fresh food has to stay cold from the farm to our distribution. Without a refrigerated vehicle we cannot collect it, so offers like last summer's go unused. The 9,000 pounds we refused averaged 3,000 pounds a month. That would have increased our fresh food by half during those three months, or about 15 more pounds per household over the summer.

**The request**
We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will pay the remaining $18,000 from our reserves, leaving us with $11,000. Insurance and fuel will cost about $4,500 a year, plus maintenance and registration. These costs are not yet in our budget.

**What the van will do**
Two local farms have told us in writing that they will give again if we can pick up cold. Four of our 23 regular volunteers have agreed to drive the van on a weekly schedule, so pickups will not depend on a single person.

Harvests vary, and we cannot promise the same 9,000 pounds next year. What the van does is remove the reason we said no last summer. In its first year, we expect it to let us accept fresh produce that we would otherwise turn away, with the largest gains during harvest months. Our quarterly reports will show you exactly how much.

**If we do not get the van**
We will again turn away fresh food that farms are ready to give, and the 610 households we serve will go without it.
