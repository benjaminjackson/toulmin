Our volunteer-run food bank serves 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food, and about 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We turned all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold. This application is about not turning it down again.

**Our record with the Foundation**

The Foundation granted us $12,000 for shelving in 2023, and we are grateful for it. Our final report on that grant was four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period. Our current treasurer, Dave Lin, will be responsible for the final report on this grant, and we will submit it within 30 days of the end of the grant year. We will also send the Foundation our fresh food pounds every quarter, so you can follow the results through the year rather than waiting for the final report.

**The request**

We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will pay the remaining $18,000, more than a quarter of the cost, from our own reserves, which will leave us with $11,000. Insurance and fuel will cost about $4,500 a year, and that is not yet in our budget.

**What the van will do**

Last summer's offer works out to about 3,000 extra pounds of produce a month during the harvest season. That is 50 percent more fresh food than we currently hand out in those months. Two of the farms have written letters saying they will give to us again if we can collect it cold. We cannot promise they will offer the same 9,000 pounds every year, because that depends on their harvests. What we can say is that without a refrigerated vehicle, we will keep turning down whatever they offer.

Four of our 23 regular volunteers have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule.

The farms have said they will give, and four volunteers have signed up to drive. The van is the piece we are missing. With it, we can say yes the next time a local farm calls.
</document>
