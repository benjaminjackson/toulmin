# Bookkeeping for hair and nail salons, from someone who used to run one

I'm Carla Benson. I managed a hair salon for six years before I became a bookkeeper. Now I work only with hair and nail salons, and I have 31 salon clients.

## What you get for $199 a month

- Your monthly books, closed by the 10th of the following month
- Payroll for up to 10 staff
- Your sales tax filings

I don't do income tax returns. Your accountant or tax preparer keeps doing those, and I give them clean books to work from.

## Clean books can lower your accountant's bill

Last tax season, 9 of my clients told me their accountant charged them less because their books came in clean. One owner said she saved $800. I can't promise your accountant will charge you less. But tidy books leave them less to sort out.

## The 10th of the month, and what happens if I miss it

I close each month's books by the 10th of the following month. Last year I missed that date three times. Since January, if I miss the 10th, you don't pay for that month.
