# Bookkeeping for hair and nail salons, from someone who ran one

I'm Carla Benson. I managed a hair salon for six years before I became a bookkeeper. Now I work only with salons. Chair and Ledger keeps the books for 31 hair and nail salons.

## What you get for $199 a month

- Your books closed every month, by the 10th of the following month
- Payroll for up to 10 staff
- Your sales tax filed

There's no contract. If you want to stop, give me 30 days' notice.

## Clean books cost less at tax time

I don't do income tax returns. Your accountant or tax preparer keeps doing those, and they get finished books from me to work from. Last tax season, 9 of my clients told me their accountant charged them less because the books came in clean. One owner said she saved $800. I can't promise you'll save that much. What I can promise is that your accountant starts from finished books.

## The 10th, and what happens if I miss it

I'm one person, so here's the honest record: last year I missed the 10th three times. Since January, if your books aren't closed by the 10th, you don't pay for that month.

## About the price

Until January I charged $149. When I added payroll, I raised the price to $199 for every client, including the ones I already had.

## Who stays

Two clients left last year. Both had sold their salons.
