Subject: Fee for the next book

Dear Nadia,

Thank you for offering me the next book. I've loved making six books with you over the past four years, and I'd like to make this one too.

Before I say yes, I need to raise my fee. I've been paid $9,000 a book since my first one with you, and each book takes me about four months. Over those years, two of our books have sold more than 20,000 copies, and one made a state reading list and went into its fourth printing this spring.

The market has moved as well. Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a book of the same length, and my agent tells me $11,000 to $13,000 is standard for an illustrator with my record. At $9,000, I'm below the bottom of that range.

For this book I'm asking $11,500. That's less than I was paid elsewhere last year, and I've set it there because I value working with you and want to keep doing it.

I'm not able to take on another book at $9,000, but I'd very much like to make this one with you. I'm happy to talk it through whenever suits you.

Best,
Elena Varga
