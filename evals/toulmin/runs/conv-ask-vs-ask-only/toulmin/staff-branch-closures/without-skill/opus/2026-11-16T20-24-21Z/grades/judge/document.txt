**Subject:** Maple Street and Route 9 branches are closing, and we're opening a member phone and chat center

Team,

I want you to hear this from me directly. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. All 14 people who work at those branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center at the main office on Elm Avenue. The pay will be the same as now or higher.

**Why we're doing this**

Over the last three years, visits to those two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Members who call us now wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. That's too long, and the reason is that we don't have enough people answering. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we need our people where our members are.

**What the new role looks like**

- Pay in the center starts $1.50 an hour above current teller pay. No one will earn less than they do today.
- The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. Shifts rotate, and staff with the most years here choose their shifts first. I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule.
- After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.

**If the new role isn't right for you**

I won't pretend this is a small change. Going from a branch to the phones, with a longer day and rotating shifts, is a real adjustment, and some of you may decide it isn't for you. That's a fair choice. If you don't take the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

You can also try the role first. If you take it and decide within six months that it isn't working, you can still leave with the same severance.

**What happens next**

- Written offers will go out by February 14.
- Your decision is due by March 1.
- Anyone who wants one can have a one-on-one with HR before then to go over the offer, the shifts, and the severance.

The people at Maple Street and Route 9 know our members as well as anyone here. That's what the center needs most, and I hope you'll bring it with you.

If you have questions, please come to me or to HR. I'd rather hear them now than have you wonder.
