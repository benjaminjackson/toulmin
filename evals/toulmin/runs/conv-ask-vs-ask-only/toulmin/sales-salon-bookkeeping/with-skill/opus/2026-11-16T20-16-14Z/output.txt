# Bookkeeping for hair and nail salons, from someone who managed one

**Monthly books, payroll and sales tax for one flat fee of $199 a month.**

I'm Carla Benson. Before I became a bookkeeper, I managed a hair salon for six years. I know how a salon week goes, and I know the paperwork is usually what gets left until last.

Chair and Ledger works only with hair and nail salons. I have 31 salon clients right now. Last year two left, and both left because they sold their salons.

## What $199 a month covers

- **Monthly books**, closed by the 10th of the following month
- **Payroll** for up to 10 staff
- **Sales tax filing**

You get all three for one price every month.

## Your books by the 10th, or that month is free

I close each month's books by the 10th of the next month. Last year I missed that date three times. So now, if I miss the 10th, you don't pay for that month. You know when your numbers will be ready, and if they're late, I pay for it, not you.

## Clean books cost less at tax time

Last tax season, nine of my clients told me their accountant charged them less because their books came in clean. One of them said she saved $800. That's about four months of my fee.

When your books are a mess, your accountant has to sort them out before they can start on your taxes, and you pay for that time. When your books arrive clean and on time, that cost goes away.

## You'll always work with me

Chair and Ledger is just me. There's no call center, and no new account manager every few months. The person you talk to is the one doing your books, and she has already learned how your salon works.

I work Tuesday to Saturday. Like most of the salons I work with, I don't answer the phone on Sundays or Mondays.

## Start with a free 20-minute call

Tell me about your salon: how many staff you have, how you pay them, and what's been piling up. I'll tell you how I'd handle it. Calls are Tuesday to Saturday, and the first one is free.
