**An Open Letter to Keystone Property Group**

To Diane Weller, Regional Manager, Keystone Property Group:

Harbor View Apartments, at 220 Grant Street, has nine floors, 112 apartments and one elevator. This year that elevator has been out of service on 41 days. One outage in August lasted 12 days straight.

For many of our neighbors, a broken elevator means being stuck at home. Twenty-three of our residents are over 75. Six use wheelchairs or walkers. During the August outage, a resident on the eighth floor missed two dialysis appointments because they had no way to get down to the street. Dialysis is not an appointment anyone can safely miss.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and gave it a passing certificate. We are not saying the elevator is unsafe when it runs. Our problem is how often it doesn't run.

In June, Keystone told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. Now Keystone tells us the same thing: a part is on order, and it should arrive in six to eight weeks. We have not been told whether this is the same part, or what happened to the first order.

Seventy-four households signed our petition asking for a second elevator. Keystone says that would cost about $400,000 and can't be done in this building. We are not here to argue that point. Instead, we are asking for three things that are within Keystone's power right now:

1. **A written date for the repair.** We need an actual date, not another range of weeks.

2. **A stair-climbing chair, with staff trained to use it**, available whenever the elevator is out of service. Then residents who cannot use the stairs can still get to the doctor, to dialysis and to the grocery store.

3. **A rent credit for every day the elevator is out of service.** Residents of the upper floors pay full rent for a building they can't fully use.

We are posting this letter in the lobby of Harbor View on December 1 and sharing it with local news, because every resident deserves to know where things stand. We welcome a written reply from Keystone, and we will share it with residents just as openly.

None of our neighbors should have to choose between waiting out an outage and missing medical care.

Sincerely,

Rosa Delgado
Secretary, Harbor View Apartments Tenants' Association
220 Grant Street
