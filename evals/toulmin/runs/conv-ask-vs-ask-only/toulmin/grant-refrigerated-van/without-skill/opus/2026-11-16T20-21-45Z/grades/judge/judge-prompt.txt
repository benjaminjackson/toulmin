**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food Recovery**

[Food Bank Name] is a volunteer-run food bank serving [Town], a community of about 9,000 people. Each month, our 23 regular volunteers hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. Families struggle most to afford these foods, and they are also the hardest for us to get.

**The problem**

Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We had to turn it all down. We had no way to pick it up cold, so it would have spoiled before it reached anyone. That is about six weeks of our current fresh food supply, lost because we didn't have the right vehicle.

**Our request**

We are asking the foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. That is more than a quarter of the cost, and a serious commitment for an organization our size.

**What the van will do**

The van will let us collect fresh food from farms and keep it cold on the way to our distribution. Two of the farms that offered produce last summer have written letters, attached, saying they will give again if we can pick up cold. Four of our volunteers have said they will drive the van on a weekly schedule.

Our goal for the first year is to recover at least 9,000 pounds of fresh food that we would otherwise have to turn away. That is the amount farms offered us last summer. Harvests vary and farms make their own decisions, so we can't guarantee the same amount again. We will report what we actually receive. At that level, the van would increase the fresh food we hand out by about one-eighth, with all of it going to families already on our rolls.

We estimate that insurance and fuel for the van will cost about $4,500 a year.

**Accountability**

Our final report on the foundation's 2023 shelving grant was four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period. We regret the delay. Our current treasurer, Dave Lin, will be responsible for this grant's final report, due 30 days after the grant year ends. We will also send the foundation our fresh food totals every quarter, so you can follow the results through the year.

**Why now**

The food is there, and local farms want to give it. A refrigerated van is the one thing standing between that food and the 610 households we serve.
