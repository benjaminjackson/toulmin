**Millbrook Community Pantry: Refrigerated Van**

Every month, Millbrook Community Pantry gives out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households in our town of roughly 9,000 people. Only about 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. That isn't because fresh food is unavailable. Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of produce over three months, and we had to turn it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or keep it cold.

That produce would have increased our fresh food by half during those three months. It works out to about 15 more pounds for every household we serve. Instead, it stayed on the farms.

We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. The van closes the one gap between food that farmers are already offering and the families who need it. Two of last summer's farms have written letters saying they will donate again if we can collect cold. We cannot promise that farms will offer the same amount every year, so we are not promising a fixed number of pounds. We are promising that when fresh food is offered, we will be able to accept it, and that we will report exactly how much we accepted.

We are putting our own money behind this. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our reserves. That is more than 60 percent of what we hold and leaves us $11,000. We would not commit that much of our cushion to something we were unsure of.

The van will be run by people already committed to it. The pantry is volunteer-run, with 23 regular volunteers. Four of them have agreed to drive the van on a weekly schedule, so pickups will not depend on any one person.

We also owe the Foundation a straight account of our last grant. In 2023 you gave us $12,000 for shelving, and our final report reached you four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period. That should not have held up our report to you. Here is what is different this time:

- Our treasurer, Dave Lin, is named as responsible for the final report, which we will deliver within 30 days of the end of the grant year.
- We will send the Foundation our fresh food totals in pounds every quarter, so you see the results as they happen and not only at the end.

Without a van, we will keep turning away fresh produce offered free by farms near us, and the families we serve will keep receiving mostly canned and boxed food. With it, food that is already being offered can reach 610 households while it is still fresh. We would be grateful for the Foundation's partnership again.
</document>
