Subject: I'm sorry about last summer

Hello,

I'm sorry for how I handled last summer. After the July 9 hailstorm, your boxes were about half-full for six weeks, and all you heard from me was one short email. I was working 16-hour days to save what was left, but I should have written more and made it right.

In our fall survey, 29 of the 41 people who answered said they'd come back if we fixed how we handle bad weeks. So here's what's changed:

- If weather cuts a box by more than a third, you get credit for that week.
- I'll send a short email every Thursday of the season.
- You can pause up to two weeks and get credit for them.
- This year we added a second field on higher ground and hail nets over the tomatoes and peppers, a $31,000 investment.

I can't promise there won't be crop loss, and the nets don't cover the greens. But if it happens, you'll hear from me that week.

A full share is still $540, with pickup Thursdays at the farm stand. Sign-up closes March 15, and 52 shares are left. The link is on our website.
