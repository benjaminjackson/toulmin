Subject: Fee for the next book

Dear Nadia,

Thank you for offering me the next book. I've really enjoyed our six books together over the past four years, and I'd very much like to do this one.

Before I accept, I'd like to discuss the fee. I've been paid $9,000 per book since my first book with you, and for this one I'm asking for $11,500.

Two of the books I've illustrated for you have sold over 20,000 copies each, and one was chosen for a state reading list. I'm proud of that record, and I think it shows what I bring to your list.

My fee hasn't changed to reflect it. Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a book of the same length. My agent tells me that $11,000 to $13,000 is the standard range for an illustrator with my record. $9,000 is below the bottom of that range, and $11,500 would still be less than I've already been paid elsewhere.

Each book takes me about four months, and I can't take on another one at $9,000. I'd much rather keep working with you, and I hope we can agree on $11,500.

I'd be glad to talk it through whenever suits you.

Best wishes,
Elena Varga
