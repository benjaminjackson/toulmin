Subject: The Night Garden – fee

Dear Nadia,

Thank you for offering me The Night Garden. I'd love to illustrate it. After six books together, I'm proud of what we've built.

Before I say yes, I need to raise the fee. My rate has been $9,000 since our first book four years ago. Since then our books have done well: two have sold more than 20,000 copies, and one was chosen for a state reading list and went into its fourth printing this spring.

The market has moved too. Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a 32-page book like this one. My agent tells me $11,000 to $13,000 is standard for an illustrator with my record, so $9,000 is now well below even the bottom of that range.

I'm asking for $11,500 for The Night Garden. At that fee, I'll deliver sketches within six weeks of signing and final art by September 1. I'll also do two spot illustrations for your marketing at no charge.

I can't take the book on at $9,000, but I very much want to keep working with you and I'm happy to talk it through. I'd like to settle this before your deadline at the end of next week, so please let me know what's possible.

Warm regards,
Elena Varga

- **The $12,000 point:** I couldn't say "every other publisher" or "at least $12,000," because neither one is true. Instead, the email puts your strongest true facts together: one publisher paid $12,500, and your agent's range starts at $11,000. That lets it say plainly that $9,000 is "well below even the bottom" of what the market pays. That's true, it's the hardest-hitting version I can stand behind, and it makes $11,500 look modest.
- **Kept private:** The email doesn't mention your $11,000 floor or that a no would leave you with a gap in your schedule. "Happy to talk it through" shows you're open to meeting in between without saying where you'd stop. "I can't take the book on at $9,000" is the line you told me you'll stand behind.
- **The four years without a raise:** I mention it once, as a plain fact, and don't press it. That keeps the tone friendly, since you want to keep working with them.
