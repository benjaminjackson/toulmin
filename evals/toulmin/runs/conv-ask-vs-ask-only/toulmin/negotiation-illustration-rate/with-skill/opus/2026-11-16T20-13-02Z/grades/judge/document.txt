Subject: Fee for the next book

Dear Nadia,

Thank you for offering me the next book. I've loved working with you and the team over the past four years, and I'd very much like to do this one.

Before I say yes, I'd like to talk about the fee. I've illustrated six books for you at $9,000 each, the same fee I received for my first. Since then, two of those books have sold over 20,000 copies. One was chosen for a state reading list and went into its fourth printing this spring.

Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a picture book of the same length. My agent tells me that $11,000 to $13,000 is the normal range for an illustrator with my record.

Each book takes me about four months. I can't take on another one at $9,000, but I'd be glad to do this one for $11,500. That's below what I was paid elsewhere last year. I've kept it there because I want to keep working with you.

Could we agree on $11,500 for this book? I'm happy to talk it through by phone if that's easier.

Best,
Elena Varga
