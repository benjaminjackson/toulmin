Dear Karen Doyle,

I am applying for the Training Coordinator position at Calder Pharmaceuticals' Westfield plant. For nine years I have taught high school chemistry. That work has meant training people to follow procedures where a mistake can hurt someone, and keeping records that show the training happened.

In 2019 I wrote our school's lab safety program. I keep a signed training record for every new teacher I train and for every student's lab safety quiz.
