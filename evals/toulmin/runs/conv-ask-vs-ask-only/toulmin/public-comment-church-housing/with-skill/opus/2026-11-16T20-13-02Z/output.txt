Some things to know before tonight:

- **Timing:** The speech is about 400 words, so it should take just under 3 minutes at a steady pace. Read it aloud once with a timer. If you run long, cut the "Now the land" paragraph first.
- **Name and address:** Most commissions ask speakers to state these first. I didn't write them in, so add them however your commission asks.
- **Staff recommendation:** The staff report is usually posted with tonight's agenda on the county website. If staff recommends approval, add one sentence after you state your ask: "Your staff has recommended approval."
- **Sunday parking:** I left this out because you don't have an answer yet, and raising it without one would give opponents an opening. If a commissioner asks you, it's fine to say: "I don't know. That's a fair question for the applicant."
- **What I left out on purpose:** The speech doesn't describe Cascade's track record or say who would manage the building, because you don't know those things. It also doesn't say whether a D rating is acceptable under county standards. With the signal, that question doesn't come up.
