Subject: Quarterly update: flat revenue, a lost customer, and a December 1 launch

Hi all,

First, an apology. This is my first update in six months. The numbers were flat and I didn't want to send bad news. There was no other reason, and it was the wrong call. From now on you'll get an update in the first week of every quarter, starting January 7. If you'd like a one-page set of numbers every month, reply and I'll add you.

**The numbers**

- Monthly recurring revenue: $41,000, the same as three months ago.
- Clinics: added 9, lost 4.
- Cash: $480,000 in the bank, losing about $44,000 a month. That's roughly 11 months of runway.

Revenue stayed flat because of one loss. Our largest customer, a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500 a month, moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. Growth everywhere else added about $5,500 a month, which only covered that loss.

**The risk I can't rule out**

Two other customers use the same records system as the chain that left. Together they pay $3,800 a month, and I can't promise they won't leave the same way. If they did, monthly revenue would fall to $37,200 and our runway would shrink to about 10 months.

**What's working**

Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a no-show rate of 7%, down from 13% before they started. That cuts missed appointments almost in half, and for a clinic a missed appointment is lost revenue. Of everything we offer, this is where the value is clearest.

**What we're doing about it**

On December 1 we start selling the reminders on their own for $150 a month, so a clinic can buy them without switching schedulers. Six clinics have already told us they'll buy at launch. That's a start, not proof. At $150 a month it would take about 37 customers to replace the account we lost. I also haven't yet confirmed which records systems the standalone product works with. In the January 7 update I'll report how many clinics have bought it.

I haven't decided whether to raise money. With about 11 months of cash, I'd value your view on that.
