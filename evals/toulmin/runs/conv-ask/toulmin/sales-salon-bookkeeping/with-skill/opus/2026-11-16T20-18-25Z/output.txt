It's about 330 words. Some notes on what I decided:

- **Who it's for:** I didn't pick between owners who do their own books and owners switching from another bookkeeper. The opening fits the first group, and the accountant savings and late-month guarantee work for both.
- **The 3 missed closes:** The page doesn't mention them. It also never says you've always hit the 10th. The free-month guarantee answers the worry without making a claim you can't back up.
- **Price:** The January increase isn't mentioned, because new visitors only see $199. If you find that no client has left since January, add something like "Every client stayed when I raised my price this year." It would be the best answer to "that's too expensive."
- **Reply time:** I left it out because you couldn't commit to one. If you settle on one later, it belongs under "You'll always deal with me."
- **Button:** I used "Book my free 20-minute call" so people know exactly what they're signing up for. Link it to your calendar.
