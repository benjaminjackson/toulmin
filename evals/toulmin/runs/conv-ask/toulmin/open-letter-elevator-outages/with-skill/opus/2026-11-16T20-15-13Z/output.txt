December 1, 2026

**An open letter to Diane Weller, Regional Manager, Keystone Property Group**

Dear Ms. Weller,

Harbor View Apartments at 220 Grant Street has nine floors, 112 apartments and one elevator. This year that elevator has been out of service on 41 days. That's about one day in every eight. In August it was out for 12 days in a row. During those 12 days, a resident on the eighth floor missed two dialysis appointments.

Twenty-three of our residents are over 75, and six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the elevator stops, they can't leave their homes. They can't get to the doctor or the pharmacy, and they can't get out quickly in an emergency.

In June, Keystone told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. Today we are being told the same thing: six to eight weeks. We have not been given a reason for the delay. We are not aware of any help offered to residents during the outages.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and issued a passing certificate. That certificate says the elevator is safe when it runs. It does not say it will run.

We ask Keystone for three things:

1. **A written date for the controller repair**, and the name of the person responsible for meeting it.
2. **A stair-climbing chair and staff trained to use it**, so residents who can't use the stairs can get in and out of the building during an outage.
3. **A rent credit for every day the elevator is out of service.**

In return, the tenants' association will:

- meet with you or your staff on any weekday evening that suits you
- keep an up-to-date list of residents who need help on the stairs, and share it with Keystone for use in emergencies.

Seventy-four of our 112 households have also signed a petition asking for a second elevator. Keystone has told us it would cost about $400,000 and can't be done in this building. We are not engineers, and we are not disputing that. We ask Keystone to put that assessment in writing, with the reasons, so every household can read it.

Without a firm repair date and help on the stairs, the next breakdown will be August again for the residents who can least manage it. We are ready to work with Keystone to prevent that, and we look forward to your written reply.

Sincerely,

Rosa Delgado
Secretary, on behalf of the Tenants' Association
Harbor View Apartments, 220 Grant Street
