Our volunteers hand out about 38,000 pounds of food a month to 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs, which works out to less than ten pounds per household each month. Local farms are willing to give us more. What stops us is that we have no way to collect it cold.

Last summer, farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We had to turn it down because we had no refrigerated vehicle. Had we been able to accept it, the fresh food we handed out over those months would have risen by half, from about 18,000 pounds to about 27,000. That is nearly 15 extra pounds of fresh food for every household we serve.

This foundation has backed us before. In 2023 you gave us $12,000 for shelving, and we owe you a plain account of what followed: our final report reached you four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period, and we should have had someone ready to step in. For this grant, our treasurer, Dave Lin, is responsible for the final report, which we will submit within 30 days of the end of the grant year. We will also send you our fresh food totals every quarter, so you can follow the results as they come in.

We are asking for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves, nearly two-thirds of what we hold. We would not commit that much of our own money to a vehicle we did not expect to put to work.

Two of the farms that offered produce last summer have written to say they will give again if we can collect it cold. Four of our 23 regular volunteers have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule. We cannot promise the farms will offer the same 9,000 pounds next year, because harvests and surpluses change from season to season. That is why the quarterly reports matter: you will see what the van actually brings in, not only what we expect.

Without the van, we will have to say no to the farms again, and the households we serve will keep receiving the same amount of fresh food while more is offered for them and turned away. With it, next summer we can say yes.
