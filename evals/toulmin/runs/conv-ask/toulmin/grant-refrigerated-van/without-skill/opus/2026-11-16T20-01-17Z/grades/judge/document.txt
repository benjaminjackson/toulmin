**Project Narrative**

[Food Bank Name] is a volunteer-run food bank serving 610 households in [Town], a community of about 9,000 people. Each month we distribute about 38,000 pounds of food. Only about 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. Those are the foods our clients ask for most and that low-income families find hardest to afford.

The fresh food is close by; what we lack is a way to move it. Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or store it until distribution day.

**Our Request**

We are requesting $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. That is a significant commitment for an organization our size, and it shows how central this van is to our mission.

**What the Van Will Do**

With a refrigerated van, we will:

- **Accept farm donations we currently refuse.** Based on last summer's offers alone, we expect to collect at least 9,000 additional pounds of fresh produce in the first year. That is a 50% increase in the fresh food we distribute during the growing season.
- **Keep perishables cold from pickup to distribution**, so more of what we receive reaches families in good condition.
- **Grow our farm partnerships.** Once we can say yes to fresh donations, we will reach out to more local growers about regular pickups.

**Who Will Run It**

The van will be operated by our volunteers, the same people who run everything we do. Of our 23 regular volunteers, four have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule. This gives us enough drivers for consistent pickups and backup when someone is unavailable.

**How We Will Measure Results**

We will track the pounds of fresh food collected each month, by source, and the pounds distributed. We will compare these figures to our current baseline of 6,000 pounds per month and report them to the Foundation.
