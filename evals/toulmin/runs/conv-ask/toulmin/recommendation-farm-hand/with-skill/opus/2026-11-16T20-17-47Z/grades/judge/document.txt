Dear Admissions Committee,

I am the farm manager at Hollis Dairy, a 400-cow operation, and I am writing to recommend Tomás Reyes for your two-year agricultural technology program. Tomás has worked for us for three years, since he was 19. The best evidence I can give you of how he will do in your program is what he already does with farm technology every week.

Tomás learned our herd management software on the job, and he now uses it to flag sick cows for our veterinarian. Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early. Our vet estimates that catching those cases early saved us about $8,000. Using herd data to make decisions about animal health is the kind of work your program trains people for, and Tomás is already doing it.

He is also someone you can count on. He runs our 3 a.m. milking shift on his own, five days a week. Apart from a family medical emergency, he has missed two shifts in three years. That emergency was last summer, when his father became seriously ill in Mexico and Tomás was away for five weeks. Before he left, he arranged for his cousin, a former employee of ours, to cover his shifts, and he came back on the day he said he would.

I want to be straightforward about one thing. Tomás's spoken English is stronger than his written English, and he knows it. He is taking a night class to improve his writing, on his own time, while still working the early milking shift. He learned our software by sticking with it, and I expect he will handle written coursework the same way.

I will keep his job open part-time on weekends while he is in the program. I would be glad to talk with anyone on the committee about him. You can reach me at 555-0187.

I recommend Tomás for your program.
