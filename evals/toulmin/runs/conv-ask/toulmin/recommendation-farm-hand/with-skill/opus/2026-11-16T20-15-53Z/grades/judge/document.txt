I am the farm manager at Hollis Dairy, a 400-cow dairy operation, and I recommend Tomás Reyes for your two-year agricultural technology program without reservation. Tomás has worked for me for three years, since he was 19. In that time he has become one of the people this farm depends on most.

Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift on his own, five days a week. Nobody checks his work at that hour. The herd gets milked and the parlor is left ready for the next shift because he does it right. In three years he has missed two shifts. I don't hand a shift like that to many people.

What makes me confident he belongs in a technical program is how he took to our herd management software. Tomás learned the system and now uses the data to flag sick cows for our veterinarian. That combination of hands-on herd knowledge and comfort with the technology is what your program is built to develop, and Tomás already has a start on it.

His spoken English is strong, and he is working on his written English in a night class he signed up for on his own. That is typical of him. When he sees something he needs to get better at, he goes and does it.

I want Tomás to finish this program, and I want him to come back to this industry with what he learns. I will keep his job open part-time on weekends while he is enrolled, so he can keep earning and stay connected to the work.
