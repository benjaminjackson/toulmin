**[Game Title]: Keep the light burning. Keep your family together.**

Winter is coming to the lighthouse. The supply boat won't come again until spring. Storms will test the lamp, the stores and the people who depend on each other to get through the dark months.

[Game Title] is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. Together you are a lighthouse keeper's family trying to survive one long winter. You win or lose as a family. Every choice is shared: who climbs the tower tonight, what you ration, what you risk when the weather turns.
