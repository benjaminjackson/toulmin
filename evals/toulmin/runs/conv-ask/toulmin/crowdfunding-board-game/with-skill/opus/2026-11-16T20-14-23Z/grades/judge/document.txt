**A cooperative game about a lighthouse keeper's family, and the winter they have to get through together.**

For 1 to 4 players. $49, with US shipping included. Expected delivery: November 2027.

**Proven at the table**

I didn't want to launch a game I only hoped would work. I wanted to launch one I'd already watched work.

Since 2023 I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. That's close to one session a week for three years. The rules and the art are now finished.

I also took the game to two conventions and sat down with anyone who wanted to play. Most of the people who've rated it on BoardGameGeek played it at my table there. It has 23 ratings so far, with an average of 8.1.

That doesn't make it a hit yet. It makes it a game that has been tested and refined over three years, and that people liked enough to go and rate. The next step is getting it to you.

**Who I am**

I'm a land surveyor. I work full time, and this is my first game. I know that gives some of you pause, because first-time projects are often the ones that ship late or don't ship at all. So here is exactly where things stand.

**Where the money goes**

I have a signed quote from the factory: $17,400 for 1,500 copies. Shipping to backers in the US costs about $9 a copy.

The $28,000 goal covers that print run, shipping to every backer and the platform and payment fees, with a small cushion left over. I haven't padded it for things I can't promise. There are no stretch goals in this campaign, because I'd rather deliver the game I've tested than promise a bigger one I haven't.

The factory run is 1,500 copies, which is nearly three times what the goal needs. If far more of you back the game than I need, nothing has to be re-quoted or renegotiated. The plan stays the same.

**When you'll get it**

Expected delivery is November 2027. I could have put an earlier date on this page, but I won't promise a date I don't believe. I'd rather give you a date I can meet.

**What it takes**

At $49 a copy, the goal is about 570 backers. 1,240 of you signed up for this launch. If half of you back it, the game gets made.

I've spent three years making sure this game works when real people sit down to play it. Back it, and help me get it to your table.
