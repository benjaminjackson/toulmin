Subject: Closing Maple Street and Route 9, and opening our member phone and chat center

To all staff,

On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Everyone who works at those two branches, 14 people in all, will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center. I want you to hear the reasons and what happens next directly from me.

**Why we're making this change**

The way our members reach us has changed. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Members who call us now wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give. We can't fix it while our people are working where fewer and fewer members come.

**What this means for Maple Street and Route 9 staff**

- Every one of you will be offered a job in the center at the same pay or higher.
- After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate.

I won't pretend the schedule is a small change. You work 9 to 5 today, and I can't promise anyone those hours in the new role. Some shifts will run into the evening. Here is what I can commit to: the people who have worked here longest will choose their shifts first.

If the new role doesn't work for you, you will receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

**What happens next**

- Written offers will go out by February 14.
- Decisions are due by March 1.
- Anyone who wants a one-on-one meeting with HR to talk through their situation can have one. Just ask.

We haven't set start dates for the center or for training yet. I'll share both as soon as they're decided.

**To everyone else**

The members who used to walk into Maple Street and Route 9 will now be calling and chatting with us. The colleagues who served them in person bring years of experience to that work. Please make them welcome.

To the 14 of you: this is a big change, and you deserve a clear and honest account of it. If you have questions this email doesn't answer, bring them to me or to HR.

Chief Operating Officer
