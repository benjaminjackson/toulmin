Subject: Closing Maple Street and Route 9, and opening a member phone and chat center

Team,

On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. All 14 people who work at those branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center at the main office on Elm Avenue. Every offer will be at the same pay or higher.

Here is why. Over the last three years, visits to those two branches fell 44%. Over the same three years, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Members have changed how they reach us, and we haven't kept up. Today a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. The new center is how we fix that, and it will be staffed by people who already know our members.

For the 14 of you at Maple Street and Route 9, some things will change, and I want to be straight about them.

Hours. The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, so it covers more of the day than the branches' 9 to 5. Shifts will rotate. The people who have worked here longest will pick their shifts first. I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule, and we are still working out how the rotation will run.

The work. Helping members by phone and chat is different from helping them at the counter. Everyone starts with 90 days of training.

Working from home. Once you finish the 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.

Your decision. You'll get your written offer by February 14, and your decision is due March 1. If you'd like to talk it through first, you can book a one-on-one with HR.

If you decide not to take the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

If you take the role and decide within six months that it isn't right for you, you can still leave with that same severance. You don't have to know for certain on March 1. You can try the job and then decide.

To everyone at our other branches: members who used Maple Street and Route 9 will start coming to you and calling us. Please help them through the change.

Thank you,

Chief Operating Officer
