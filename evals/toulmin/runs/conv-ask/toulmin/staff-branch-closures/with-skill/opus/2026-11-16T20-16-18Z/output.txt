**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31**

Team,

I want you to hear this directly from me. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Each of the 14 people who work at those two branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center at our main office on Elm Avenue.

**Why we're doing this**

Our members have changed how they reach us. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Right now, members who call us wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. Many of you have heard members complain about it. We can't shorten that wait while staff are in branches that fewer members visit, so we're moving staff to where our members now go.

**What this means for Maple Street and Route 9 staff**

- Every one of you will be offered a job in the center, at the same pay or higher.
- Written offers will go out by February 14. Decisions are due March 1.
- After 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week.
- If you decide not to take the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

**The biggest change is the schedule**

I want to be straight with you about this. The branches were open 9 to 5. The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. Staff who have been here longest will choose their shifts first. I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule. We haven't decided yet how often shifts will rotate or whether evening shifts will pay more. I'll let you know as soon as we have.

I know this will be harder for some of you than for others, especially if you have children at home or other commitments in the evening. If you'd like to talk it through, HR will meet with you one-on-one. Just ask.

**Why I hope you'll stay**

The people at Maple Street and Route 9 know our members and know how we do things here. Members need that experience when they call or chat with us. Phone and chat is now how most of our members reach us, and this is where we're putting our people. I hope every one of you will come with us.

Thank you for everything you've done for our members at those branches.

Chief Operating Officer
