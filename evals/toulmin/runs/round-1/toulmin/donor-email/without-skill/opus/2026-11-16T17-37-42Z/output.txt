I didn't include the line saying a gift this month guarantees every child on the waiting list a place in January, because the numbers don't support it:

- **What it would take:** 61 children × $1,150 is **$70,150**.
- **The most the match can produce:** $30,000, which is $15,000 from donors plus $15,000 matched.
- **Even if everyone gave:** if all 340 lapsed donors gave their old average of $85, you'd raise $28,900, or $57,800 with the match. That's still short.
- **A realistic result:** last year's email raised $1,240. Even double that with the match comes to about $5,000, which covers roughly 4 children.

Your audience already has a reason to distrust you, and a former board member forwards your emails with commentary. A promise that can't be kept is the one thing that could make this email backfire. So I wrote it around what's true and still strong: the match doubles their gift, and $575 now pays for a full year for one child.
