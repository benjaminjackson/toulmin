**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Approval to replace display advertising with reader membership from January 2027

**Decision requested**
Approve ending display advertising on December 31, 2026 and launching a reader membership program in January 2027, with a progress review at the end of June.

**Why change**
Ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 in two years, about 20% a year. If that trend continues, ads will bring in about $67,000 in 2027. [Why we are not running ads and membership side by side: one or two sentences.]

**What the pilot showed**
In this spring's six-week pilot, 212 readers became members at $8 a month. The only promotion was two newsletter mentions. That is 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers, worth about $20,000 a year in recurring revenue. [X] of them are still paying today.

**The target**
To match last year's $84,000, we need 875 members, or 6% of our newsletter list. To match where ad revenue is heading, we need about 700. Going from 212 to 875 over 2027 takes about 55 new members a month. The pilot added about 150 a month with almost no promotion. We don't expect that pace to hold, because the first people to join are the most committed readers. But the target is about a third of that pace, and in 2027 we will be promoting membership for the first time. A newsroom of our size in [state] now gets 40% of its budget from membership. Their market differs from ours, but they show the model can support a newsroom like ours.

**The cost in 2027**
Ad revenue stops in January, while membership builds month by month. If we stay on target, we will be earning at the $84,000 yearly rate by December, but we will collect about $55,000 during 2027, before cancellations. That is $12,000–$29,000 less than ads would probably have brought in. Ending ads saves $[X] in sales costs. We propose to cover the remaining gap from [reserves / bridge grant / other].

**Checkpoint**
By June 30 we should have about 540 members. If we have fewer than [450], I will return to the board in July with options, including bringing back sponsorships.

**Our ad sales colleague**
[Name] has sold advertising for us part-time for 11 years. I have spoken with [Name] about this proposal, and [plan agreed with them: new role, transition period, or support].

**Recommendation**
Ad revenue has fallen two years running, and the pilot showed readers will pay with almost no prompting. I recommend the board approve the move to membership from January, with the June 30 review as the point to change course if needed.
