**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Replacing display advertising with reader membership from January 2027

**Recommendation**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising on December 31, 2026 and launching a reader membership program in January 2027. Our target is 875 paying members by December 2027. At $8 a month, that replaces last year's ad revenue in full on an annual basis. I'll report the member count at every board meeting, and we'll have a formal decision point in July.

**Why now**
Ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 in two years, a drop of 36%, or about 20% a year. If that continues, ads will bring in about $67,000 in 2027 and less after that. The question is not whether we replace this income but how soon we start.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week pilot this spring, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. Our only promotion was two mentions in the newsletter. That's $20,352 a year in recurring revenue from 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers. [X] of those 212 are still paying today.

**What the target requires**
875 members is 6% of our current newsletter list. Starting from the pilot base, we need about 55 new members a month. The pilot brought in about 150 a month without a campaign. A newsroom of our size in [state] now covers 40% of its budget through membership. Their market isn't ours, but it shows the model works for organizations like us.

**Risks, stated plainly**
- **2027 cash.** Members will join over the course of the year, so membership will bring in less cash in 2027 than ads would have, roughly $50,000 if we grow steadily to 875. We will cover the gap of about $[X] from [reserves / grant / cost reduction].
- **Slower growth after launch.** The pilot reached our most engaged readers first, and later members will be harder to win. The target needs about a third of the pilot's monthly pace, which gives us room for that.
- **Why not run both?** Keeping ads means continuing to pay the cost of selling them, about $[X] a year in staff time and ad-tech fees, for income that is falling 20% a year. [An ad-free site is also part of what members get.]

**Our colleague in ad sales**
[Name] has sold our advertising for 11 years. [Describe the plan: a role in the membership program, a transition period, or notice and severance terms.] They have been told about this proposal before this memo.

**Checkpoint**
By June 30, 2027 we should have 500 members. If we have fewer than 400, I will bring the board options at the July meeting, including [cost reductions / reintroducing advertising].

**The board is asked to approve:**
1. Ending display advertising on December 31, 2026.
2. Launching reader membership in January 2027 with a target of 875 members by December 2027.
3. A formal review at the July 2027 board meeting against the June 30 checkpoint.
