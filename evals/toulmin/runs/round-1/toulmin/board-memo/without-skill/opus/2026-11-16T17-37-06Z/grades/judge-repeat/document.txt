**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], [Title]
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Ending display ads and launching reader membership in January 2027

**Decision requested**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising on [date] and launching a reader membership program in January 2027, with a formal review at the June board meeting.

**Why now**
Display ad revenue has fallen from $131,000 to $84,000 over two years, a 36% drop. If it keeps falling at this rate, ads will bring in about $67,000 in 2027. Each year we spend staff time protecting a smaller source of income.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week pilot this spring, 212 readers signed up at $8 a month. Our only promotion was two mentions in the newsletter. That's 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers, and it works out to about $20,000 a year. [X of the 212 are still paying today.] A newsroom of our size in [state] reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget.

**Our goal**
Our goal is for membership to replace our ad revenue within a year. That takes about 875 members at $8 a month, or 6.25% of our current newsletter list. Getting there means adding about 13 members a week. The pilot added about 35 a week with almost no promotion.

**The first-year gap**
Membership revenue will grow over the course of the year, so 2027 membership income will be lower than a full year of ads even if we hit the target. If we grow steadily from 212 to 875 members, membership will bring in about $52,000 in 2027. That is about $15,000 less than our projected ad revenue. We propose covering the gap with [reserves / bridge grant / other].

**Our ad sales colleague**
[Name] has been with us for 11 years. [Proposed plan for their role.]

**Risks and how we'll track them**
- The people who joined the pilot are likely our most loyal readers, so later members may be harder to sign up.
- We don't yet know how many members will cancel over a full year.
- I'll report member numbers and cancellations to the board every month.
- If we have fewer than 500 members by June 30, I'll bring the board options, including [sponsorships / bringing back ads].

I'm happy to go through the numbers in detail before the vote.
