**YES ON MEASURE 4 — RIDGEVIEW DOOR SCRIPT**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm [name], a neighbor volunteering for Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**THE CASE**
"If Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. Measure 4 keeps it open, and it brings back Sunday hours at all five branches in the city.

"Here's the cost: 19 cents for every thousand dollars of assessed value, for six years, and then it ends. For a typical Ridgeview home, that's about $10 a month, or less than a cup of coffee a week.

"And people are using the library more: visits are up 22 percent since 2021. This isn't the time to close a branch."

**ASK**
"Can we count on your Yes vote in March?"
→ **Yes:** "Thank you!" Mark **1**. Ask if they'd take a yard sign.
→ **Unsure:** "What would help you decide?" Listen, then answer below. Mark **3**.
→ **No:** "Thanks for your time." Mark **5**. Leave politely. Don't argue.

**IF THEY SAY…**
**"It's a blank check."** → "It's a fixed rate, 19 cents per thousand, and it ends after six years unless voters renew it. It pays for two things: Sunday hours, and keeping the Ridgeview branch open."
**"I'm on a fixed income."** → "That's fair, and it's real money. For most homes here it's about $10 a month, it stops after six years, and it keeps the branch down the street open."
**"I rent."** → "Your vote counts the same, and it's your branch too."

Never name or criticize anyone who opposes the measure.

**WHAT IT COSTS** (assessed value → per year / per month)
$400,000 → $76 / $6.33
$500,000 → $95 / $7.92
**$640,000 (Ridgeview typical) → $122 / $10.13**
$800,000 → $152 / $12.67
$1,000,000 → $190 / $15.83
*Their home: assessed value ÷ 1,000 × $0.19 = cost per year.*
