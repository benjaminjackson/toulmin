**YES ON MEASURE 4: Library Levy | Door Script (Ridgeview)**

**INTRO**
"Hi, I'm [name]. I'm a volunteer with Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**CONNECT**
"Do you or anyone in your household use the Ridgeview branch?"
*(Listen. Use what they tell you.)*

**WHY IT MATTERS**
"Without Measure 4, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. Measure 4 keeps it open and brings back Sunday hours at all five branches in the city. And people are using the library more: visits are up 22% since 2021."

**COST: SAY IT STRAIGHT**
"The rate is 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value, for six years. For a typical Ridgeview home, around $640,000, that's about $10 a month, or about $122 a year."
*(Outside Ridgeview, city median home of $410,000: about $6.50 a month, $78 a year.)*

**THE ASK**
"Can we count on your yes vote on Measure 4 in March?"
- **Yes:** "Thank you! Can we send you a reminder before the election?"
- **Undecided:** "What would help you decide?" *(Answer briefly and leave a flyer.)*
- **No:** "Thanks for your time. Have a good day." *(Don't argue. Move on.)*

**IF YOU HEAR…**
- **"It's a blank check."** "It has a fixed rate, 19 cents per $1,000, and it ends after six years. It raises $2.3 million a year for specific things: Sunday hours at all five branches, and keeping the Ridgeview branch open."
- **"I'm on a fixed income."** "That's fair. It's real money, and I won't pretend otherwise. For most homes here it's about $10 a month, and that keeps the branch in this neighborhood open."
- **"I rent."** "Your vote counts just as much. About 4 in 10 households in the city rent, and the branches serve everyone."
- **"The last one failed."** "It did, 48 to 52. It was close. That's why every conversation matters."

*Stick to these numbers. If you don't know an answer, say so, and take their contact info for follow-up.*
