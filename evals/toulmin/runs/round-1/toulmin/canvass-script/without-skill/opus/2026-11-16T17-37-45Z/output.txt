**Why the coffee line doesn't hold up**

- **City median home ($410,000):** $410,000 ÷ 1,000 × $0.19 = about **$78 a year, or $6.50 a month.** That's more than a cup of coffee at most cafés.
- **Ridgeview median home ($640,000):** about **$122 a year, or $10 a month.** That's roughly two cups.
- **"Average household" is also off.** 38% of households rent and get no levy bill directly, and $410,000 is the median home value, not the average.

Ridgeview is the worst place to overstate the cost. Many residents are retirees on fixed incomes who watch every bill, and a local leader is already calling this "a blank check." If a resident pulls out their assessment and finds the levy costs twice what our volunteer said, that one exchange could undo the whole visit. The real cost is still modest: **about 33 cents a day** for a typical Ridgeview home and about 21 cents a day citywide. Volunteers can say that without risk.

**Things to check before printing**

- **"Blank check" reply:** It points voters to the voter pamphlet for what the levy pays for. Make sure the ballot language actually names Sunday hours and the Ridgeview branch. If it's binding, say "it's written into the measure."
- **Senior relief:** If your state or county offers a property-tax exemption or deferral for seniors or people with disabilities, add one line under "Fixed income." It could be the most persuasive thing a volunteer says in Ridgeview. I didn't include one because I don't know your local rules.
