**YES ON MEASURE 4: Door Script (Ridgeview)**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm [name]. I'm a volunteer with Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**ASK, THEN LISTEN**
"Have you had a chance to think about Measure 4 yet?"
*(Listen first. Their answer tells you which lines below to use.)*

**WHY IT MATTERS HERE**
"If Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. The levy keeps it open, and it brings back Sunday hours at all five branches. People are using the library more than ever: visits are up 22% since 2021."

**WHAT IT COSTS (give the real number)**
"It's 19 cents for every $1,000 of assessed value, and it lasts six years. For a typical Ridgeview home, around $640,000, that comes to about $122 a year. That's about $10 a month, or roughly 33 cents a day."

**IF THEY SAY…**
- **"It's a blank check."** "It's a fixed rate, 19 cents per $1,000, and it ends after six years. The voter pamphlet lists exactly what it pays for."
- **"I'm on a fixed income."** "That's fair, and it's a real cost. For a typical home here it's about $10 a month. Many neighbors feel it's worth that to keep their branch open, but it's your call." *(Don't argue.)*
- **They rent.** "Measure 4 keeps the Ridgeview branch open and brings back Sunday hours across the city."

**CLOSE**
"Can we count on your yes on Measure 4? Ballots are due [DATE]."
*Thank them either way.*

**RECORD:** 1 Strong Yes · 2 Lean Yes · 3 Undecided · 4 Lean No · 5 Strong No · NH (Not Home)
