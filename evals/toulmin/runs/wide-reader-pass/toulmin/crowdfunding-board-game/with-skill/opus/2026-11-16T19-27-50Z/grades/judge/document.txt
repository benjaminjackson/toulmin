**A cooperative game about getting a family through the winter.**

The supply boat has stopped coming. The lamp still has to be lit every night. In this cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players, you work together to get a lighthouse keeper's family through to spring. You win together or you lose together.

**Why a lighthouse**

A lighthouse keeper's job is to keep a promise to strangers they'll never meet: the light will be on tonight. In a hard winter, keeping that promise means a family pulling together, sharing what little they have, and deciding together what can wait and what can't. That's the game. It works with one player at the kitchen table or four around it, and it's built for the kind of talk that happens when everyone at the table is on the same side.
