**A cooperative game about getting a family through the winter.**

The supply boat has stopped coming. The lamp still has to be lit every night. In this cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players, you work together to get a lighthouse keeper's family through to spring. You win together or you lose together.

**Tested hard before asking you for a dollar**

I'm not a publisher. I work full time as a land surveyor, and this is my first game and my first campaign. So I've done what I do at work: measure, check, and measure again.

- **140 playtest sessions** since 2023
- **About 60 different players**, not just friends and family
- **2 conventions**, where strangers sat down and played it
- **An 8.1 average rating** from 23 players on BoardGameGeek

Twenty-three ratings isn't a crowd, and I won't pretend it is. But every one of those ratings came from someone who played the game, and the 140 sessions behind them shaped every rule in the box. The game didn't get to this point by luck. Sixty people played it, broke it and told me what was wrong, and I fixed it.

And 1,240 of you have already signed up to hear when it launches.

**Where your $49 goes**

**$49 gets you a copy, shipped anywhere in the US.** The funding goal is **$28,000**, and I'd like you to see exactly how it adds up:

- **Manufacturing: $17,400.** This is a real factory quote for 1,500 copies, not an estimate.
- **Shipping to backers: about $9 a copy.** At the goal, that's about $5,150.
- **What's left: about $5,450,** which covers platform fees, payment fees and costs the quote doesn't include.

Reaching the goal takes about **572 backers**. That's fewer than half the people already on the launch list. If everyone who signed up backs the game on day one, we're funded.

**What a first-time creator owes you**

You're right to ask whether someone who has never published a game can deliver one. I can't point to past games. What I can do is show my numbers and keep showing them. The factory quote is in hand and the budget is above. I'll post an update every month until the game is at your door, and that includes the months when the news isn't good.

If the campaign doesn't reach $28,000, no one is charged.

**Why a lighthouse**

A lighthouse keeper's job is to keep a promise to strangers they'll never meet: the light will be on tonight. In a hard winter, keeping that promise means a family pulling together, sharing what little they have, and deciding together what can wait and what can't. That's the game. It works with one player at the kitchen table or four around it, and it's built for the kind of talk that happens when everyone at the table is on the same side.

I've spent three years making sure this game works. Now I need your help to get it printed.

**Back it at $49 and help the family get through to spring.**
