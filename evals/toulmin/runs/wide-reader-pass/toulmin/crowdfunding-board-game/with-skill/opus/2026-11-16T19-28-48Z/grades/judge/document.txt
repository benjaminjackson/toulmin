**One lighthouse. One family. One winter to get through together.**

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You don't play against each other. You play as a lighthouse keeper's family, and you all make it to spring together, or you don't.

**Tested at the table, not just on paper**

Since 2023 I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. I took the game to two conventions and put it in front of strangers. On BoardGameGeek, 23 people have rated it, and their average is 8.1 out of 10.

Twenty-three is a small number, and I won't dress it up. Nobody can buy this game yet, so there's no sales record to point to. What those numbers do show is that people who sit down with this game, including people who've never met me, want to play it again. Each of those 140 sessions changed something. The version you're backing is the one that came out the other end.

Before launch, 1,240 people signed up to hear when this campaign went live. If you're one of them, thank you. You're a big part of why I believed this was worth printing.

**Who's making it**

I'm a land surveyor. I work full time, and I designed this game in the hours around that job. This is my first published game and my first campaign.

I know that's the first thing a careful backer checks. I can't show you a track record, so I'll show you the numbers instead. In my day job I measure things carefully and sign my name to the result, and I've planned this campaign the same way.
