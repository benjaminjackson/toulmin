Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches close March 31. Every employee there is offered a new role

Team,

I want you to hear this from me directly. We will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches on March 31. Each of the 14 people who work at those two branches is being offered a job in our new member phone and chat center at the same pay or higher. Nobody at those branches loses their job unless they choose to.

**Why we're doing this**

Members have changed how they reach us. In the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. A member who calls us today waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and it isn't fair to the colleagues who answer once those 9 minutes are up.

The new center puts experienced people where members now come to us. The Maple Street and Route 9 teams know our members and our products, and a member on the phone needs exactly that.

**What changes for the people moving**

- **Pay:** the same as today or higher.
- **Hours:** the center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. That's a real change from the branches' 9-to-5 schedule, and it means some evening shifts. I'm not going to play that down.
- **Working from home:** after 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week.

**If the new role isn't right for you**

Anyone who decides not to take the new role will receive two weeks of pay for every year they've worked here. That's a fair choice to make, and we'll support you either way.

**What happens next**

In the next two weeks, we'll meet one-on-one with each person at Maple Street and Route 9. We'll go through the role, the shift rotation and your own situation. Please bring your questions, especially about schedules. That's where we most need to hear from you.

Closing a branch is not a small thing. Both of these teams have served their members well for years, and I'm grateful to every one of you. I believe the center is a better way to serve our members. For many of you it's also a better way to work: the same pay or more, two days a week at home, and members who reach a real person faster.

If you have questions before your meeting, reply to this email or come and find me.
