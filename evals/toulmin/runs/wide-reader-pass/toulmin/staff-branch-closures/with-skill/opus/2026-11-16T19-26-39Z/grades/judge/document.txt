**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: a new role for everyone who works there**

Team,

I want you to hear this from me first. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. All 14 people who work at those two branches are being offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, **at the same pay or higher.** Nobody is losing their job because of this decision.

**Why we're doing this**

Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. A member who calls us today waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we haven't kept up. When they call or start a chat, the person who answers should be someone who already knows them and knows how we do things. That means the people who serve them at the counter now.

**What changes for Maple Street and Route 9 staff**

- **Pay:** the same as you earn now, or higher.
- **Hours:** the center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. That means some of your days will start earlier or end later than the 9-to-5 you work now. I know this is a real change, especially if you have childcare or other commitments, and I'd like to hear about your situation before schedules are set.
- **Working from home:** after 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **If you choose not to take the new role:** you will receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here. For example, someone with 10 years here would receive 20 weeks of pay.

**Why I think this is the right move for our people**

The work at these two branches has been shrinking. The work in phone and chat has been growing fast. This change moves our people to the growing work and keeps every job. It protects every paycheck and gives staff a flexibility the branches never could: two days a week at home. And for all of us, it means members get a quicker answer instead of 9 minutes on hold.

**What happens next**

Within the next two weeks I'll meet with the Maple Street and Route 9 teams together, and then with each of you one-on-one. You can ask anything, tell me what you need from the schedule, and take time to decide. Please let us know your decision by [decision date]. If you have questions before then, contact me or [contact name].

I'm grateful for the work you do for our members every day, and I want to make this change with you, not to you.

Chief Operating Officer
