**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31 – new member phone and chat center**

Team,

I'm writing about a decision that affects all of us, and most directly our 14 colleagues at Maple Street and Route 9.

**On March 31, we will close the Maple Street and Route 9 branches.** Everyone who works at those two branches is being offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, **at the same pay or higher.** No one at either branch is losing their job because of this decision.

**Why we're doing this**

The way our members reach us has changed. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Today a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and it isn't fair to the colleagues answering those calls. We're moving our people to where our members are.

**What the new role looks like**

- **Pay:** the same as now, or higher.
- **Hours:** the center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, compared with 9 to 5 at the branches. Shifts will rotate, so some of your days will start earlier or end later than they do now.
- **Training:** 90 days.
- **Working from home:** after training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **Location:** [center address].

This is a different job from the one you have now. Evening shifts and serving members by phone and chat instead of in person are real changes, and each of you will weigh them for yourself.

**If you decide not to take the role**

You will receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here. For example, someone with eight years here would receive 16 weeks of pay.

**What happens next**

[Name/HR] will meet with each person at Maple Street and Route 9 individually by [date] to go through the offer and answer questions. We'll ask for your decision by [date]. Members will be notified on [date].

To everyone at our other five branches: members may ask you about this, and we'll make sure you have the details before they do.

To the Maple Street and Route 9 teams: you've served those members well, and that experience is exactly what members on the phone need. My door is open to any of you, and so is my inbox.
