**An Open Letter to [Management Company]**
**From the [Building Name] Tenants' Association**

Our building has nine stories, 112 apartments and one elevator. This year that elevator has been out of service on 41 days, which is more than one day in every eight. In August it was out for 12 days in a row.

For many of our neighbours, a broken elevator is more than an inconvenience. Twenty-three of our residents are over 75. Six use wheelchairs or walkers. For a resident in a wheelchair on an upper floor, a broken elevator can mean no way to get down to the street: no way to get to an appointment, to buy groceries, or out in an emergency.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and gave it a passing certificate. We are glad it is safe to ride. But a safety certificate does not tell anyone whether the elevator will be running tomorrow, and this year it too often has not been.

You have told us that a new controller part is on order and should arrive in six to eight weeks. You told us the same thing in June. Eight weeks from June ended in August, the month of our longest outage. Residents can no longer plan their lives around that estimate.

Seventy-four of our 112 households, two out of every three, signed a petition asking for a second elevator. You answered that it would cost about $400,000 and cannot be done in this building. We take that answer seriously. But you have not shown us the reasons behind it, and the problem the petition was about has not gone away.

We ask for four things:

1. **The part.** A copy of the order confirmation for the controller part, the supplier's delivery date, and the date by which it will be installed.
2. **A plan for outages until then.** Notice to every household as soon as the elevator stops working, an estimate of when it will be back, and help arranged for residents who cannot use the stairs.
3. **The reasons.** A written explanation of why a second elevator cannot be built here, and what you will do instead to keep one elevator running reliably for 112 households.
4. **A meeting** with the tenants' association to go through these answers.

We ask for a written reply by [date]. We will post your reply in the lobby beside this letter so that every resident can read it.

We want to work with you on this. But our neighbours who cannot take the stairs need more than another estimate.

[Name]
Secretary, on behalf of the [Building Name] Tenants' Association
