**Project Narrative: A Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food**

Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down, because we had no way to pick it up cold or keep it cold. A refrigerated van would let us say yes.

**Who we are**

We are an all-volunteer food bank serving a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs, roughly one pound in six. Last summer showed that what limits our fresh food is equipment, not supply.

**Our record with the Foundation**

In 2023 the Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late. That should not have happened. For this grant we commit to two reports: a short report on pounds collected, within 30 days after the first summer's pickups end, and a final report on or before the Foundation's due date.

**What the van will do**

At our usual 6,000 pounds a month, three summer months of fresh food comes to about 18,000 pounds. The 9,000 pounds we were offered would have added half again as much, or about 15 more pounds of fresh food for every household we serve.

Over a full year, that raises our fresh food from about 72,000 to about 81,000 pounds. We treat that as a floor, because it counts only the farm offers we have already received and had to refuse. With cold transport in place, we will also be able to accept cold donations at other times of year.

Four of our 23 regular volunteers have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so pickups will not depend on any one person.

**Our request and our share**

The van costs $68,000. We ask the Foundation for $50,000. We will cover the remaining $18,000, more than a quarter of the cost, from our own reserves. That brings our reserves down to $11,000. We are willing to do that because the food is already being offered to us, and we are already turning it away.

**How we will measure it**

We will weigh and log every load the van brings in. Our target for the first summer is 9,000 pounds of farm produce, the amount offered to us last year. Our report will give the actual figure against that target.

**If the van is not funded**

Next summer will go the way last summer did. The farms will offer, and we will have to say no.
