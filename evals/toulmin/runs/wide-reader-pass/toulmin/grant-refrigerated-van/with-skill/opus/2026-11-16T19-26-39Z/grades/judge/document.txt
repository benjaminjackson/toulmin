**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van, and we will pay the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. The van will let us accept the fresh produce that local farms offer us and that we now have to turn away.

**Our last grant from you**

In 2023 the Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late. That should not have happened. For this grant, one board member will be responsible for reporting. We will submit reports at six and twelve months, each showing the pounds of food the van collected and where it came from.

**The need**

Each month we distribute about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. About 6,000 pounds of that, fewer than one pound in six, is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

In summer, supply is not what holds us back. Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We turned all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or store it.

**What the van will do**

With a refrigerated van, we can collect from farms on a weekly schedule and keep the food cold on its way to us. Had we had the van last summer, it would have meant:

- 27,000 pounds of fresh food over those three months instead of 18,000, half again as much
- nearly 15 more pounds of fresh produce for each household we serve
- about 81,000 pounds of fresh food for the year instead of 72,000

Our goal for the first year is to accept every farm offer we receive and to collect at least the 9,000 pounds we turned away last summer. Our reports will show whether we met that goal, and if we fall short they will say why.

**Who will run it**

We are run entirely by volunteers, and 23 of them serve with us regularly. Four have already committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so the route will not depend on any one person.

**Our share**

We are not asking the Foundation to carry this alone. Our $18,000 is more than a quarter of the van's cost and nearly two-thirds of our reserves, and it will leave us with $11,000. We are making that commitment because the food is there for us to collect: the farms offered it last summer.

**If we do not get the van**

If farms make the same offers next summer, we will have to turn them down again. With your help, those 9,000 pounds of fresh produce will reach the 610 households we serve.
