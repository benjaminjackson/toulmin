**I rewrote the front desk training.** New hires now work the desk alone after 5 shifts instead of 9. That's almost half the time it used to take to get someone to work the desk alone.

Better facilities and a steadier team worked together, and the team is the part I'm responsible for.

- Before I take over the budget, I go through it line by line with you or whoever prepares it now.
- For my first three months, I review payroll results against budget with you every month, so we catch any problems early.

My results so far already help payroll. If each person who leaves is replaced by one new hire, the old turnover and old training meant about 81 paid training shifts a year (9 hires × 9 shifts). At current turnover and with the new training it's about 25 (5 × 5), roughly 70% fewer. I plan to keep managing the desk with that kind of cost in mind.

**What I'm asking.** I'm asking to be appointed Front Office Manager. For seven weeks the desk has run under me with no one else in the role, so there would be no handover and no time spent getting someone new up to speed. I'd welcome the chance to talk this through with you.
