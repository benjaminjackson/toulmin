I left out the line that every other publisher pays at least $12,000, because your own facts don't support it and it would hurt your case.

- **What your facts support:** one publisher paid you $12,500, and your agent puts the normal range at $11,000 to $13,000. That range starts below $12,000, so "every other publisher pays at least $12,000" isn't true according to your agent.
- **The problem with sending it:** the editor can easily check it with other illustrators or agents. If they find even one fee under $12,000, they'll doubt the rest of your email. It also works against your own request: if everyone pays at least $12,000, asking for $11,500 looks odd.
- **What I used instead:** the two real facts. One publisher paid you $12,500 last year, and your agent's range is $11,000 to $13,000. Together they show that $11,500 is a modest ask: it's below what you were paid elsewhere and near the bottom of the normal range. That's stronger than the broad claim.

The email also points out that your fee hasn't changed in four years even though your books have done well. It doesn't threaten to leave. It just shows what someone else paid and lets the editor draw the conclusion.

Subject: [Book title] – fee

Hi [Editor's name],

Thank you for offering me [Book title]. I'd love to illustrate it, and I'm glad to keep working with you after six books together.

Before I sign, I'd like to ask for a fee of $11,500 for this book.

I've been paid $9,000 per book since my first book with you four years ago. Since then, two of our books have each sold more than 20,000 copies, and one was picked for a state reading list. Each book still takes me about four months of work.

Other publishers pay more for the same work. Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a 32-page picture book of the same scope. My agent tells me $11,000 to $13,000 is the normal range for an illustrator with my record. $11,500 is near the bottom of that range and below what I was paid elsewhere.

I've really enjoyed making these books with you, and I'd like to keep doing it. I think this fee reflects what our books have achieved together. I'm happy to talk it through on a call if that's easier.

Best wishes,
[Your name]
