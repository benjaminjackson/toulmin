I wrote the email without the line "every other publisher pays at least $12,000." I think it would hurt your case, for three reasons:

- **Your facts don't support it.** You know of one publisher that paid $12,500. Your agent's range starts at $11,000, which actually contradicts "at least $12,000."
- **It works against your own number.** If every other publisher pays $12,000 or more, asking for $11,500 looks odd, and the editor may wonder what the claim is really based on.
- **It's easy to check.** Children's book editors usually know what illustrators get paid. If they catch one overstatement, they'll doubt the rest of the email too.

What you can say accurately is just as strong: one publisher paid you $12,500 last year, and your agent puts the normal range at $11,000–$13,000. That makes $11,500 look modest and loyal rather than greedy, so that's what the email says.
