Good evening, Commissioners. My name is [your name]. I've lived on Alder Road for nineteen years, two blocks from the church. I'm also a member of that church. So I'm here as a neighbor and as a member. And I'm asking you to approve the sixty-four apartments on our back lot.

I want to start with what my neighbors are worried about, because I'm one of them.

The traffic study says this project adds 310 car trips a day on Alder Road. It says the intersection at Alder and 4th drops from a C to a D at rush hour. I drive through that intersection. I'll be sitting in that line too.

I'm not going to stand here and tell you that doesn't matter. I'm telling you I've weighed it. And I think it's worth it.

Here's what's on the other side of the scale.

The county's own housing report says we are short about 2,100 affordable homes. That's the county's number, not mine.

Right now, 412 households are on the housing authority's waiting list. The average wait is three years. Three years of not knowing where you'll live.

Every one of these sixty-four apartments would rent to a household earning under sixty percent of the area median income. These are exactly the homes this county has said it doesn't have.

And the land is already there. The lot is 1.8 acres. It sits empty six days a week. This isn't a field or a forest. It's a parking lot.

On parking: the plan has seventy-two spaces for sixty-four apartments. That's a space for every apartment, and eight more.

Now, sixty-four homes won't close a gap of 2,100. I know that. But no single project will. The gap only closes one project at a time, on land like this, when someone is willing to build.

So here's the choice in front of you tonight.

If you vote yes, Alder and 4th gets slower at rush hour. My neighbors and I will feel that.

If you vote no, the intersection stays a C. And sixty-four households that could have had a home tonight stay on that list. The county stays 2,100 homes short. Nothing about that gets better by waiting.

I've lived on Alder Road for nineteen years. It's been a good place to live. I'd like the next family who needs a home here to have a chance at one.

Please vote yes. Thank you.
