Subject: Q update: flat MRR after losing our largest customer, 11 months of runway, reminders launching standalone next month

Hi all,

I didn't send an update for the last two quarters, and I should have. You'll get one every quarter from now on, including the bad quarters.

**The short version**
- MRR: $41,000, the same as three months ago
- Cash: $480,000. Net burn is about $44,000/month, so we have about 11 months of runway.
- Team: 6

**What happened**
We signed 9 new clinics and lost 4 customers. One of the 4 was our largest customer, a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500/month. They moved to the scheduler that comes with their electronic records system.

Without that chain, the rest of the business grew from $35,500 to $41,000 in MRR, about 15% in the quarter. New sales are working. But a single customer was big enough to wipe out a quarter of growth, and that's a concentration risk I should have flagged sooner.

**The bigger risk**
The chain didn't leave because our product got worse. They left because their records system now includes a scheduler, and one less vendor was worth more to them than a better tool. Other clinics on records systems with built-in schedulers can make the same choice. I think this is the main threat to our current product, and I'd rather you hear it from me.

**What we're doing about it**
Our strongest result has nothing to do with the calendar. Clinics using our automatic text reminders have a 7% no-show rate, down from 13% before they started. That's close to half the missed appointments gone, and every missed appointment is revenue the clinic loses.

So next month we start selling reminders on their own for $150/month. That lets us sell to clinics that schedule inside their records system, like the chain we just lost, instead of competing with a tool they get bundled in.

**What I'll report next quarter**
- How many clinics bought reminders on their own, and how many of them came from clinics we couldn't sell the full scheduler to
- MRR and runway, measured the same way as above
- Whether the standalone results change our plan for the next 11 months

**How you can help**
If you know owners of physical therapy clinics or chains, especially ones using their records system's scheduler, an introduction would help. We'll get them a reminders trial in front of them as soon as the product launches.

Thanks for your patience over the last six months. Reply with any questions. I'm happy to get on a call with anyone who wants to go through the numbers.
