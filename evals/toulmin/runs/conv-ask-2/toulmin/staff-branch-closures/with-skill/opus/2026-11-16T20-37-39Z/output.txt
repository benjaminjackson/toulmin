**Subject:** Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: what it means for you

To all staff,

I wanted you to hear this from me. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. The 14 people who work at those two branches will be offered jobs in a new member phone and chat center at our main office on Elm Avenue.

Here is why. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same three years, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Today a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. Members have changed how they reach us, and we haven't kept up. The new center puts our people where members are now looking for us.

To the 14 of you at Maple Street and Route 9: every one of you will be offered a job in the center, at your current pay or higher. Here is what the offer gives you and what it asks of you.

- **Hours will change.** The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule. Shifts will be picked by seniority, so the people who have been here longest choose first.
- **Working from home.** After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **Timing.** Written offers will go out by February 14, and your decision is due March 1.

If the new job isn't right for you, you'll get severance of two weeks' pay for every year you've worked here. If you take the job and decide within six months that it isn't working, you can still leave with that same severance. You don't have to know today whether you'll like it. You can try it.

I know this is a big change. Some of you chose branch work because you like seeing members in person, and evening shifts will be hard for some of you to fit around your lives. I won't pretend otherwise. What I can promise is this: every one of you has a job here if you want it, at the same pay or better, and anyone who tries it and finds it doesn't work has a way out.

If you'd like to talk this through, HR will meet one-on-one with anyone who asks. Please contact HR to set up a time.

Thank you for the care you've given our members at Maple Street and Route 9. That care is exactly what members calling us need.

Chief Operating Officer
