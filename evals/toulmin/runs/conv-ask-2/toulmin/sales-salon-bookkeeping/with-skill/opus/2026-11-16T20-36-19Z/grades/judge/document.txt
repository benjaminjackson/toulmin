## Clean books cost less at tax time

Your accountant charges for the time it takes to sort out your numbers. Last tax season, 9 of my clients told me their accountant charged them less because their books came in clean. One of them saved $800, which is four months of my fee.

## My deadline, and what happens if I miss it

I close each month's books by the 10th of the next month. Last year I missed that date 3 times. So since January, if I miss the 10th, you don't pay for that month.

Chair and Ledger is just me. When you call, you talk to the person who does your books. I answer the phone Tuesday through Saturday. I don't answer on Sundays or Mondays.
