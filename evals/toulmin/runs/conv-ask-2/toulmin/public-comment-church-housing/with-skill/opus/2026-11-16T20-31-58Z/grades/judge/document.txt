Good evening, commissioners. I've lived on Alder Road for 19 years, two blocks from the church whose permit you're considering tonight. I'm also a member of that church. I'm asking you to approve the permit, with the builder's traffic signal at Alder and 4th as a condition of approval.

I'll start with traffic, because I live with it. Some mornings around 8, cars already back up past my house. The traffic study says this project adds about 310 trips a day on Alder Road. It says that without changes, the intersection at Alder and 4th drops from a C to a D at rush hour. That's a real cost. I'm not going to stand here and tell you traffic won't get worse.

But the builder has offered to pay for a signal at that intersection, and the same study says that with the signal it stays at a C. That's why I'm asking you to make the signal a condition. If it's in the permit, it isn't optional.

On parking, the plan has 72 spaces for 64 apartments. And the church has put in writing that neighbors can park in its lot on weekdays when there's space.

Construction will take about 20 months, with trucks on Alder Road. That will be hard on my street. The church has promised to set up a neighbor group for the construction period, and I've committed to serving on it. If there are problems, I'll be one of the people taking them to the builder.

Now, what we get for it. The county's own housing report says we are short about 2,100 affordable homes. Right now 412 households are on the housing authority's waiting list, and the average wait is three years. All 64 of these apartments would rent to households earning under 60 percent of the area median income. They'd be built and run by Cascade Housing Partners, a nonprofit. And they'd go on 1.8 acres of parking lot that sits empty six days a week.

If the answer is no, that lot stays empty, and those 412 households keep waiting.

I'm the neighbor who will hear the trucks and sit at that light. I think 64 homes are worth it. Please approve the permit, with the signal as a condition. Thank you.
