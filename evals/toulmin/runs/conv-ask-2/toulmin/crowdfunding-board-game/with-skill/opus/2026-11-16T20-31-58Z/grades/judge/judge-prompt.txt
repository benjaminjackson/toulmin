# Lantern Winter

A lighthouse keeper's family has a long winter ahead. Lantern Winter is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players, and your job is to see them through to spring.

## It's finished

The art is done. The rules are done. I have a signed factory quote for 1,500 copies. This campaign doesn't pay to develop a game. It pays to print one that already exists.

## Proven at the table

Since 2023 I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. Every rule in the box has been through that table, many times over.

I took Lantern Winter to two conventions. On the main board game ratings site it has an average rating of 8.1 from 23 players. Most of those ratings came from convention-goers who sat down at my table, played it, and went home and rated it.

Then 1,240 people signed up to hear the moment this campaign went live. At $49 a copy, Lantern Winter is fully funded if fewer than half of them back it.

You don't have to take my word for how it plays. A board game reviewer with about 90,000 YouTube subscribers has a copy and is posting a video during launch week.

## Who's making it

I'm Owen Hale, and this is my first published game. I work full time as a land surveyor, a job where the numbers have to be right and I sign my name to them. I've brought that habit here, so here are the numbers.

## Where your money goes

The goal is $28,000:

- **$17,400:** the factory run of 1,500 copies, from a signed quote
- **About $5,150:** shipping to US backers at about $9 a copy, for the 572 backers it takes to reach the goal
- **About $2,800:** platform and payment fees, roughly 10%
- **About $2,650:** freight from the factory and import costs

## What you get, and what I promise

- **One copy of Lantern Winter for $49, with US shipping included.**
- **Expected delivery: November 2027.** That is the earliest date I can stand behind, so it's the only one I'll give you.
- **An update every two weeks until every copy has shipped.** You won't have to wonder what's happening.
- **A full refund on request if delivery is more than three months late.** Any backer can ask.
- **No stretch goals and no expansions.** You're backing one finished game, and that's what I'll ship.

## Why now

Without this campaign, Lantern Winter stays a finished game that nobody can buy. With 572 backers, it goes to the factory.

The campaign runs for 30 days from March 4. If you've played it at a convention, tell a friend. If you haven't, watch the review during launch week and decide for yourself.

Thank you for reading, and for helping me bring this family through the winter.

— Owen Hale

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
