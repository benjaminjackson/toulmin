The email is about 190 words. A few choices you may want to change:

- **Your agent isn't named.** You weren't sure whether they're involved, so the $11,000–$13,000 range is stated as the standard rate with no source. If your agent is fine being named, change it to "My agent tells me the standard rate…", which gives the number more weight.
- **Your $11,000 floor isn't in it.** The email asks for $11,500 and says only that $9,000 won't work, so you still have room to negotiate. Don't mention the gap in your schedule either.
- **"Dear Nadia"** reads warmly, but it isn't overly familiar for a managing editor you've worked with for four years.
- **Before you send:** if you know the state or the title of the reading-list book, add it. A named title is stronger than "one of them."
