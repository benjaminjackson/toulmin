I left out the claim about cutting new-hire training time in half. Here's why:

- **Nothing in your background supports it yet.** You haven't seen their current program, so a hiring manager would likely read it as a guess.
- **It could worry them.** In a GMP plant, much of the training is required by regulation and has to be documented. "Half the time" can sound like cutting corners on compliance, which is the main risk the person in this role has to manage.

Instead, the letter offers something you can actually deliver: training that's faster to absorb and easier to check. If you want a number in there, the interview is a better place for it. Once you've asked how long onboarding takes now and where people get stuck, you can propose a specific target.

A few notes:
- **Google Classroom:** I wrote that you "moved your courses fully onto" it. If that's not accurate, change it to something true, like "I use Google Classroom to manage coursework for five classes."
- **Training records:** If you've done any kind of written sign-off or competency check for students or new teachers, name it. It's your strongest link to GMP training records.
- **Length:** At roughly 380 words, it should fit on one page with normal margins.
