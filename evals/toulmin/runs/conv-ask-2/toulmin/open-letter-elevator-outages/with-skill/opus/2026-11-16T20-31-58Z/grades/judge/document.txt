**An Open Letter to Keystone Property Group**

To: Diane Weller, Regional Manager, Keystone Property Group
From: The tenants' association of Harbor View Apartments, 220 Grant Street

Harbor View has nine floors, 112 apartments and one elevator. So far this year, that elevator has been out of service on 41 days. That is about one day in every eight. In August it was out for 12 days in a row.

For many of us, a broken elevator is a hard climb. For some of our neighbours it means being trapped at home. Twenty-three residents of this building are over 75, and six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the elevator stops, they cannot get to the doctor, the pharmacy or the grocery store. In an emergency they cannot get out quickly.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and issued a passing certificate, and we are glad it is safe. But a safe elevator that keeps breaking down does nothing for a resident on the ninth floor who cannot use the stairs.

Keystone has told us that a new controller part is on order and should arrive in six to eight weeks. We were told the same thing in June. As far as we know, the part has still not arrived.

We are asking Keystone for three things:

1. **A written date for the repair.**
2. **A stair-climbing chair, with trained staff to operate it,** for residents who cannot use the stairs while the elevator is out.
3. **A rent credit for every day the elevator is out of service.**

We will do our part. The association will keep a list of residents who need help on the stairs and share it with Keystone so they can be reached quickly in an emergency. We can meet with you any weekday evening.

Seventy-four households have signed a petition asking for a second elevator. Keystone has told us a second elevator would cost about $400,000 and cannot be built in this building. We are not engineers, and we are not asking you to take our word over yours. We are asking you to share the estimate and the reasons behind it, so residents can see the numbers for themselves.

Until the elevator is reliable, every breakdown traps the same neighbours in their homes. A repair date, a way down the stairs and fair rent for days without service are not much to ask. We look forward to your written reply.

Sincerely,

Rosa Delgado
Secretary, Harbor View Apartments tenants' association
On behalf of the 74 households who signed our petition
