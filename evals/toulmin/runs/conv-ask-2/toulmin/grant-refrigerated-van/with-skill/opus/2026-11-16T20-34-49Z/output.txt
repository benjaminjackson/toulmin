**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van for Millbrook Community Pantry**

Last summer, local farms offered Millbrook Community Pantry about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down. We had no way to pick it up cold, and it would have spoiled before it reached the families it was meant for. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van so that next season we can say yes.

**Who we serve**

Millbrook Community Pantry is run entirely by volunteers in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. Only about 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

**What the van changes**

We're not short of fresh food on offer, and the families are already coming to us. What we lack is a way to bring that food in cold. Two of the farms that made offers last summer have given us letters saying they will give again if we can collect it cold. If this season's offers match last summer's, the van would bring in about 3,000 more pounds a month during the harvest months. That would raise the fresh food we hand out in those months by half. We can't promise the farms will offer the same amount, so we will report what actually comes in.

Four of our 23 regular volunteers have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule.

**Cost and our share**

The van costs $68,000. We will cover $18,000 from our own reserves, leaving us $11,000. We are putting more than a quarter of the cost on the table because this is the single biggest thing holding back how much fresh food we can offer. Insurance and fuel will cost about $4,500 a year, and that is not yet in our budget. Maintenance will add to that.

**Our last grant from the Foundation**

In 2023 the Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of those months, and no one else was assigned to the report. We have fixed that. Our current treasurer, Dave Lin, will be responsible for the final report on this grant, due 30 days after the grant year ends. We will also send the Foundation our fresh food totals every quarter, so you can see from the first harvest whether the van is doing what we say it will.

**If we don't get the van**

Without cold transport, we will keep turning away food that farms want to give. Last summer that came to 9,000 pounds. It's food our 610 households could have had, and it went to waste.

We ask the Foundation for $50,000 to help us bring it in.

Joan Pruitt
Director, Millbrook Community Pantry
