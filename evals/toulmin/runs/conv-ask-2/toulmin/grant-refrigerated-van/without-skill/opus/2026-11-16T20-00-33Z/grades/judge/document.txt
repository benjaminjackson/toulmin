**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food Recovery**

**The Need**

[Food Bank Name] is a volunteer-run food bank serving 610 households in [Town], a community of about 9,000 people. We distribute about 38,000 pounds of food each month. Only about 6,000 pounds of that, roughly 16 percent, is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

There's plenty of fresh food nearby. What limits us is that we can't move it cold. Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down because we had no refrigerated vehicle to collect it and no way to keep it cold until distribution. That produce was grown a few miles from families who needed it, and it never reached them.

**The Project**

We request $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves, more than 60 percent of what we hold, because this van addresses the biggest gap in what we offer. The van will let us collect perishable donations directly from farms and keep them at safe temperatures from pickup to distribution.

**Expected Outcomes**

- Accept the farm donations we currently turn down. Based on last summer's offers, that is about 9,000 additional pounds of produce, which would increase our fresh food distribution by about 50 percent during the harvest months.
