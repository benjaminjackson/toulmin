Hollis Dairy

To the Admissions Committee:

I am the farm manager at Hollis Dairy, a 400-cow dairy operation, and I am writing to recommend Tomás Reyes for your two-year agricultural technology program. Tomás has worked for me for three years, since he was 19, and he is one of the people I rely on most.

Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift on his own, five days a week. Nobody else is on the farm at that hour. He is responsible for the herd, the equipment and anything that goes wrong until the rest of the crew arrives. In three years he has missed two shifts.

He has also taken on work that goes beyond milking. He learned our herd management software and now uses it to flag sick cows for our veterinarian. Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early, and our vet estimated that this saved the farm about $8,000. That is the kind of work your program teaches: using data and technology to make better decisions about animal health. Tomás is already doing it, and he learned it on the job.

Last summer his father became seriously ill in Mexico, and Tomás had to leave for five weeks. He told me two days before he left. In those two days he arranged for his cousin, who had worked for us before, to cover all of his shifts. He came back on the day he said he would. He handled a family emergency in a way that protected the farm, and that told me a lot about him.

You should know that his written English is weaker than his spoken English, which is good. He knows this and is taking a night class to improve it. When he took a dairy safety course, he failed the written test the first time and passed it the second. I expect he will approach written coursework the same way: if something doesn't go well the first time, he will keep working at it until he gets it.

While he is in the program, I will keep a part-time weekend job open for him here, so he can keep working with the herd while he studies.

I recommend Tomás without reservation. If you would like to discuss him further, please call me at 555-0187.

Sincerely,

Mike Hollis
Farm Manager, Hollis Dairy
