Dear Admissions Committee,

Five days a week, Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift by himself. At that hour there is no one else to check the work. The cows, the equipment and the start of our whole day depend on him getting it right, and he does. In three years he has missed two shifts.

He also learned our herd management software, and he now uses it to flag sick cows for our veterinarian. Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early.
