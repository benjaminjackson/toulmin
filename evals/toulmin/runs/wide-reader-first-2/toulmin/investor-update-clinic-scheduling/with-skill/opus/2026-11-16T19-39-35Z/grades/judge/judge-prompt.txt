**Subject: Quarterly update: MRR flat at $41K, lost our largest customer, reminders launching on their own**

Hi all,

First, an apology. I didn't send updates for the last two quarters. You should have heard from me, especially going into a quarter like this one. Here is where things stand, good and bad.

**The numbers**
- **MRR:** $41,000, the same as three months ago
- **Clinics:** 9 added, 4 lost
- **Cash:** $480,000
- **Net burn:** about $44,000/month, which gives us roughly 11 months of runway (into October 2027) at the current rate

**We lost our largest customer**
A 12-clinic chain paying $5,500/month left us for the scheduler built into their electronic records system. That was about 13% of our revenue.

The rest of the business grew enough to cover it. Without that chain, MRR went from $35,500 to $41,000, about 15% growth in one quarter. That's why the headline number is flat rather than down.

The risk is real all the same. Records systems that come with a scheduler are a threat to more customers than this one, and I'd rather say so than have you raise it first.

**What's working: reminders**
Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a no-show rate of 7%, down from 13% before they started. That's nearly half as many missed appointments. It's the clearest result we deliver, and it's the reason for our next step.

**What we're doing next**
Next month we start selling the reminder feature on its own for **$150/month**. A clinic will no longer have to replace its scheduler to buy from us. That opens us up to clinics that are happy with the scheduler they already have, which is exactly the kind of customer we just lost.

**Where you can help**
- **Introductions:** if you know PT clinic owners or operators, especially ones using the scheduler in their records system, an introduction would help. They're the first audience for standalone reminders.
- **Your view:** if you have thoughts on the standalone pricing or on our runway, I'd like to hear them. I'm glad to get on a call with any of you. Just reply to this email.

Thank you for your patience over the last six months.

[Your name]
</document>

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
