The rest of the business grew enough to cover it. Without that chain, MRR went from $35,500 to $41,000, about 15% growth in one quarter. That's why the headline number is flat rather than down.

Next month we start selling the reminder feature on its own for **$150/month**. A clinic will no longer have to replace its scheduler to buy from us. That opens us up to clinics that are happy with the scheduler they already have, which is exactly the kind of customer we just lost.
