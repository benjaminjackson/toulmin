**One winter. One lighthouse. One family that has to make it to spring.**

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You play a lighthouse keeper's family getting through the hardest season of the year. You win together or you lose together. You can also play it alone.

**Proven at the table: 140 playtests**

Since 2023 I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. Each session showed me something to fix, and I fixed it. I've taken the game to two conventions and watched strangers play it.

On the main board game ratings site, 23 players have rated it. The average is 8.1.

Twenty-three isn't a big number, and I won't pretend it is. But those are 23 people who played an unfinished game and gave it an 8.1. Then 1,240 people signed up to hear when this campaign launched. That's why I'm here.
