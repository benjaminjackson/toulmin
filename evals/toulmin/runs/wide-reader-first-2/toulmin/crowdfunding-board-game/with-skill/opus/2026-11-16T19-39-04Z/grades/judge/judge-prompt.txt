# Keep the light burning. Get your family through the winter.

**[Game Name]** is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You play a lighthouse keeper's family through one long winter. You don't play against each other. You plan together, make hard choices together, and win or lose together.

## Played 140 times before you see it

I've been building this game since 2023. In that time it has been played in **140 playtest sessions by about 60 different people**. It went to **two conventions**, where strangers sat down and played it cold. On BoardGameGeek it has an **average rating of 8.1 from 23 players**.

That's not a huge number of ratings, and I won't pretend it is. But they come from people who actually played the game, often more than once, and many of them told me what was broken. Most of what's in the box today is there because of them.

## Who's making it

I'll be straight with you: this is my first published game. I'm not a publishing company. I work full time as a land surveyor, and I designed this game in the evenings and on weekends.

So here's what I have done before asking for your money:

- **I have a factory quote in hand:** $17,400 to print 1,500 copies.
- **I know my shipping costs:** about $9 per copy to reach backers in the US.
- **I set the goal to cover the real costs**, not to look good on the funding bar.

## Where your money goes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Funding goal** | $28,000 |
| **Your price** | $49, including US shipping |
| **Printing (factory quote, 1,500 copies)** | $17,400 |
| **Shipping to you** | about $9 per copy |
| **Backers needed to fund** | about 572 |

The rest of the goal covers platform and payment fees and the costs that come up between the factory and your door.

## 1,240 of you already said "tell me when"

That's how many people signed up for this launch email. If a little under half of you back the game, it gets made. That's the whole plan. There's no big publisher behind it, just the people who wanted to know when it went live.

## What I'm asking

**Back [Game Name] at $49.** That gets you a copy with US shipping included. If you've played it at a table or a convention, please tell a friend who likes co-op games. If you have questions about the game, the factory, or the timeline, ask me in the comments. I'll answer every one myself.

Thank you for getting this far. Let's get the family through the winter.
</document>

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
