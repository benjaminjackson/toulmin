**An Open Letter to [Management Company]**
From the [Building Name] Tenants' Association
November 16, 2026

Our building has 112 apartments on nine floors. All of us share one elevator.

So far this year, that elevator has been out of service on 41 days. One outage in August lasted 12 days in a row. For more than a month of this year, getting home meant climbing the stairs, as many as eight flights.

For many of our neighbours, the stairs are not an option. Twenty-three residents are over 75, and six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the elevator stops, they cannot leave their homes. If they are already out, they cannot get back.

We know the elevator passed its city inspection in September, and we are glad it is safe when it runs. But the certificate tells us nothing about how often it doesn't run. Our neighbours are not stuck indoors because the elevator is unsafe. They are stuck because it is so often out of service.

In June, you told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. That meant mid-August at the latest, which is the month the elevator was out for 12 days. It is now November, and we have been told the same thing again: six to eight weeks. Residents cannot plan around a date that keeps moving.

**We ask you to reply in writing to all residents and answer three questions:**

1. When was the controller part ordered, from whom, and what delivery date has the supplier confirmed?
2. Until the repair is finished, how will residents who cannot use the stairs be helped during an outage?
3. On the second elevator: what is the assessment behind the $400,000 estimate and the conclusion that it cannot be done in this building?

That last question matters to most of this building. Seventy-four of our 112 households, two in every three, signed our petition asking for a second elevator. We are not asking you to ignore the cost. We are asking you to show residents how you reached your answer, so we can understand it and talk with you about what can be done.

We would welcome a meeting with you to go through your reply. We want the same thing you do: a building where every resident can get to their own front door.

Sincerely,

[Name]
Secretary, on behalf of the [Building Name] Tenants' Association
