I recommend not saying the change will make everyone's job better. Your facts don't back that up, and the 14 people moving would know it isn't true on the first day.

- **Better:** pay stays the same or goes up, there are two work-from-home days after training, and the work is where members now contact you. That's a 60% rise in phone and chat, with members waiting 9 minutes on hold.
- **Harder:** the branch is gone, the hours run from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. instead of 9 to 5, shifts rotate, and work from home only starts after 90 days.

If the email says "better for everyone," staff will measure it against the rotating shifts and stop trusting the rest of it. The draft below says plainly what gets better and what gets harder. It runs about 400 words, and every figure in it is one you gave me.
