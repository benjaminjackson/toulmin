**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: what it means for you**

To all staff,

I want you to hear this from me directly and at the same time as everyone else. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. All 14 people who work at those two branches will be offered a role in our new member phone and chat center.

**If you work at Maple Street or Route 9, here is what this means for you:**

- **You have a job.** Every one of you is offered a role in the center.
- **Your pay will not go down.** Every offer is at your current pay or higher.
- **Your hours will change.** The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. Some of your shifts will start earlier or end later than the 9-to-5 day you work now.
- **You can work from home.** After 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week.
- **If you decide not to take the role,** you will receive two weeks of pay for every year you have worked here.

**Why we are doing this**

Over the last three years, visits to these two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Our members have changed how they reach us. Right now, a member who calls waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give them, and it isn't fair to the colleagues who answer those calls.

The people best placed to fix that are the ones who already know our members and our products. That's you.

**What I won't pretend**

This isn't an easy change. Closing a branch means leaving a place you know, the people you work beside and members who know you by name. The center's day is longer than the branch day, and your schedule will vary. If the new hours are a problem for you because of childcare, a second job or anything else, please tell me. I would rather know now.

Here is what I can tell you for certain: the role keeps your pay whole or raises it, gives you two days a week at home once training is done, and puts you where our members most need help today.

**What to do now**

Please read this carefully and take some time with it. If you have questions about the role, the hours or the severance, reply to this email or call me. I will talk with anyone who wants to, one on one.

Thank you for everything you have done at Maple Street and Route 9. I'm grateful for it.

Chief Operating Officer
