**Project Narrative: A Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food**

Before anything else, we want to address our last grant. In 2023 the Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving, and our final report reached you four months late. That was our responsibility, and we understand it bears on how you weigh this request.

**The need**

We are a volunteer-run food bank in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we distribute about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households, roughly 62 pounds per household. Only about 6,000 pounds of that, less than one-sixth, is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

The limit is not supply. Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We turned all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or store it cold. That is about 3,000 pounds a month, half again what we now distribute in fresh food, that never reached the families we serve.

**What the van changes**

A refrigerated van lets us collect from farms at harvest and keep that food cold on the way to our neighbors. Without one, the same offers will come next summer and we will have to refuse them again.

Our first-year goal is to accept the farm produce we have been turning away: at least 9,000 additional pounds of fresh food, raising our fresh distribution from about 6,000 to about 9,000 pounds a month during harvest season. For each household, that is roughly five more pounds of fresh food a month in those months. Our monthly distribution figures will show plainly whether we reach it.

**Who will run it**

We have 23 regular volunteers. Four have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so pickups will not depend on any one person.

**Our request**

The van costs $68,000. We ask the Foundation for $50,000. We will cover the remaining $18,000, more than a quarter of the cost, from our own reserves, which will bring them down to $11,000. We are willing to make that commitment because the farm food is already being offered; what we lack is the means to bring it in.

We would welcome any questions, and we are grateful for the Foundation's past support of our work.
