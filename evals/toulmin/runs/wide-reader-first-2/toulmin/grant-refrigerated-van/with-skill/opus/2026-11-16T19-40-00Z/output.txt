**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

In 2023 the Foundation granted us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant reached you four months late. You were entitled to it on time, and we understand that you will weigh it as you read this request.

**The need**

Our food bank is run entirely by volunteers and serves a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we distribute about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. That works out to roughly 10 pounds of fresh food per household each month, and less than a sixth of everything we hand out.

The fresh food is not limited by supply. Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We turned it all down because we had no way to collect it and keep it cold. It was grown in this county and offered free, and it never reached the families who rely on us.

**What the van changes**

A refrigerated van lets us collect farm produce and other perishable donations without them spoiling on the way. If we had owned one last summer, the produce we turned away would have increased our fresh-food distribution by half in each of those three months. We would have gone from about 6,000 pounds a month to about 9,000, or about 15 pounds per household instead of 10.

Our goal for the van's first summer is to accept the farm produce we are offered instead of refusing it. We will measure our results against the 9,000 pounds we had to turn away last year and report them to the Foundation.

If we cannot buy the van, nothing about last summer changes. The farms will offer, and we will have to say no again.

**Who will drive it**

We have 23 regular volunteers. Four of them have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so pickups will not depend on any one person.

**The cost and our share**

The van costs $68,000. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000. We will pay the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. That is more than a quarter of the purchase price and most of the savings we hold. After the purchase, our reserves will be $11,000. We are making that commitment because cold transport is the one thing standing between local farm produce and the 610 households we serve.

**Our request**

We respectfully ask the Foundation for $50,000 toward the purchase of a refrigerated van. We would be glad to answer any questions or provide more detail on any part of this request.
