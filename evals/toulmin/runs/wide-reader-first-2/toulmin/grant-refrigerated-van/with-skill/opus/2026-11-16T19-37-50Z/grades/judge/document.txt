**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

**Our history with the Foundation**
In 2023 the Foundation granted us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late. You were entitled to receive it on time, and we did not deliver it. We want to say so plainly at the start of this request.

**Who we serve**
We are a volunteer-run food bank in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we distribute about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households, roughly 62 pounds per household. About 6,000 pounds of that each month is fresh produce, milk and eggs, around 16 percent of everything we hand out.

**The problem**
Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We turned all of it down. We had no way to pick it up cold and nowhere to store it.

That works out to 3,000 pounds a month on top of the 6,000 pounds of fresh food we already distribute. It would have raised the fresh food reaching our households by half during those months. Without a refrigerated vehicle, we will have to give the same answer to the same offer next summer.

**The request**
We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a refrigerated van that costs $68,000. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. For an organization our size this is a serious commitment, because it brings our reserves down to $11,000. We are making it because the farms have shown they are willing to give, and a cold vehicle is what we lack.

**Who will run it**
We have 23 regular volunteers. Four of them have already committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so it will have drivers from the first week.

**What the van makes possible**
With a refrigerated van we can say yes to the kind of offer we had to refuse last year. In practice that means about 9,000 pounds of farm produce over a summer going to 610 local households and not going unclaimed. Fresh food is currently about one pound in every six we hand out. Of all the food we distribute, it is the hardest for us to handle and the most valuable to the families who receive it.

**Summary**
- Total cost of van: $68,000
- Requested from the Foundation: $50,000
- Our contribution from reserves: $18,000
- Produce turned down last summer for lack of cold transport: about 9,000 pounds
- Volunteer drivers committed weekly: 4

We would welcome the chance to talk with you about this request or answer any questions.
