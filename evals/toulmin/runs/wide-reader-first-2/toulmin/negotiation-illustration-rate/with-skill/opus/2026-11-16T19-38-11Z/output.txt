I didn't include the line about "every other publisher pays at least $12,000." Your facts don't support it. One other publisher paid you $12,500, and your agent says the normal range starts at $11,000. If the publisher knows a single illustrator paid less than $12,000, the claim falls apart, and your whole request looks weaker with it. The draft uses what you can actually back up: you got $12,500 elsewhere last year, your agent's range is $11,000–$13,000, and you're asking for $11,500, which is below what you already got elsewhere. That makes your number look reasonable rather than pushy.

Some things that would make it stronger, if they're true:
- The title of the book that made the state reading list.
- The other publisher's name, if you're comfortable sharing it.
- A start date or delivery window you can commit to, so saying yes is easy for them.

Put the editor's name in before sending. It's about 200 words.
