Before you send it:

- **Placeholders:** You'll need to add Tomás's last name, the farm's name, the college's name, and your name and contact details.
- **The 5-week absence:** I included it, framed as a family emergency he came back from. A college probably won't see it as a mark against him. Mentioning it also shows you're being straight with them, which makes the rest of the letter more believable. I left out that he gave only 2 days' notice. That matters to an employer more than to a school. If you'd prefer to leave the whole thing out, you can cut that paragraph and the letter still holds up.
- **Written English:** I mentioned it because the program will see his written work anyway. If you raise it first and point to the night class, it reads as a strength. You can cut this paragraph too if you don't want to bring it up.
- **One thing to check:** I said he came back "to the same shift and the same standard of work." I assumed that because he still works for you, so please confirm it's accurate.
