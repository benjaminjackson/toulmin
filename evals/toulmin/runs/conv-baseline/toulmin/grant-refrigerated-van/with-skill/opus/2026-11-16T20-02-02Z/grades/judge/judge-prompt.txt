**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We turned all of it down. We had no way to pick it up and keep it cold.

We are a volunteer-run food bank serving 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food. Only about 6,000 pounds of that, roughly one pound in six, is fresh produce, milk or eggs.

The Foundation has supported us before, with $12,000 for shelving in 2023. Our final report on that grant was four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period. Our current treasurer, Dave Lin, will be personally responsible for the final report on this grant, due 30 days after the grant year ends. We will also send the Foundation our fresh food totals every quarter, so you can see what the van is bringing in as the year goes on.

**What the van changes**

The food is already available. Last summer's offers came to about 3,000 extra pounds a month. Two of those farms have written to us saying they will give again if we can pick up cold. We can't promise they will offer the same amount next summer. If they do, the van would raise our summer fresh food distribution by half, from about 18,000 to 27,000 pounds. That is roughly 15 more pounds of fresh food for every household we serve.

The drivers are also in place. Four of our 23 regular volunteers have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule.

**Cost and our share**

The van costs $68,000. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 and paying the remaining $18,000, more than a quarter of the cost, from our own reserves. Insurance and fuel will come to about $4,500 a year. After our contribution, our reserves of $11,000 would cover those costs for more than two years while we build them into our operating budget.

**If we don't get the van**

Without a refrigerated van, next summer will look like last summer. Farms will offer us fresh food, and our volunteers will have to say no.

**How you will know it worked**

We will count every pound of fresh food the van collects and report the totals to the Foundation each quarter. At the end of the year, you will be able to set those numbers against the 9,000 pounds we turned away last summer.
