**Subject: Changes to our Maple Street and Route 9 branches**

Team,

I want to share an important decision with you directly. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches and open a new member phone and chat center. Every one of the 14 colleagues at those two branches will be offered a role in the center at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're making this change**

How our members reach us has changed. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Today, members who call us wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and it puts real strain on everyone answering those calls.

The new center puts experienced people where members need them most. Our Maple Street and Route 9 colleagues know our members and our products. That knowledge will help us answer members faster and get it right the first time.

**What this means for Maple Street and Route 9 staff**

- You are offered a role in the center at the same pay or higher.
- The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, compared with branch hours of 9 to 5. Shifts will rotate, so some of your days will start earlier or end later than they do now.
- After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- If you decide not to take the new role, you will receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

I know this is a big change. Moving from a branch to phone and chat work, and to rotating hours, is a real adjustment, and it will suit some of you more than others. Your manager and HR will meet with each of you one-on-one by [date] to go over the role, the schedule and your options. You'll have until [date] to decide, and you can ask as many questions as you need to before then.

**What this means for everyone else**

If you work at one of our other five branches, your location, role and hours aren't changing. [Add one line on any expected impact, such as members from the closing branches moving to nearby locations.]

Thank you for the care you show our members every day. I'm grateful to the Maple Street and Route 9 teams especially, and I hope many of you will help us build something members will really feel.
