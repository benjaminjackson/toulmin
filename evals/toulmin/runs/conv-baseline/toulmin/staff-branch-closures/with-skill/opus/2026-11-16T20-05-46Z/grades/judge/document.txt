**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: what it means for you**

Team,

I want you to hear this from me first. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Each of the 14 people who work at those branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're doing this**

Over the last three years, visits to these two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we haven't kept up. A member who calls us today waits 9 minutes on hold on average. That isn't the service our members deserve. The people best placed to fix it are experienced staff who already know our members and our products.

**What the new role offers**

Everyone at both branches is being offered a role, so no one there loses their job because of this change. Pay will be the same or higher. After 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week. And you'll be working in the part of the credit union that's growing.

**What will be harder**

The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. The branches were open 9 to 5, so this means some evening work, and I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule. We haven't set shift lengths or the rotation pattern yet, and I'll share them as soon as they're decided. What I can promise now is that staff with the most years here will pick their shifts first.

I know this schedule won't work for everyone. If the new role doesn't fit your life, you can turn it down and receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here. That's a real option, and no one will think less of anyone who takes it.

**What happens next**

- **By February 14:** everyone at Maple Street and Route 9 will receive a written offer.
- **By March 1:** please let us know your decision.
- **Anytime before then:** if you'd like a one-on-one conversation with HR, you can have one. [How to book.]

To everyone at Maple Street and Route 9: thank you. Closing a branch is hard on the people who built it, and I don't take that lightly. To everyone else: please support your colleagues through this change.

If you have questions, reply to this email or talk to HR.
