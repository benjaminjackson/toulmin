Subject: Closing Maple Street and Route 9, and opening our member phone and chat center

Team,

I want you to hear this directly from me. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Each of the 14 people who work at those branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher. The center will be at our main office on Elm Avenue.

**Why we're doing this**

The way members reach us has changed. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Today a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and it isn't fair to the colleagues answering those calls. The new center puts more of our people where members are now trying to reach us.

**What changes for the Maple Street and Route 9 teams**

Some of this is good news, and some of it is a real change. I want to be plain about both.

- Your job is secure, at the same pay or higher.
- After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. The branches are open 9 to 5. Shifts will rotate, and staff with the most years here will pick their shifts first. I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule, and I know that will be hard for some of you.
- The work itself is different. Helping members by phone and chat is not the same as serving them across a counter.

**You have a choice**

Written offers will go out by February 14, and decisions are due March 1. If you decide not to take the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here. If you take the role and find within six months that it isn't right for you, you can still leave with that same severance. You don't have to know for certain now.

Anyone who wants a one-on-one with HR before deciding can have one. Please contact [HR contact] to set it up.

**For everyone else**

The people at Maple Street and Route 9 have served our members well for years. Whether they join the center or decide to move on, please give them your support over the next few months.

I'll share more details as we have them. If you have questions in the meantime, my door is open.

Thank you,

[Your name]
Chief Operating Officer
