Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches: what's changing and what it means for you

To everyone at Maple Street and Route 9,

On March 31, we will close the Maple Street and Route 9 branches. I want you to hear the whole picture from me, including the parts that won't be easy.

The most important thing first: every one of you will be offered a job. All 14 of you will be offered a role in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're doing this**

Over the last three years, visits to your two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union went up 60%. Members who call us now wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we need to be there when they do. You know our members and our products better than anyone we could hire, and the center needs exactly that.

**What this means for your job**

A branch that keeps losing visits is not a secure place to work. The center is where our work is growing. After 90 days of training, you'll be able to work from home two days a week, which a branch job has never offered.

**What's harder**

The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. I can't promise anyone 9 to 5. Staff with the most years here will choose their shifts first. We're still working out how often each person will work until 8, and I'll tell you as soon as we know. I also know that answering phones and chats is different work from serving members face to face. You'll have 90 days of training to learn it.

**If the new role isn't right for you**

If you decide not to take it, you'll receive 2 weeks of pay for every year you've worked here. It's your choice to make.

**What happens next**

- You'll receive a written offer by February 14.
- Your decision is due by March 1.
- If you'd like a one-on-one meeting with HR before you decide, you can have one. Contact [HR contact name and email] to set it up.

These branches have served our members well because of you. I'm asking you to bring that same care to the members who are waiting for us on the phone. Thank you.

[Your name]
Chief Operating Officer
