Some notes before tonight:

- **Sunday parking:** Someone may ask where the congregation will park on Sundays if the lot becomes housing. You didn't tell me, so the speech doesn't mention it. If the plan covers it, have the answer ready. If you know it now, it's worth adding one sentence.
- **"Not a failing intersection":** In traffic ratings, F is the failing grade, so a D is not failing. Some counties do set D as the lowest grade they'll accept, though. If your county has a standard, check it so a commissioner can't catch you off guard.
- **Signal-timing review:** This is something you'd be asking for, not something in the plan. That's why the speech words it as a request. Drop it if you'd rather not ask.
- **Mentioning your membership:** I put it in the first line on purpose. If an opponent brings it up instead, it can make you look like you were hiding it. Saying it yourself also makes your point about living on Alder Road more believable.

Time yourself reading it aloud once. If you go over, cut the parking paragraph first.
