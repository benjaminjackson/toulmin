Good evening, Commissioners. My name is [name]. I've lived on Alder Road for 19 years, two blocks from [church name]. I'm also a member of that church, so I'll say that up front. I'm here to ask you to approve the 64 apartments on its back parking lot.

I'll start with traffic, because I'm one of the people who will sit in it. The study estimates 310 more car trips a day on Alder Road. It says the intersection at Alder and 4th drops from a C to a D at rush hour. That's my intersection. A D at rush hour means I'll wait at that light a little longer at the busiest times of day. I've weighed that against what's on the other side, and it isn't close.

Here's what's on the other side. Your own housing report says this county is short about 2,100 affordable homes. The housing authority has 412 households on its waiting list right now, and the average wait is three years. This project would house 64 of them. That's about one in every six families on that list. Every unit would rent to households earning under 60 percent of the area median income.

It does that without taking any open land or tearing anything down. That lot is 1.8 acres of asphalt that sits empty six days a week. I walk past it, and most days there's nothing there.

On parking, the plan provides 72 spaces for 64 units, more than one per home. [One sentence on where Sunday congregants will park, if known.]

On traffic, I'd ask the commission to make it a condition of approval that county staff review the signal timing at Alder and 4th once the building is occupied. That way the rush-hour delay gets managed instead of just accepted.

Think about what happens if you say no. The lot stays empty six days a week. The 412 families keep waiting, three years on average. The county's 2,100-home shortfall stays exactly where it is. And the traffic on Alder Road is the same traffic we have now.

I've lived on Alder Road for 19 years. I would rather have 64 families who finally got off that waiting list as my neighbors than keep an empty parking lot next door. Please approve this project. Thank you.
