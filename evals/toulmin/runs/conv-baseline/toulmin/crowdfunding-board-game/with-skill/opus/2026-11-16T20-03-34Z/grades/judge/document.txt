**A lighthouse. A family. One long winter.**

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You don't play against each other. You play for the lighthouse keeper's family, and together you have to see them through the winter.

**Proven at the table**

I'm not asking you to take a first-time designer's word that this game is good. I'm asking you to look at what happened when people played it.

Since 2023, the game has been played 140 times by about 60 different people. That's more than two sessions per player on average. Nobody sits down for a second and third game of something they didn't enjoy. Every one of those sessions helped shape the rules that will be in your box.

I've taken it to two conventions. On the main board game ratings site, the 23 people who have rated it give it an average of 8.1. That's not many ratings yet, and I won't pretend it is. But it's a strong start, and I'd rather show you an honest 23 than a number I can't back up.
