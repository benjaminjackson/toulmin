**AN OPEN LETTER TO KEYSTONE PROPERTY GROUP**
From the Harbor View Tenants' Association
Harbor View Apartments, 220 Grant Street

November 16, 2026

Ms. Diane Weller
Regional Manager, Keystone Property Group

Dear Ms. Weller,

Harbor View Apartments has nine floors, 112 apartments and one elevator. So far this year, that elevator has been out of service on 41 days, about one day in every eight. In August it was out for 12 days in a row.

For many of our neighbors, an outage is more than an inconvenience. Twenty-three residents of this building are over 75. Six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the only elevator stops, a resident in a wheelchair on an upper floor cannot get to a doctor's appointment, to the grocery store or to the street.

In June, Keystone told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. That window closed in August. It is now November, and we are being told the same thing again: six to eight weeks.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and issued a passing certificate. That certificate means the elevator is safe when it runs. It does not mean the elevator will run. Forty-one days out of service shows the difference.

We ask Keystone to do three things by November 30, 2026:

1. **Give tenants a written date** by which the repair will be completed.
2. **Provide a stair-climbing chair, and staff trained to use it,** so residents who cannot walk the stairs can get in and out of the building whenever the elevator is down.
3. **Credit tenants' rent** for every day the elevator is out of service.

Seventy-four of our 112 households, two in every three, also signed a petition asking for a second elevator. Keystone has told us that would cost about $400,000 and cannot be done in this building. If that is so, we ask Keystone to explain why in writing, and to include that explanation with its reply, so tenants can see what the decision is based on.

If nothing changes, we expect more of what we have had all year: another estimate, another wait, and more stretches like August, when our oldest and least mobile neighbors were cut off in their own homes.

Our members have waited since June. They asked us to make this letter public, and we are posting it in our lobby and sharing it with local news media. We look forward to your written reply by November 30.

Sincerely,

Rosa Delgado, Secretary
On behalf of the Harbor View Tenants' Association
