Hi all,

First, an apology. I didn't send updates for the last two quarters. That's on me, and you'll hear from me every quarter from now on.

The short version: revenue was flat this quarter because we lost our largest customer. We have about 11 months of runway. Our text reminder feature is clearly working, and next month we start selling it on its own.

**The numbers**
- MRR: $41,000 (flat vs. three months ago)
- New clinics: 9
- Customers lost: 4, including our largest
- Cash: $480,000
- Net burn: ~$44,000/month
- Runway: ~11 months
- Team: 6

**What happened**
Our largest customer, a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500/month, moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. New clinics made up for that loss and the other three, so MRR held steady. But we didn't grow.

This is the risk I take most seriously. Records vendors are bundling scheduling, and multi-site chains are the most likely to consolidate onto one system. Competing as a standalone scheduler against a free built-in option is a hard position, and this quarter showed it.

**What's working**
Clinics using our automatic text reminders have a 7% no-show rate, down from 13% before they started. That removes nearly half of their missed appointments. A no-show is a slot the clinic usually can't refill, so owners can put a dollar figure on this.

**What's next**
Next month we start selling reminders as a standalone product at $150/month. This lets us sell to clinics that already have a scheduler, including the ones built into their records system, instead of competing with it. The customer we just lost is exactly the kind of clinic this is built for.
