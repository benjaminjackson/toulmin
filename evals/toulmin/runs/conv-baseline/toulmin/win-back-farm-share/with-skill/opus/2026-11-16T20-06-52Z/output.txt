The main thing working against you is a grievance, not the price. These members paid full price for six weeks of half boxes, got no credit, and got one short email. So the email apologises first and then shows what has changed.

<final_document>
Subject: We let you down last summer. Here's what we've changed.

Hi [Name],

You didn't renew your share this year, and I think I know why.

After the July 9 hailstorm destroyed about half our crops, your box was half its usual size for six weeks. You'd paid for full boxes. We didn't offer a refund or credit, and I sent you one short email about it. That wasn't good enough, and I'm sorry.

Here's what we've done since then:

- We added a second field on higher ground, so all our crops aren't in one place anymore.
- We put up hail nets over our tomatoes and peppers.
- Members can now pause their share for up to two weeks and get credit for those weeks.

Together, the field and the nets cost $31,000. A full share is still $540, the same as last year.

I can't promise good weather. I can promise that if something goes wrong again, you'll hear from me quickly and you'll get the whole story.

Sign-up closes March 15, and 52 shares are left. I'd really like to have you back.

[Your name]
[Farm name]
</final_document>

Two things to check before you send it:

- **The promise near the end is mine, not yours.** You didn't tell me you'd communicate differently. I added it because poor communication was half the complaint. Only keep it if you'll actually do it.
- **There's no make-good offer.** The half boxes were worth about $74 per member (half of $540 ÷ 22 weeks × 6 weeks). The apology would carry more weight with a returning-member credit of around that amount. I didn't put one in because you didn't mention one, but it would probably help.
