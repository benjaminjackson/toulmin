The main thing these 97 households hold against you is that they paid full price for six half boxes and heard almost nothing about it. So the email apologises for that first and asks them back only after it.

Subject: We let you down last summer

On July 9, hail destroyed about half our crops. For the next six weeks your box held about half of what it should have, and you paid full price for it. We didn't offer any credit, and all you got from us was one short email. That wasn't good enough, and I'm sorry.

Here's what we've changed for this season:

- You can now pause your share for up to two weeks and get credit for those weeks.

[Your name]

It's about 160 words. A few things to think about before you send it:

- **Credit for crop losses.** The question a lapsed member is most likely to ask is "what happens if it hails again?" The email doesn't answer that. The pause credit only covers weeks they choose to skip, not short boxes. If you'd give a partial credit after a big loss, saying so would be your strongest point. I didn't include it because it isn't your policy yet.
- **Better communication.** You could add something like "If something goes wrong this season, you'll hear about it from me in detail." Only add it if you mean it.
- **What the nets don't cover.** I kept that claim narrow on purpose. The nets only protect the tomatoes and peppers, so the email doesn't say other crops are safe.
