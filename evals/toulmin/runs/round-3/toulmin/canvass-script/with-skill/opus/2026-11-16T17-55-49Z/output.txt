I've written the card for Ridgeview. I'd leave out the coffee line, though, and the card doesn't use it.

**Why the coffee line doesn't work, especially in Ridgeview:**
- **Citywide:** 19¢ × 410 = **$77.90 a year, about $6.50 a month**. That's roughly the price of a latte, so "less than a cup of coffee" is a toss-up at best.
- **Ridgeview:** 19¢ × 640 = **$121.60 a year, about $10 a month**. That's clearly more than a cup of coffee, and these are the first doors you knock.
- **"Average household" doesn't fit either.** $410,000 is the median, not the average. And 38% of households rent, so they don't get the tax bill at all.
- **It plays into the opposition's argument.** The neighborhood association president is already telling these voters they can't trust the campaign. A retiree who checks the number against their own tax statement and finds it's wrong will believe the "blank check" letter. If they hear the real number from your volunteer first, the volunteer gains their trust.

If you want the cost to sound small and still be accurate, "about 33 cents a day" is true for a typical Ridgeview home. Citywide it's about 21 cents a day.

**Choices I made on the card:**
- **The branch closing is the main point.** In Ridgeview the levy keeps their own library open, and without it the branch closes in July. That matters more to them than any figure.
- **"Blank check" is answered with your facts:** a fixed amount ($2.3 million a year), two named uses, and a six-year end date.
- **The 48–52 result from the last levy is left off the card.** Use it in volunteer training ("this is winnable, every door counts"), not at the door.
- **The association president isn't named.** Volunteers should answer the argument, not the person.

**Check these before printing:**
1. **Is the ballot language legally binding on how the money is spent?** If it is, volunteers can say "the measure requires it," which answers "blank check" more strongly. If it isn't, keep the wording on the card as it is.
2. **Does your state or county have a senior property-tax exemption or deferral program?** If so, it's the best answer for retirees on fixed incomes. Add one line about it, but only once you've confirmed it exists.
3. **Other precincts need different cost lines.** Elsewhere use "$78 a year, about $6.50 a month for a typical home." For renters, skip the tax math and talk about the branches and Sunday hours.

The spoken part is about 190 words, which takes roughly 75 seconds at a natural pace.
