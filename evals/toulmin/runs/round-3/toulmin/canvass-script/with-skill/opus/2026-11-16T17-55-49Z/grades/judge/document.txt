**YES ON MEASURE 4: RIDGEVIEW DOOR SCRIPT**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm [first name]. I'm a neighbor volunteering for Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**ASK, THEN LISTEN**
"Do you or anyone in your household use the Ridgeview library?"
*(Let them talk. Use what they tell you.)*

**THE CASE**
"Here's what's at stake: if Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. Measure 4 keeps it open and brings back Sunday hours at all five branches. People are using the library more than ever. Visits are up 22% since 2021."

**THE COST: GIVE THE REAL NUMBER**
"I want to be straight with you about the cost. It's 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value. For a typical Ridgeview home, around $640,000, that's about $122 a year, roughly $10 a month. And it ends after six years."

**IF THEY RAISE "BLANK CHECK"**
"I understand the worry. But it's a fixed amount, $2.3 million a year, for two things: keeping Ridgeview open and restoring Sunday hours. It's a set rate, and it ends in six years."

**THE ASK**
"Can we count on your yes vote on Measure 4 in March?"

- **YES:** "Thank you! Can we send you a reminder when ballots go out?" *(Record contact info.)*
- **UNSURE:** "What would help you decide?" *(Answer only from this card. Leave the flyer.)*
- **NO:** "Thanks for your time. Have a good day." *(Don't argue. Move on.)*

*Don't know an answer? Say "Good question, I'll find out," and write it down.*
