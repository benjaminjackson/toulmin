**The math on the coffee line.** At 19 cents per $1,000:
- **The city's median home ($410,000):** $77.90 a year, or about $6.50 a month. That's about what a café latte costs, so "less than a cup of coffee" is borderline even here.
- **Ridgeview's median home ($640,000):** $121.60 a year, or about $10 a month. That's clearly more than a cup of coffee.
- **Renters (38% of households):** they don't get a levy bill directly, so "the average household" doesn't match the home-value figure the cost is based on.

The first doors are retired homeowners on fixed incomes. They know their assessments, and the neighborhood association president has a letter in the paper this week calling the levy "a blank check." If a volunteer gives a low number and the voter works out $10, that proves the opponent's point. Giving the real number first is what makes the rest of the pitch believable. The strongest thing you have in Ridgeview isn't the price anyway. It's that their own branch closes in July if the levy fails.

**YES ON MEASURE 4: RIDGEVIEW DOOR CARD**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm [name], a volunteer with Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**THE CORE**
"If Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview branch on [street] closes this July. Measure 4 keeps it open, and it brings back Sunday hours at all five branches in the city.

On a typical Ridgeview home it costs about $10 a month, $122 a year. It's a set rate, and it ends after six years.

Library visits are up 22% since 2021. People here use this branch, and we'd like to keep it."

**ASK**
"Can we count on your yes vote in March?"

**IF THEY SAY…**

*"It's a blank check."*
"It's a set rate, 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value, and it ends after six years. It raises $2.3 million a year for two things: keeping this branch open and bringing back Sunday hours."

*"I'm on a fixed income."*
"That's fair, and I won't tell you $10 a month is nothing. What it pays for is keeping our branch open. Without it, the branch closes in July."

*"Does anyone still use the library?"*
"More people than before. Visits are up 22% since 2021."

- **Yes:** "Thank you! Can I leave this with you?" Hand over the literature and mark the card.
