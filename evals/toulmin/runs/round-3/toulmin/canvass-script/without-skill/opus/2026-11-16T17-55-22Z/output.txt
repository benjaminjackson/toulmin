I wrote the card, but I left out the "less than a cup of coffee a month" line because the numbers don't support it:

- **Typical home in the city ($410,000):** 410 × $0.19 = **$77.90 a year, about $6.50 a month.** That's closer to two coffees than one.
- **Ridgeview ($640,000):** 640 × $0.19 = **$121.60 a year, about $10 a month.**
- **"Average household" is also inaccurate,** because 38% of households rent and don't pay the levy directly.

Ridgeview is the worst place for that line to be wrong. Many residents there are retirees who check their tax bills closely. An opponent is already calling this a "blank check" in the paper. The last levy lost by four points. If a volunteer says "less than a coffee" and the voter works out $10 a month, the voter stops trusting the rest of the pitch. A daily figure is accurate and still sounds small: **about 21 cents a day citywide and about 33 cents a day in Ridgeview.** The card uses those numbers.

**Things to check before printing:**
- **Rate:** I assumed 19 cents per $1,000 is a yearly rate. If it isn't, the cost figures change.
- **Closing date:** The card says the branch closes "in July." I read that as July 2027, after the March vote. Confirm the date.
- **Senior tax relief:** Find out whether your county or state has a property-tax exemption or deferral for seniors. If it does, add it to the fixed-income answer. In Ridgeview it would be your strongest point. I didn't add one because I don't know if it exists.
- **Other precincts:** Outside Ridgeview, change the cost line to "On a typical $410,000 home, that's about $6.50 a month, around 21 cents a day."
