**YES ON MEASURE 4 — RIDGEVIEW DOOR SCRIPT** (60–90 sec)

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm [name], a volunteer with Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. I'm a neighbor and I'll be quick. Do you have a minute?"

**THE STAKES**
"Without Measure 4, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. The levy keeps it open and brings back Sunday hours at all five branches in the city. Library visits are up 22% since 2021, so people are using them more than ever."

**THE COST (say it plainly)**
"It's 19 cents per thousand dollars of assessed value. For a typical Ridgeview home, that's about $122 a year, roughly $10 a month or 33 cents a day. It lasts six years, then ends unless voters renew it."

**ASK**
"Can we count on your yes vote in March?"
→ YES: "Thank you! Can I put you down for a reminder before the election?"
→ UNSURE: "What would help you decide?" (Listen. Use the answers below.)
→ NO: "Thanks for hearing me out. Have a good day." (Mark the card and move on. Don't argue.)

---

**IF THEY SAY…**

**"It's a blank check."**
"I understand that worry. The rate is fixed at 19 cents, it ends after six years, and the money is for specific things: keeping this branch open and restoring Sunday hours."

**"I'm on a fixed income."**
"That's a real concern, and I won't pretend $10 a month is nothing. It's what it costs to keep a branch in walking distance for this neighborhood."

**"How much does it raise?"**
"$2.3 million a year, citywide."

**"Didn't this fail last time?"**
"It did, 48 to 52. It was that close. That's why every conversation matters."

**Don't know an answer?** "Good question. I'll find out and get back to you." Write it down.
