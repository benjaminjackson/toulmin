**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** November [__], 2026
**Re:** Ending display advertising and launching reader membership on January 1, 2027

**Request**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising and launching a reader membership program on January 1, 2027. Our goal is for membership to replace our ad revenue within 12 months.

**Why now**
Display ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 in two years, a 36% drop. If that trend continues, ads will bring in roughly $60,000–$67,000 next year. We are spending staff time and page space on a source of money that keeps shrinking.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week spring pilot, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. We promoted it only twice in the newsletter. That's 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers and about $20,000 a year at current size. A newsroom of similar size in another state reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget.

**What it will take**
- Replacing last year's $84,000 takes about 875 paying members, roughly 6% of our newsletter list. Matching projected ad revenue (~$65,000) takes about 680.
- We'll plan for about 1,000 sign-ups over the year to account for card fees and cancellations.
- To get there: a January launch campaign, a regular membership ask in every newsletter and on our site, and a year-end push.

**Risks and how we'll manage them**
- **Retention:** [X] of 212 pilot members are still paying. We'll track monthly cancellations from day one.
- **Cash gap:** Ad income stops in January, but membership will grow gradually. We'll cover the gap with [reserves / bridge funding / other].
- **Checkpoints:** I'll report member numbers to the board every quarter. If we have fewer than 400 members by June 30, I'll bring options back to the board, including limited sponsorships.

**Staffing**
Our part-time ad sales lead has been with us for 11 years and knows our community's businesses better than anyone. I propose [offering a transition into a membership and sponsorship role / other plan], effective [date].

**Motion requested**
That the board approve ending display advertising effective January 1, 2027, and launching a reader membership program, with quarterly reports to the board and a formal review at six months.
