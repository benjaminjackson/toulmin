I didn't include the guarantee. As far as I can tell, this email can't fund every child on the waiting list, and the claim would likely be picked apart by readers who are already wary of you.

- **The cost:** 61 children × $1,150 = **$70,150**.
- **Best case:** if all 340 lapsed donors gave their average $85, that's $28,900. With the match, it's $57,800. That's still $12,000 short.
- **Likely case:** last year this group gave $1,240. Even at three times that, with the match, you'd raise under $8,000. That funds about six or seven children.

These are the people who left angry over Eastside. If you promise "every child" and the waiting list is still there in February, the former board member gets a ready-made "they're doing it again" note to forward. So the draft below sticks to promises you can keep: the match is real, $575 from them pays for a full year for one child, and every gift helps.

Before sending, please check:

2. **January places.** The draft says funded children "come off the waiting list." Confirm that a funded child really can start in January.
