I left out the line saying their gift guarantees every child on the waiting list a place in January. The numbers don't support it:

- **Cost to clear the list:** 61 × $1,150 = $70,150.
- **Best case from this list:** If all 340 lapsed donors gave their average $85 and the full $15,000 match came in, you'd raise about $43,900. That still leaves about $26,000 to go.
- **Realistic case:** Last year this group gave $1,240. Even if the match triples the response, you'd likely raise somewhere in the low thousands.

People who give and then find out "every child" wasn't placed tend to stop giving again. Your former board member may also forward this email with comments. So the draft makes a promise you can keep: each $575, once matched, pays for one child's full year of lessons.
