# Steady Plate

## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian

Knowing what to eat with type 2 diabetes is one job. Cooking it every day is another. Steady Plate does both for you.

I'm Lena Ortiz, RD. I plan every menu myself, and every meal on it has 45 grams of carbohydrate or less. You don't have to count, weigh or guess. Every Sunday, 10 cooked lunches and dinners arrive at your door.

### What I've seen, and what I can't promise

I won't promise that your A1C will go down. No honest meal service can.

Here's what I can tell you. 14 customers shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months. Ten went down, three stayed about the same, and one went up. That's a small group. I also don't track whether anyone changed their medication during that time, so I can't give the meals all the credit. What I do know is that every meal those customers ate from us had 45 grams of carbs or less.

We have 88 active customers. 37 of them have been with us for more than six months.

### "Won't I get tired of the same food?"

The menu runs on a four-week cycle. Each week, you can swap any meal for one of 6 other choices. That way you're eating to a plan without eating the same thing every week.

### What it costs

- **Weekly plan:** 10 meals for $129, about $12.90 a meal
- **Trial week:** $59
- **No contract:** skip a week or cancel any time
- **Order deadline:** Thursday at noon for that Sunday's delivery
- **Delivery area:** within 25 miles of downtown

### Talk to me before you start

Anyone can book a 15-minute phone call with me before their first order. Tell me how you eat now, what you've tried before, and anything I should know about your diet, including allergies. You'll get your answers from the dietitian who plans the meals.

Steady Plate doesn't replace your doctor's advice. Keep working with your doctor on your medication and care. We take one daily job off your list: deciding what's for lunch and dinner, and cooking it.

### Start with a trial week

Order by Thursday at noon and your first meals arrive on Sunday. If it isn't for you, there's nothing to cancel beyond that week. If it is, the full plan has no contract.
