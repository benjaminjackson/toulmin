**Dinner without the math**

Living with type 2 diabetes means thinking about food all day: counting carbs, reading labels, wondering whether tonight's dinner will push your numbers up. Steady Plate takes ten of those decisions off your plate each week.

I'm Lena Ortiz, a registered dietitian, and I plan every menu myself. Every lunch and dinner is cooked, portioned and kept to 45 grams of carbohydrate or less, so you know what you're eating before you open the container.

**What some of our customers have seen**

Fourteen customers shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months. In 10 of them, A1C went down, by an average of 0.6 points. Three stayed about the same, and one went up.

That's a small group, and I didn't track whether anyone's medication changed over those months, so I can't promise your A1C will drop. What I can tell you is that 88 people currently get their meals from us, and 37 of them have been with us for more than six months.

Steady Plate doesn't replace your doctor's advice or your treatment plan. It's meant to make following that plan easier.

**How it works**

- **Start with a call.** Before your first delivery, you and I talk for 15 minutes about what you eat now, what you don't like, and what you're hoping for.
- **Choose your meals.** Each week you can swap any meal for one of six other choices.
- **Order by Thursday at noon** and your meals arrive that Sunday.
- **The menu rotates every four weeks**, so you'll see dishes come back around, and if a dish doesn't suit you, you can swap it out.

We deliver within 25 miles of downtown.
