**Subject: HiveSense half-year update and bridge round**

Dear investors,

This update covers our customers, revenue, competition and cash, and ends with what we're asking of you.

**Customers**

We have 31 paying commercial beekeepers, up from 12 a year ago.

Our largest customer runs 4,000 hives. Last winter he lost 18% of his colonies, compared with 31% the winter before. He says the sensors were part of that. He also changed his mite treatment over the same period, so we can't yet say how much of the improvement came from us.

**Revenue and margins**

Revenue for the half was $212,000. 70% of it came in March and April, when beekeepers get their colonies ready for pollination season, so most of our sales come in the spring.

Each sensor costs us $38 to make and sells for $55, leaving $17 per sensor. On top of that, each hive pays $4 a month for the app, so we have some income for the rest of the year.

**Competition**

In May, a large farm equipment company launched a hive sensor at $45, $10 below ours. Their sensor measures only temperature. Ours measures temperature, sound and hive weight, which together give a fuller picture of how strong a colony is. As far as we know, no customer has left us. Three have asked to test the competitor's sensor, and we'll tell you what they decide.

**Cash**

We spend about $95,000 a month and have $340,000 in the bank. Not counting new revenue, that lasts into March, just before the season when most of our sales come in.

We're cutting spending to $75,000 a month by February by leaving two open roles unfilled.

We are not forecasting a profit in 2027. Over the last half, revenue averaged about $35,000 a month. To break even at the lower spending level, we'd need roughly twice that.

**The bridge round**

We're raising $600,000 on a convertible note with a 20% discount and a $6 million cap. One angel investor has committed $250,000, so $350,000 is still open. The round closes on December 15.

With the bridge and the lower spending, our cash lasts until about the end of 2027, before counting any revenue. That takes us through next year's pollination season, which is where most of our annual sales are won or lost.

Everyone who invests will get a monthly cash report from me.

If you'd like to take part, please reply to me by December 1.

Thank you for your continued support.

HiveSense

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
