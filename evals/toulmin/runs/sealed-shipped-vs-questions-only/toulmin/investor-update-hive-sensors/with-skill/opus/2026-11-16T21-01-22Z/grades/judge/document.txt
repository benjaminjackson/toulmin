Hi all,

This is where we stand after the last six months, and what we're asking of you.

**The short version**

We're raising a $600,000 bridge round on a convertible note with a 20% discount and a $6M cap. One angel has already committed $250,000, and we'd like the remaining $350,000 to come from you. If you want in, please reply to me by December 1. The round closes December 15.

**What's working**

- 31 commercial beekeepers now pay us, up from 12 a year ago.
- We make money on every sensor. Each one costs $38 to make and sells for $55. Each hive also pays $4 a month for the app.
- Revenue for the last six months was $212,000.

**What's hard**

- We spend about $95,000 a month, well more than we bring in, and we have $340,000 in the bank.

**What we're doing about it**

We will bring spending down to $75,000 a month by February by not filling two open roles.

I'm not going to promise you profitability next year. I don't yet have numbers solid enough to stand behind that. After the 2027 spring season, I'll send you a report on how many hives we're covering, what we sold and what that means for when we break even.

**Why the bridge, and why now**

Without it, our cash runs out around March, even after the spending cut and before counting any revenue. March is when the season that brings in most of our sales begins.

With the bridge we'd have about $940,000. At the lower spending level, that carries us through the 2027 season and into late 2027 before counting any revenue. That gives us a full selling season to show that beekeepers choose us over a cheaper rival, and to give you real numbers on the path to break-even.

You've backed us from 12 beekeepers to 31. I'd like you with us for the next season. If you have questions before you decide, reply or call me.
