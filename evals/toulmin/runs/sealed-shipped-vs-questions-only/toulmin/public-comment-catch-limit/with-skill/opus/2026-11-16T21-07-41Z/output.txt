**What I did with the "out of business" line:** The strongest true version is the two captains who told you they'd sell. It's specific, it's in their words, and nobody can call it false. The comment sets it beside your 2016 numbers and the three-year length of the cut, so the commission can see the risk for themselves. It never says outright that every boat will close.

**Things to check before the meeting:**
- **Start with your name.** The comment doesn't include it because I don't know it. Most commissions ask speakers to say their name and where they're from at the start.
- **"If a 3-fish limit or a shorter season can get the stock where it needs to be":** I worded it this way because neither of us knows whether those options cut the catch enough. A commissioner may ask whether they do. If you can, ask commission staff beforehand whether they've looked at 3 fish or a shorter season. A yes would let you make the ask more firmly.
- **Survey:** I attributed it to your association and gave it once. You don't know what the other 17 customers said, so it doesn't appear as a percentage.
- **The $27,000 and $80,000 figures** assume a drop the size of 2016's (about 19%) applied to your current 160 trips. If you're asked, that's where they come from.
