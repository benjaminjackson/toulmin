**Bring the Majestic back**

The Majestic on Main Street has been dark since 2022. When it closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. Since then, the nearest cinema has been 38 miles away, a 76-mile round trip for a night at the movies.

We can reopen it. We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and to fix the roof. If we raise that, the building's owner will rent the Majestic to us for $1 a year for ten years.

$36,000 of it is already pledged. A local bank has promised $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30, and three local businesses have pledged $11,000 in writing. That leaves $49,000 to come from the rest of us.

Many of you will remember that we've tried this before. Three years ago we set out to raise $120,000 and reached $47,000. That wasn't enough, so we returned every dollar. This time the goal is $35,000 lower, and the bank and three businesses are already committed. The $49,000 still needed is only $2,000 more than this town gave last time. Split across our 6,500 residents, it comes to about $7.50 each.

The same promise holds. It's all or nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge will be returned in full.

We're also planning beyond opening night. Forty-six volunteers have already signed up to run the box office and the snack bar, and the rent will be $1 a year.

If we fall short, the bank's pledge and the $1 lease both go, the screen stays dark, and the closest movie stays 38 miles away.

Please pledge today, and ask a neighbour to do the same.

Ruth Albers
Chair, the volunteer group to reopen the Majestic

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
