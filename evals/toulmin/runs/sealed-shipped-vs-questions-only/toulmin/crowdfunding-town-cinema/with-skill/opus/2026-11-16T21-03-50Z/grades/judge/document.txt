**Bring the Majestic Back**

The Majestic on Main Street has been dark since 2022. When it closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. Now the nearest cinema is 38 miles away.

We can reopen it by Thanksgiving. We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and fix the roof. If we raise it by June 30, the building's owner will rent us the Majestic for $1 a year for ten years. Forty-six volunteers have already signed up to run the box office and the snack bar. With almost no rent and volunteers behind the counter, the Majestic will be far cheaper to run.

Much of the money is already pledged. A local bank will give $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30, and three local businesses have pledged $11,000 in writing. That leaves $49,000 to raise from the rest of us.

Some of you backed our last campaign. Three years ago we aimed for $120,000, reached $47,000, and refunded every dollar. This time the goal is lower, and the bank and local businesses are already on board. What's left to raise is $49,000, just $2,000 more than our last campaign raised.

This campaign is all-or-nothing again. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge is returned in full, and the Majestic stays dark.

There are two things you should know. The roof estimate dates from last fall, and the contractor says it could rise by up to 15%. Also, we can't promise new films in their opening week, because that depends on the distributors. What we can promise is that you'll see where every dollar goes. We'll post a public spending report at the town library every three months.

The campaign runs from May 1 to June 30. Pledge what you can, and tell your neighbors. Let's turn the lights back on at the Majestic.

Ruth Albers, Chair
