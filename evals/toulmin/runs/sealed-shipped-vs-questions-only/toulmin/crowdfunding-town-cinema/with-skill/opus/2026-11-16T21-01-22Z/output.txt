**Bring Back the Majestic**

The Majestic on Main Street has been dark since 2022. When it closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. Since then, the nearest cinema has been 38 miles away.

We can reopen it. We need $85,000 by June 30 to replace the projector and sound system and fix the roof. If we raise it, the building's owner will rent us the Majestic for $1 a year for ten years.

We aren't starting from zero. A local bank has pledged $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30, and three local businesses have pledged $11,000 in writing. That leaves $49,000: about $7.50 for every person in town.

Some of you remember our last campaign. Three years ago we set out to raise $120,000, reached $47,000, and refunded every pledge. This time the goal is smaller, more than 40% is already pledged, and what's left is just $2,000 more than we raised last time.

The promise is the same: all or nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge is returned in full.

If we do reach it, 46 volunteers are already signed up to run the box office and snack bar, and we plan to reopen by Thanksgiving. Every three months we'll post a public report at the town library showing how the money was spent.

Two things you should know. Our roof estimate is from last fall, and the contractor says it could rise by up to 15%. And we can't promise new films in their opening week, because that's up to the distributors.

What we can promise is a screen on Main Street again, and a night at the movies without a 76-mile round trip.

Pledge by June 30. Let's turn the lights back on.

Ruth Albers
Chair, volunteer group to reopen the Majestic
</final_document>

**Things to check before you publish:**

- **The $49,000 figure:** It assumes the bank counts the businesses' $11,000 toward "the rest." If the bank only counts money from the public, the gap is $60,000 and the comparison with last time gets weaker. Please confirm with the bank.
- **The roof cost:** The pitch says the price could rise, but not what you'll do if it does. That's because you didn't tell me and I didn't want to make up a promise. If you have a plan, such as a contingency fund or a stretch goal above $85,000, add one sentence after the roof line. Readers will ask.
- **Running costs:** You didn't have ticket prices, running costs or the reason it closed, so I left out any claim that the cinema will break even. Once you know those, they're your best answer to "will it last this time?"
- **Rewards and names:** When you decide on reward tiers, put them just before the final call to pledge. If the group or the bank has a name it wants used, swap it in.
