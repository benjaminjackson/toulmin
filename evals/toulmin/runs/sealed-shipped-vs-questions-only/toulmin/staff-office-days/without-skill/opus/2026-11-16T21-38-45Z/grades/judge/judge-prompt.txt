**Subject: Working in the office Tuesday to Thursday from September 1**

Everyone,

I'm writing before Friday's all-staff meeting (10 a.m., main studio) so you have time to read this before we talk it through together.

From September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. That includes the partners. Until now we've been coming in one day a week. From September we'll be here on all three days with you.

This is a real change, and it affects some of you more than others. Fifteen of you joined us as fully remote employees in 2021 and 2022, and you took the job on that basis. We are changing that, and I won't pretend we aren't.

**Why we're doing this**

Last year we lost two large hospital projects at the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. We had good design and good people, but to the client we didn't look like a team. That cost us two major projects, and it will cost us more if we don't fix it.

Our review records also show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point of leading a project. That is not a judgement on them. Much of what makes someone ready to lead is learned by working next to people who already do it. Five of our six project leads already come in three days a week. We want everyone to be in the room with them.

**What this means for you**

- **From September 1:** in the office every Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.
- **Colleagues who live more than two hours away:** the four of you can keep working remotely until December 31, 2027. From January 2028 you'll come in one day a week.
- **Commuting support:** if you live more than 30 miles from the office, the firm will pay up to $150 a month toward your commute. This applies to everyone, not only those hired as remote.
- **Desks:** we don't have a desk for everyone yet. New desks arrive in August, before the start date.
- **Exceptions:** to be straight with you, we can't offer exceptions beyond the four colleagues above.
- **Review:** we will review how this is working with you six months in, in March 2028.

**Questions**

If you have questions before Friday, send them to me or to HR. I'd rather hear them now than have you sit on them. I'll answer as many as I can at the meeting.

Paul Whitaker
Partner
