Subject: Working in the office three days a week from September 1

Hi everyone,

Before Friday's all-staff meeting I want you to have this in writing, so you have time to think it over before we talk.

From September 1, 2027, everyone will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. This ends fully remote work at the firm. The partners have decided on the policy and the three days. I want to explain why.

Last year we lost two large hospital projects in the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. Clients are hiring a team, and they could see that we weren't working like one.

Our review records show a second problem. Junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. Much of what makes someone ready to lead is learned by sitting near people who already do it. You hear how they handle a difficult client call and you can turn and ask a question. Five of our six project leads already come in three days a week on their own. We think working apart is a large part of the delay, and we want everyone earlier in their career to have those leads nearby.

The partners haven't set that example. We've been coming in one day a week. From September we'll be in all three days.

For the 15 of you hired as fully remote in 2021 and 2022, this changes the terms you joined on. I know that's a big change to ask of you. The four of you who live more than two hours from the office can keep working remotely until December 31, 2027, and will come in one day a week from January 2028. There will be no other individual exceptions.

To help with travel, anyone who lives more than 30 miles from the office will get up to $150 a month toward commuting. This applies to everyone, not only the people hired as remote.

We don't yet have a desk for everyone. New desks arrive in August, before the change starts.

We'll review the policy with you after it has been running for six months.

On Friday I'll go through all of this and answer your questions. If you'd like to raise something before then, come to me or to HR.

Paul Whitaker
