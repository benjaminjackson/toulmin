Subject: Working in the office from September 1, 2027

Everyone,

From September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. That includes the three partners. We have been coming in one day a week, and we will be in all three days with you.

I know this is a real change, especially for the 15 of you who joined in 2021 and 2022 as fully remote. You took those jobs on terms that suited your lives, and we are changing those terms. That is not a small thing, and I won't pretend it is. Here is why we have decided to do it.

Last year we lost two large hospital projects at the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. Our work was good enough to reach the final round. What cost us the projects was how we came across as a team. A client handing us a hospital wants to see a team that already works well together, and they could see we didn't.

The second reason is how our junior staff are developing. Our review records show that juniors hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. Much of what gets someone ready to lead is picked up by working near people who already lead: hearing how a hard client call is handled, watching a drawing set get marked up, asking a quick question instead of waiting for a meeting. That is hard to learn through a screen, and our newer colleagues are paying for it in their careers.

What this means in practice:

- From September 1, 2027, everyone works in the office Tuesday to Thursday.
- The four colleagues who live more than two hours from the office will keep working remotely until the end of 2027. From January 2028, they will come in one day a week.
- There will be no other exceptions.
- Anyone who lives more than 30 miles from the office can claim up to $150 a month toward commuting costs.
- We don't have a desk for everyone yet. New desks arrive in August, before the change starts.

We are telling you now, more than nine months ahead, so that anyone who needs to change childcare, travel or other arrangements has time to do it.

I will go through all of this at the all-staff meeting on Friday at 10 a.m. in the main studio. If you would like to raise something before then, send it to me or to HR.

Paul Whitaker
</document>

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.
