- **The March error:** The letter says plainly what happened and then gives the 600 error-free entries since the check went in. It doesn't promise you'll never make another mistake. Instead it says you build systems that catch mistakes before they reach a court.
- **The paralegal who left:** David will probably know about this already, so the letter says it plainly and doesn't guess why they left. Your weekly check-in comes right after it. That shows how you'd supervise without claiming the check-ins would have changed anything.
- **The meeting:** You're asking to meet before the December 15 deadline, which leaves time to talk before requests are due.
- **What would make it stronger:** If a lawyer you support would vouch for you, or you've been checking the newer paralegals' work beyond training, one sentence on either would strengthen the supervision case.

Subject: Request for promotion to Senior Paralegal

Dear David,

I'm writing to ask to be considered for promotion to Senior Paralegal in the January review cycle. I'm requesting the title and the $9,000 salary increase that comes with it.

I have been a paralegal in the litigation group for five years. Last year I supported four lawyers across 23 cases, two of which went to trial, and billed 1,640 hours against the group's target of 1,550.

The senior role means supervising the other paralegals, and I already do some of that work. I trained all three paralegals the group has hired in the last two years. Two are still with us, and one left after eight months.

In 2023 I built the group's court-deadline tracking sheet. Two other groups in the firm have since adopted it. Last March I entered a filing deadline wrong in that sheet. Another paralegal caught the error, and we filed two hours before the court's cutoff. That was too close. In April I added a second-person check to every entry. Since then the sheet has recorded about 600 deadlines, and none has been wrong or missed. I can't promise I'll never make a mistake. I can promise to build systems that catch mistakes, mine or anyone else's, before they reach a court. That is the work I would bring to the senior role.

If promoted, I commit to two things:

- Running a short weekly check-in with the paralegals I supervise, so that questions and problems come up early.
- Completing the state paralegal certification by June 2027.

I would like to discuss this with you in person before requests are due on December 15. Please let me know a time that works for you.

Thank you for considering my request.

Best regards,
Jenna Morales
