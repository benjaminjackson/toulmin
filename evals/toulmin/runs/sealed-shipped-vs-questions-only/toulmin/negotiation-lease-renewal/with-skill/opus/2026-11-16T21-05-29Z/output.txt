The email is about 270 words. A few things to check before you send it:

- **Year five:** The $5,740 figure is your $5,100 with 3% added each year ($5,253, $5,411, $5,573, $5,740). From year four on, you'd be paying more than his $5,650. If his number turns out to be a flat rate for all five years, you can point that out when you talk.
- **Signature:** I signed it "Pho Lan" because you didn't give your own name. Add it above the restaurant name if you'd like.
- **The empty unit:** The email only states its listing price and how long it's been empty. It doesn't claim the unit is a fair comparison, since you don't know its size or condition.
