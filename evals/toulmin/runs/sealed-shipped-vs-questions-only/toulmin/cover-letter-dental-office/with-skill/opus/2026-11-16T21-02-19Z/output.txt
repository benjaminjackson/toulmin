Dear Dr. Pierce,

I'm applying for the office manager position at Brookside Family Dental. For eleven years I was the office manager at a veterinary clinic with four vets and 14 staff, a practice a little larger than yours.

I handled the clinic's scheduling and billing. In my last three years there, invoices unpaid for more than 90 days fell from about $38,000 to $9,000. In 2014 I also managed the clinic's move from paper records to AVImark practice software.

Your posting lists Dentrix, which I haven't used yet. I've signed up for the 20-hour Dentrix online training course and will finish it before my first day. I've already moved one practice from paper to software, and I'm confident I can learn a new system quickly.

I left the clinic, giving two weeks' notice, when my mother was diagnosed with a serious illness. I cared for her for the next seven years, until she died last spring. During that time I managed her insurance claims and her appointments with six specialists. For the past four years I've also been the volunteer treasurer at my church, keeping the books in QuickBooks.

Dr. Sue Kim, the clinic's head veterinarian, will give me a reference. I can start within two weeks of an offer and can work your Tuesday and Thursday evening hours.

I'd welcome the chance to talk with you about the role.

Sincerely,
Linda Marsh

The letter is about 240 words. Some notes:

- **Supervising staff:** I didn't say you managed the 14 staff. The letter only says you were office manager at a clinic of that size, which is accurate. If they ask in an interview, you can explain what that involved in your own words.
- **Things to check:** I described your mother's diagnosis as "a serious illness." If you'd rather say less, just put "when my mother became ill." If a two-week start date would leave you short of time for the 20-hour Dentrix course, change that line to say you'll finish the course by your start date.
- **Sending it:** The posting closes on the 21st, so send it a day or two early. A subject line like "Office Manager application – Linda Marsh" works well.
