This comes to about 240 words. Some notes on what I decided:

- **"A staff attorney position," not "a third attorney."** You don't have a plan to replace the attorney leaving in June. Until you do, this hire may keep you at two lawyers instead of three. The summary mentions the June departure and doesn't promise a third attorney, so it stays true either way. If you secure money to replace her before you submit, change the request line to "a third staff attorney."
- **I included the county renewal.** It's one sentence, and the foundation will probably see it in your budget anyway. It's better coming from you. Cut it if the application has a separate budget section where it fits better.
- **What I left out because you don't know it yet:** how many more tenants the new attorney will serve, a start date, and a comparison for the 58%. The six-month reporting and the January data request are there to make up for those gaps with commitments you can actually keep.
- **Placeholders to fill in:** clinic name, county, and the family fund's name. Check whether the fund is OK with being named.
- **Check the budget numbers.** $45,000 + $20,000 + $13,000 = $78,000 a year, or $156,000 over two years.
