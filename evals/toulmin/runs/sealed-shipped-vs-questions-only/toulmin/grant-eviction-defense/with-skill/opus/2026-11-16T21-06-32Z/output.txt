A few things to settle before you submit:

- **The June departure is the biggest risk to this summary.** It says "third attorney," but it also says (as it should) that one attorney is leaving. A reviewer will immediately ask whether you'll be back to two. If you plan to refill that seat, add one sentence saying so. If you won't, the request becomes "keep us at two attorneys," and the summary needs rewriting.
- **Check which year the county renewal is.** It's November 2026 now, so "this October" probably means October 2027. Put the year in. Also find out whether the county money pays for the current attorneys. If your $13,000 share comes from a budget that depends on that contract, a reviewer may connect the two.
- **I presented the 58% as your record, not as proof that lawyers change outcomes.** Without a comparison figure, it can't support a stronger claim. Promising the clerk data shows the reviewer how you'll fill that gap.
- **The new tenant numbers are estimates.** About 80 tenants a year assumes the new attorney matches your current per-attorney pace from day one. A first-year attorney may handle fewer cases, so lower the number if you think that's likely.
- **Add your county's name** in the first sentence. You didn't give it, so I left it out.
