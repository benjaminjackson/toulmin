- **Turnover:** The email admits last year's turnover before giving the July numbers. These customers lived through it. Owning it makes "only 1 of 18 since July" believable instead of sounding like marketing.
- **Price:** Your $165 and the apps' $140 each appear once, next to what the extra $25 pays for. Nothing suggests you'll match their price.
- **"You'll always know who's coming":** This is the strongest true line about cleaners, and it holds even on backup days. Cut it if your backup assignments sometimes change.
- **Sending:** Before it goes out, check whether $165 is the right price for these customers. The email quotes the 3-bedroom rate, so customers with other home sizes may need a different version. Also, if your mailing tool can fill in each customer's cleaner and backup by name, use that. It's the most convincing thing you could add.
