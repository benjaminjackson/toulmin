A few things to check before you send it:

- **Price after the $99 visit:** the email doesn't say what they'll pay after the first cleaning. Customers will ask. If it goes back to $165, adding "then $165 after that" makes the offer clearer.
- **"Runs until December 31":** I kept your wording. Decide whether that means they book by then or get cleaned by then, because people will ask.
- **Advance notice or a remedy:** if you later commit to telling customers ahead of time when the backup is coming, add one sentence about it. That's the strongest thing you could add to the cleaner promise.
- **Greeting:** if your email tool can fill in first names, swap "Hello" for the customer's name.
