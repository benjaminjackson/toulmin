I've drafted the memo, but I didn't write it to say membership *will* replace ad revenue within a year. Your numbers support something close to that, but not that exact claim. Two members of your board come from advertising, and they're the ones most likely to spot the gap.

**What your numbers support**
- Ads are falling about 20% a year ($131k to $84k over two years). If that keeps up, keeping ads means about $67k next year and $54k the year after, not $84k. That's your strongest point.
- The 212 pilot members are worth $20,352 a year, about a quarter of last year's ad revenue.
- Matching $84k takes 875 paying members, which is 6.25% of your newsletter list. That means adding about 13 members a week for a year. The pilot brought in about 35 a week with two newsletter mentions.

**Where the bold version breaks**
- Members join gradually, so even if you reach 875 by December, 2027 membership income would be about $52k. Ads on their current trend would bring about $67k. Someone on the board will work this out, and a memo that says "will replace" won't survive it.
- The 35-a-week pace came from your most loyal readers. It will slow, and some members will cancel.
- The 40% figure from the other newsroom shows membership can work. It doesn't predict what you'll get.

**What I did instead**
The memo makes "replace ad revenue within a year" a target: 875 paying members by December 31, 2027. It adds a June 30 checkpoint of 500 members and says what you'll do if you miss it. That gives the board something to hold you to, which works better on a sceptical board than a flat promise. If you'd still rather say "will," change the first paragraph. Leave the year-one cash section as it is either way.

**Before you send it**
1. **Are the 212 pilot members still paying?** The $20,352 figure assumes they are. If some have cancelled, use the current number and redo the sums.
2. **Your ad salesperson.** I couldn't decide this for you, so it's a bracket in the memo. Please talk to them before the memo goes out. They shouldn't find out from a board document.
3. **Why not run ads and membership side by side?** The board will ask, and your notes don't give a reason. I wrote one ("splitting a small staff…"). Swap in your own if it's different.
4. **The year-one shortfall** (about $15k, less whatever ad sales cost you) needs a funding source. Fill in that bracket.
5. **Naming the peer newsroom** makes the 40% figure more convincing, if they don't mind.
