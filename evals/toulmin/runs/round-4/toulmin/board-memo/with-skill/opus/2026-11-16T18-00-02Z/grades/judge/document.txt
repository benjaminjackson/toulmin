**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Replacing display ads with reader membership from January 2027

**Recommendation**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising on January 1, 2027, and moving to a reader membership program. Our target is for membership to bring in what ads did, $84,000 a year, by the end of 2027. I'm proposing a mid-year checkpoint so the board can hold us to it.

**Why now**
Ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 in two years, roughly 20% a year. If that continues, ads will bring in about $67,000 next year and $54,000 the year after. Keeping ads does not keep $84,000. It keeps a number that is shrinking every year.

**What the pilot showed**
In six weeks this spring, with two newsletter mentions and no other promotion, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. That's about 35 a week, and 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers. Those members are worth $20,352 a year, a quarter of last year's ad revenue. [Peer newsroom], a newsroom about our size, now covers 40% of its budget through membership.

**What it will take**
Matching $84,000 takes 875 paying members, or 6.25% of our current newsletter list. That means adding about 660 members over twelve months, or 13 a week. The pilot brought in 35 a week with almost no effort.

**Risks and how we'll manage them**
- *The pace will slow.* Early sign-ups come from our most loyal readers. That's why the target is 13 a week, not 35.
- *Members cancel.* The 875 target counts paying members after cancellations. I'll report sign-ups and cancellations to the board monthly.
- *Year-one cash.* Members build up over the year, so membership will bring in less than $84,000 during 2027 even if we hit the target. Steady growth from 212 to 875 members brings in about $52,000 in 2027. Ads on their current trend would likely bring about $67,000. We'll cover the gap of roughly $15,000, less the cost of ad sales we no longer carry, from [source].
- *Why not run both?* Asking readers to pay to support us while we sell their attention to advertisers weakens the pitch. Running both would also split a small staff between two sales efforts. [Confirm or replace with your reason.]

**Checkpoint**
By June 30, 2027, we should have at least 500 paying members. If we don't, I'll come back to the board with options, including returning to ad and sponsorship sales.

1. Approve ending display advertising effective January 1, 2027.
2. Adopt a membership target of 875 paying members by December 31, 2027, with a checkpoint of 500 by June 30, 2027.
