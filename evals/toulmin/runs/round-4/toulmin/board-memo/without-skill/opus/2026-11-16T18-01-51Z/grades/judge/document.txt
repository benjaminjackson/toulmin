**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** November [X], 2026
**Re:** Approval to end display advertising and launch reader membership in January 2027

**Request**
I am asking the board to approve ending display advertising and launching a reader membership program in January 2027. Our goal is for membership revenue to fully replace ad revenue within 12 months.

**Why now**
Display ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 over two years, a 36% drop. If that trend continues, ads will bring in about $67,000 in 2027. Ads are a shrinking source of revenue that we don't control. They also slow our site and get in the way of what we most want readers to do, which is support us directly.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week pilot this spring, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. We promoted it only with two newsletter mentions and did no other marketing. That is about $20,000 a year in recurring revenue from 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers. A newsroom of similar size in another state reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget.

**The math**
Each member brings in $96 a year. Replacing $84,000 takes about 875 members, or 6.25% of our current newsletter list. That is roughly four times the pilot result, and we will be backing it with a year of steady promotion instead of two mentions. Matching projected 2027 ad revenue takes about 700 members.

**Targets (net of cancellations; reported to the board each quarter)**
- March 31: 400 members
- June 30: 550 members
- September 30: 700 members
- December 31: 875 members ($84,000 a year in membership revenue)

**Risks and how we'll manage them**
- **Transition gap.** Membership will grow over the year, but ad revenue stops in January. If we hit our targets, 2027 membership revenue will be about $52,000, about $32,000 below last year's ad revenue. I propose covering this from [reserves / bridge funding source].
- **Slower growth.** If we are below 550 members on June 30, I will bring the board options. These include underwriting and sponsorships, which don't require display ads.
- **Staff.** Our ad sales colleague has been with us for 11 years. [Describe the plan discussed with them, e.g., a transition into membership and sponsorship work.]

**Decision requested**
1. End display advertising effective [date].
2. Launch reader membership in January 2027, with the targets above.
3. Authorize up to $[amount] from [source] to cover the 2027 transition gap.
