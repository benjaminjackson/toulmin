I wrote the card, but I left out the "less than a cup of coffee a month" line. It doesn't hold up, and Ridgeview is the worst place to use it.

**The math:**
- **Median city home ($410,000):** 410 × $0.19 = $77.90 a year, about **$6.50 a month**. That's more than a regular coffee and close to a latte. It's also the *median*, not the "average household."
- **Ridgeview median ($640,000):** 640 × $0.19 = $121.60 a year, about **$10 a month**. That's two coffees or more.
- **Renters (38% of households):** They don't get a levy bill, so "costs your household" doesn't really apply to them either way.

In Ridgeview, voters are retired homeowners on fixed incomes who know their assessed values and can do this math at the door. The opposition is already calling the levy a "blank check." If a volunteer's number is off by half, it backs up that framing and hurts the volunteer's credibility on everything else they say. The last levy lost by 4 points, so it isn't worth the risk. Giving the real number up front works better. About $10 a month to keep their own branch from closing is a defensible ask.
