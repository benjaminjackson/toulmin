"Here's why I'm out today. If Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. *[Pause.]*

For a typical Ridgeview home it comes to about $10 a month."

**IF THEY SAY…**

**"It's a blank check."**
"I've heard that. It's actually tightly limited: a fixed rate of 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value, for six years, and then it expires. It raises $2.3 million a year and pays for exactly two things: Sunday hours at all five branches and keeping Ridgeview open."

**"I'm on a fixed income."**
"That's fair, and it's real money. On a typical Ridgeview home it's about $10 a month, or $122 a year. You can check yours: take your assessed value, divide by 1,000, and multiply by 19 cents. For that, the Ridgeview branch stays open."

**"How much exactly?"**
"19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value. A $640,000 home pays about $122 a year."
