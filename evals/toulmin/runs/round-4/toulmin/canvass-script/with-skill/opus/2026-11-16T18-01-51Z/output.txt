I'd drop the coffee line. It doesn't hold up with your own numbers, and Ridgeview is where it would hurt you most:

- **Citywide:** 19¢ per $1,000 on a $410,000 home is **$77.90 a year, about $6.50 a month**. That's roughly what a latte costs, not less than a cup of coffee.
- **Ridgeview:** a $640,000 home pays **$121.60 a year, about $10 a month**. That's two or three cups, and Ridgeview is the first place you're knocking.
- **"Average household" isn't accurate either.** 38% of households rent and get no levy bill, and $410,000 is the median home, not the average.

The neighborhood association president is already calling this "a blank check." If a volunteer says "less than a cup of coffee" to a retired homeowner who can work out $122 a year, that becomes the opposition's next letter. These people watch every dollar, and giving them the exact figure will earn more trust than a comparison they can disprove. **33 cents a day** is true and still sounds small. Citywide it's about 21 cents a day.
