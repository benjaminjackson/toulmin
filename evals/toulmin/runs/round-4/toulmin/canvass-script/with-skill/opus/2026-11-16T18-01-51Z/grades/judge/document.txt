**YES ON MEASURE 4 — RIDGEVIEW DOOR SCRIPT**

**Open**
"Hi, I'm [name], a volunteer with Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

"Do you or anyone in your household use the Ridgeview library?" *(Listen. Use their answer.)*

**Why it matters here**
"Here's why I'm knocking in Ridgeview first: if Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. Measure 4 keeps it open and brings back Sunday hours at all five branches. More people are using our libraries than before. Visits are up 22% since 2021."

**The cost, straight**
"I know folks here watch every dollar, so here's the exact cost. It's 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value. For a typical Ridgeview home, about $640,000, that comes to about $122 a year. That's roughly $10 a month, or 33 cents a day. It ends after six years."

**"Blank check"**
"You may have seen the letter in the paper calling this a blank check. It isn't one. It raises a set amount, $2.3 million a year, for six years, and it spells out what it pays for: keeping this branch open and bringing Sunday hours back. Then it expires unless voters choose to renew it."

**Ask**
"Can we count on your yes vote on Measure 4 in March?"

- **Yes:** "Thank you! Do you know how you'll vote, by mail or in person?" *(Mark YES.)*
- **Undecided:** "What's your biggest question about it?" *(Note it. Leave literature.)*
- **No:** "Thanks for your time. Have a good day." *(Mark NO. Don't argue.)*

*Say the real numbers. Never round them down.*
