**YES ON MEASURE 4: DOOR SCRIPT (Ridgeview)**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm [name], a volunteer from [neighborhood]. I'm talking with neighbors about Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Got a minute?"

**ASK**
"Do you or anyone in your household use the Ridgeview library?" *(Listen. Let them talk.)*

**WHAT'S AT STAKE**
"If Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview branch closes this July. Measure 4 keeps it open and brings back Sunday hours at all five branches. People are using the libraries more than ever: visits are up 22% since 2021."

**THE COST** *(homeowners only; renters, skip to Close)*
"It's 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value. On a typical Ridgeview home that's about $10 a month, or $2.34 a week. That's less than a cup of coffee a week."

**CLOSE**
"The last library levy lost 48 to 52. This one will be close too. Can we count on your yes vote in March?"
