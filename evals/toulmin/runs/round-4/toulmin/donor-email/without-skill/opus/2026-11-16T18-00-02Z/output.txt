I left out the promise that a gift this month guarantees every child a place in January. Your own numbers don't support it, and a promise that doesn't come true would cost you more than a weaker subject line would:

- **Total needed:** 61 children × $1,150 is **$70,150**.
- **Best possible case:** if all 340 lapsed donors came back at their $85 average, that's $28,900. With the $15,000 match on top, you'd reach about $43,900, which still isn't enough.
- **Likely case:** last December this group gave $1,240. Even if this year's response were five times better, it would cover about 10% of the cost.

If you send the guarantee and 50 kids are still waiting in February, the people most likely to point it out are the donors who already feel let down. That includes the former board member who forwards your emails with her comments. So the draft makes claims you can stand behind: the match doubles their gift, and $575 becomes a full year of lessons for one child.
