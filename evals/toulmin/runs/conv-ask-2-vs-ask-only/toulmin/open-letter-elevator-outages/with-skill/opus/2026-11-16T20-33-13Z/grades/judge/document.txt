**An Open Letter to Keystone Property Group**
From the Harbor View Apartments Tenants' Association, 220 Grant Street
December 1, 2026

Dear Ms. Weller,

Harbor View has nine floors, 112 apartments and one elevator. This year that elevator has been out of service on 41 days, more than a month in total. In August it was out for 12 days in a row.

For most of us an outage means a hard climb. For some of our neighbours it means much more. Twenty-three residents are over 75, and six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the elevator stops, they cannot get down to the street to see a doctor, pick up a prescription or buy groceries. If there were an emergency, they would need someone to carry them down the stairs.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and passed it. We do not question that it is safe when it runs. Our concern is that too often it does not run.

In June, residents were told that a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. Five months later, we are being told the same thing.

Seventy-four of our 112 households signed a petition asking for a second elevator. Keystone has told us that would cost about $400,000 and cannot be done in this building. We have no engineering study of our own, so we are not pressing that request today. Instead we are asking for three things that can be done now:

1. **A written date for the controller repair**, and a written update if that date changes.
2. **A stair-climbing chair kept in the building, and staff trained to use it**, so that residents who cannot manage the stairs are not trapped in their homes during an outage.
3. **A rent credit for every day the elevator is out of service.**

Here is what we will do. The association will keep a list of residents who need help on the stairs and share it with Keystone, so staff know who to reach first during an outage or an emergency. We are also ready to meet with you on any weekday evening to work out the details.

We are posting this letter in our lobby and sharing it with Channel 7 because this affects every household in the building. We hope the next notice we post will be Keystone's written commitment.

Sincerely,

Rosa Delgado
Secretary, Harbor View Apartments Tenants' Association
