Hollis Dairy

To the Admissions Committee:

I am the farm manager at Hollis Dairy, a 400-cow dairy operation, and I am writing to recommend Tomás Reyes for your two-year agricultural technology program. Tomás has worked for me for three years, since he was 19, and he is one of the people I rely on most.

Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift on his own, five days a week. Nobody else is on the farm at that hour. He is responsible for the herd, the equipment and anything that goes wrong until the rest of the crew arrives. In three years he has missed two shifts.

Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early, and our vet estimated that this saved the farm about $8,000. That is the kind of work your program teaches: using data and technology to make better decisions about animal health. Tomás is already doing it, and he learned it on the job.

Last summer his father became seriously ill in Mexico, and Tomás had to leave for five weeks. He told me two days before he left. Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
