I left out the "every other publisher" line. You only know about one other publisher, so that would be stating something you can't back up. Here's the strongest true version: "The rate for this work is well above $9,000." The $12,500 fee and your agent's range come right after it to support it, and those are your strongest facts anyway.

<final_document>
Subject: Fee for the next book

Dear Nadia,

Thank you for offering me the next book. I've loved working with you over the past four years, and I'd very much like to do this one.

Before I accept, I need to raise the fee. I've been paid $9,000 per book since my first title with you, and six books later it's still the same. In that time, two of our books have sold more than 20,000 copies. One was chosen for a state reading list and went into its fourth printing this spring.

The rate for this work is well above $9,000. Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a book of the same length. My agent tells me $11,000 to $13,000 is the normal range for an illustrator with my record.

For this book I'm asking for $11,500. That's in the lower half of that range, and I think it fairly reflects what our books have done for your list. Each one takes me about four months, and I can't take on another at $9,000.

If this needs to go to anyone else for approval, I'm happy to send whatever would help. I hope we can make it work. I'd love to keep making books with you.

Best wishes,
[Your name]
</final_document>

About 215 words.

- **What's left out on purpose:** The email doesn't mention your $11,000 minimum or that you'd have a gap in your schedule. If Nadia offers less than $11,500, you can decide then whether to come down.
- **Tone:** It's warm but not casual, since you weren't sure how formal you two usually are.
- **Book titles:** If you find the titles before sending, put them in. "*[Title]* was chosen for the [State] reading list" is more convincing than "one was."
- **Your agent:** Check whether your agent normally handles fees before you send this. If they do, they may want to send it or be copied on it.
