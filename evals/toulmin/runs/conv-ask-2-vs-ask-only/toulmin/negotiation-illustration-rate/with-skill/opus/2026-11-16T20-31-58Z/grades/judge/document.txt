Subject: The Night Garden

Dear Nadia,

Thank you for offering me The Night Garden. After six books together over four years, I'd love to illustrate it, but first I need to raise the fee with you.

My fee has been $9,000 since my first book with you. Since then, two of our books have each sold more than 20,000 copies. One of them made a state reading list and went into its fourth printing this spring.

Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a book of the same length. The standard rate for an illustrator with my record is $11,000 to $13,000. A fee of $9,000 is below the bottom of that range, and I can't take on The Night Garden at that fee.

I'm asking for $11,500. At that fee, I can commit to sketches within six weeks of signing and final art by September 1. I'll also add two spot drawings for your marketing at no charge.

I know you need my answer by the end of next week. I'd be glad to talk it over by phone before then if that would help.

Best wishes,
Elena Varga
