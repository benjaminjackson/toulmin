Subject: The Night Garden – fee

Dear Nadia,

Thank you for offering me The Night Garden. I'd love to illustrate it, and I've really valued the six books we've made together over the past four years.

Before I confirm, I need to raise the fee. I've been at $9,000 per book since our first project, and my record has grown a lot since then. Two of our books have each sold more than 20,000 copies, and one was chosen for a state reading list.

The market has moved as well. Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a 32-page book of the same scope. My agent says the standard range for an illustrator with my record is $11,000 to $13,000.

For The Night Garden I'm asking $11,500. That's toward the low end of that range, and it reflects how much I value working with you. Each book takes me about four months, and I'm not able to take on another one at $9,000.

I can deliver final art by September 1, and I'd be glad to confirm everything by the end of next week. If it would help to talk it through, I'm happy to set up a call.

Best wishes,
Elena Varga
