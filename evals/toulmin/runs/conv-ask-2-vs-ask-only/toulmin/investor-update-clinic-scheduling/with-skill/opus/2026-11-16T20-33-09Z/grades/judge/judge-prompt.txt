Subject: Investor update — November 2026

Hi all,

This is our first update in six months. I skipped the last two because the numbers were flat and I didn't want to send bad news. That was the wrong call. From now on you'll get an update in the first week of every quarter, and the next one is January 7.

In short, revenue held steady even though we lost our largest customer. We have about 11 months of runway. On December 1 we start selling our text reminders as a standalone product. I'd like 20 minutes on the phone with each of you before the end of the year.

**Numbers**
- MRR: $41,000, the same as three months ago
- Customers: 9 added, 4 lost
- Cash: $480,000
- Net burn: about $44,000 a month, which gives us roughly 11 months

**The customer we lost**
Our largest customer was a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500 a month, about 13% of revenue. They moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. New clinics made up the loss, which is why MRR is flat and not down.

That risk hasn't gone away. Two other customers use the same records system, and together they pay $3,800 a month. I can't promise they won't leave the same way. Records systems that come with their own scheduler are the biggest threat to our core product.

**What we're doing about it**
Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a no-show rate of 7%, down from 13% before they started. That cuts missed appointments almost in half, and it's the result clinics care about most.

From December 1 we'll sell reminders on their own for $150 a month. A clinic won't need to use our scheduler to get them, so we can keep selling to clinics whose records system already includes one. Six clinics have told us they'll buy at launch. That's $900 a month. It's a start, but it doesn't close the gap to our burn. I'm not putting a customer target on it yet. You'll see actual numbers on January 7.

**Runway**
I haven't decided yet whether we raise, cut burn or aim for break-even. If nothing changes, we run out of cash in about 11 months, so I need to decide soon. I'd value your views before I do.

**Two asks**
1. **20 minutes on the phone.** Please reply with a time that works for you. I'd like to talk through the runway decision with each of you.
2. **Introductions to physical therapy clinic chains.** They're the buyers for both the scheduler and the standalone reminders.

If you'd like a one-page set of numbers every month, reply and I'll add you to the list.

Thank you for your patience over the last six months.

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
