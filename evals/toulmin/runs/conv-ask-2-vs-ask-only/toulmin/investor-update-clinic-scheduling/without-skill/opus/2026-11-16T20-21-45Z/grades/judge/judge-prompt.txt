Subject: [Company] quarterly update: flat revenue, losing our largest customer, and what's next

Hi all,

First, I owe you an apology. This is my first update in two quarters. The numbers were flat and I didn't want to send bad news. That was the wrong call. From now on you'll get an update in the first week of every quarter, starting January 7. I'll also send a one-page set of numbers every month to anyone who wants it. Just reply and ask.

**The numbers**
- Monthly recurring revenue: $41,000, the same as three months ago.
- Clinics: we added 9 and lost 4.
- Cash: $480,000 in the bank. We're losing about $44,000 a month, so we have about 11 months of runway.

**The loss that matters**
One of the four clinics we lost was our largest customer, a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500 a month. They moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. Revenue from new clinics made up for the loss, which is why revenue held flat instead of falling. But it also means this quarter's sales went into replacing revenue, not growing it.

The risk is bigger than one customer. Two other customers use the same records system and together pay $3,800 a month. I can't promise they won't leave the same way. I also don't yet know how many of our clinics overall use a records system that has its own scheduler.

**What's working**
Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a 7% no-show rate, down from 13% before they started using them. That is the clearest value we deliver today.

**What's next**
On December 1 we start selling the reminder feature on its own for $150 a month. Six clinics have said they'll buy it at launch. If all six follow through, that's $900 a month. It's small, but it's our first test of whether reminders can sell on their own. I'll report how it went on January 7.

**What I'm asking for**
1. **20 minutes on the phone with each of you.** I haven't decided whether to raise more money or cut costs, and I'd like your view before I do. Please reply if you're open to a call.
2. **Introductions to physical therapy clinic chains.** If you know someone who runs or manages operations at a multi-clinic practice, I'd be grateful for an intro.

Thank you for your patience over the last six months. You'll hear from me on January 7.

[Your name]
[Company]

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
