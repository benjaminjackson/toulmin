# Bookkeeping for hair and nail salons. Only salons.

I'm Carla Benson. I managed a hair salon for six years before I became a bookkeeper. Now salons are my only clients. I work with 31 of them. Last year 2 left, and both had sold their salons.

## What you get for $199 a month

- Your books closed every month by the 10th
- Payroll for up to 10 staff
- Your sales tax filed

Until January I charged $149. I raised the price when I added payroll, and I raised it for every client, including the ones who'd been with me for years.

I don't do income tax returns. Your accountant or tax preparer keeps doing those. The difference is that they get clean books from me.

## Clean books cost you less at tax time

Last tax season, 9 of my clients told me their accountant charged them less because their books came in clean. One said she saved $800. If your accountant spends every spring sorting out your year, you're paying for that time.

## The 10th, and what happens if I miss it

I close each month's books by the 10th of the next month. Last year I missed that date 3 times. Since January, if I miss the 10th, you don't pay for that month.

## Working with one person

Chair and Ledger is just me. You won't be handed from one staff member to another, and you'll always know who did your books. I take calls Tuesday to Saturday. I don't answer the phone on Sundays or Mondays.

There's no contract. If it isn't working for you, give me 30 days' notice and you're done.
