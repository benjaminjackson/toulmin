# Lantern Winter

A lighthouse keeper's family has a long winter ahead. Lantern Winter is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players, and your job is to see them through to spring.

## It's finished

The art is done. The rules are done. I have a signed factory quote for 1,500 copies. This campaign doesn't pay to develop a game. It pays to print one that already exists.

## Proven at the table

Since 2023 I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. Every rule in the box has been through that table, many times over.

I took Lantern Winter to two conventions. On the main board game ratings site it has an average rating of 8.1 from 23 players. Most of those ratings came from convention-goers who sat down at my table, played it, and went home and rated it.

Then 1,240 people signed up to hear the moment this campaign went live. At $49 a copy, Lantern Winter is fully funded if fewer than half of them back it.

You don't have to take my word for how it plays. A board game reviewer with about 90,000 YouTube subscribers has a copy and is posting a video during launch week.

## Who's making it

I'm Owen Hale, and this is my first published game. I work full time as a land surveyor, a job where the numbers have to be right and I sign my name to them. I've brought that habit here, so here are the numbers.
