**[Food Bank Name]: Refrigerated Van Request**

[Food Bank Name] is a volunteer-run food bank serving a town of about 9,000 people. Each month our 23 regular volunteers hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

We are asking the foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van.

**The need**

Fresh food is the hardest part of our work because it has to stay cold from pickup to distribution, and we have no refrigerated vehicle. Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down because we had no way to collect it cold. That food was donated and available a short drive away. The only thing missing was a way to get it to our families safely.

**What the van will do**

With a refrigerated van, our volunteers can pick up donated produce from local farms and bring it in cold. Two of the farms that offered produce last summer have written letters saying they will donate again if we can pick up cold.

Our goal is to accept the farm produce we now have to refuse. Last summer's offers came to about 3,000 pounds a month during the harvest season. That would increase the fresh food we hand out by up to half during those months. Over a full year, it would add about 12 percent to the 72,000 pounds of fresh food we now distribute. Farm donations vary from season to season, so this is a target based on last year's actual offers, not a guarantee.

**Who will run it**

Four of our regular volunteers have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule. We estimate insurance and fuel at about $4,500 a year.

**Our investment**

We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves, more than a quarter of the van's cost. This will leave us $11,000 in reserve. We are making this commitment because the van solves a problem we have not been able to solve any other way.

**Accountability**

The foundation supported us in 2023 with $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late because our treasurer at the time was on an extended medical absence. Our current treasurer, Dave Lin, will be responsible for the final report on this grant and will submit it within 30 days of the end of the grant year. We will also send the foundation our fresh food totals every quarter, so you can follow the van's results during the year instead of waiting until the end.

**Summary**

Last summer, local farms offered us 9,000 pounds of fresh produce, and we had to say no. A refrigerated van closes that gap. With the foundation's $50,000 and our own $18,000, we can say yes the next time a farm calls.
