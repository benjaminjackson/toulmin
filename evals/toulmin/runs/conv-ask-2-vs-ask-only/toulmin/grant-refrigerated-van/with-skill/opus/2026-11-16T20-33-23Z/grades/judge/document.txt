Last summer, local farms offered Millbrook Community Pantry about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or store it. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van so that next time we can say yes.

Millbrook Community Pantry is run entirely by volunteers in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

A refrigerated van will let us collect fresh food from farms and keep it cold on the way back. Two of the farms that offered produce last summer have written to say they will give again if we can pick up cold. We cannot promise they will offer the same 9,000 pounds. If they offer a similar amount, it will add about 3,000 pounds of fresh food a month during the growing season. That is a 50 percent increase in those months, or about five more pounds of fresh food for each household every month.

We have 23 regular volunteers, and four of them have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule.

The van costs $68,000. We will cover $18,000 from our own reserves, more than a quarter of the price, which will leave us with $11,000. Insurance and fuel for the van will cost about $4,500 a year. That cost is not yet in our budget, and we would rather tell you now than have you find it later.

The Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving in 2023, and our final report on that grant was four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period. Our treasurer is now Dave Lin, and he will be responsible for this grant's final report, due 30 days after the grant year ends. We will also send the Foundation our fresh food totals every quarter, so you can see what the van is bringing in during the year and not only at the end.

Those quarterly reports will track two numbers: how many pounds of fresh food we hand out, compared with today's 6,000 a month, and how much of the produce farms offer us we are able to accept.

Without a refrigerated van, we will keep turning that produce away, and fresh food will stay at about 6,000 of the 38,000 pounds we hand out each month. With it, food that local farms already want to give can reach the 610 households we serve. We respectfully ask the Foundation for $50,000 toward that van.

Joan Pruitt, Director
Millbrook Community Pantry
