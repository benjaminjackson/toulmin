**Project Narrative: A Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food**

Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or store it.

Every month our volunteers give out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. In each of those three summer months, the produce we turned away would have raised our fresh food by half.

**What the van will do**

With a refrigerated van, we can collect produce from the farms and keep it cold on the way back to us. In harvest months, accepting the produce we turned down last summer would take our fresh food from about 6,000 pounds a month to about 9,000. Over a full year, that adds about 9,000 pounds of fresh food, roughly one-eighth more than we give out now.

Two of the farms have written to say they will give again if we can pick up cold. We can't promise the farms will offer the same 9,000 pounds every year, so we will report to you what actually comes in.

We are run entirely by volunteers. Of our 23 regular volunteers, four have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule.

**Accountability**

In 2023 the Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late, because our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period.

Our current treasurer, Dave Lin, will be responsible for the final report on this grant, due 30 days after the grant year ends. We will also send the Foundation our fresh food pounds every quarter. You will see what the van is bringing in throughout the year, not only at the end.

**The request**

The van costs $68,000. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 and will pay the other $18,000 from our reserves, which is more than a quarter of the cost. That leaves us $11,000 in reserve. Insurance and fuel will cost about $4,500 a year, and that cost is not yet in our budget.

**If we do not get the van**

Without a refrigerated vehicle, we'll be in the same position the next time farms offer us fresh food: our volunteers will have to say no. Last summer that meant 9,000 pounds.
