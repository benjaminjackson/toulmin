Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches will close March 31

To all staff,

I'm writing to tell you about a decision that affects all of us, and 14 of our colleagues most of all. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Everyone who works at those two branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center at the main office on Elm Avenue, at the same pay or higher. Every one of them has a job here if they want it.

Here's why. Over the last three years, visits to those two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union went up 60%. Members who call us now wait an average of 9 minutes on hold. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we haven't kept up. This decision is final.

The new role is a real change, and I won't pretend otherwise. The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. Staff with the most years here will choose shifts first, but I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule. After 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week.

Many of you know our current phone team lost about 30% of its people last year. That's a fair thing to worry about. Part of what has made that work hard is too many calls for too few people, and adding 14 colleagues who know our members is part of how we fix it. We've also built in a safety net: if you take the new role and decide within six months that it isn't right for you, you can still leave with the full severance.

What happens next:

- Written offers go out by February 14.
- Decisions are due by March 1.
- If you choose not to take the new role, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.
- Anyone who wants a one-on-one with HR to talk through their situation can have one.

Some questions don't have answers yet, including when training starts and how members at Maple Street and Route 9 will be served after March 31. I'll share those answers as soon as we have them, and I won't guess before then.

To the staff at Maple Street and Route 9: you know our members, and that is exactly what members on the other end of the phone need. Whatever you decide, thank you for the work you've done.

Chief Operating Officer
