**A lighthouse. A family. One long winter.**

The supply boat won't come again until spring. Until then it's the keeper, the family and the light.

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. Together you see a lighthouse keeper's family through a winter. You don't play against each other. Everyone at the table wins together or loses together, and the winter is hard.

**Tested at the table, 140 times**

I started playtesting in 2023. Since then I've run 140 sessions with about 60 different people. That's more than two games per person, because people who played it asked to play again.

I took it to two conventions to find out what strangers thought. On the main board game ratings site, 23 people have rated it so far, and the average is 8.1 out of 10.

The game hasn't been sold yet, so I can't show you a sales record. What I can show you is what has to come first: when people play it, they come back.
