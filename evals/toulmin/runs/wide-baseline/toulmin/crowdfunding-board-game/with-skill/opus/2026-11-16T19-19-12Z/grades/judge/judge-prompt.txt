<document>
**One winter. One lamp. One family.**

The supply boat won't come back until spring. The lamp has to burn every night. The family who keeps it has to get through the winter together.

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You don't play against each other. You play against the season, and you win or lose as a family.

**Proven at the table**

I've been building this game since 2023. In that time it has been through 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people, and I've taken it to two conventions. Every session taught me something, and most of them changed something. The game you'd be backing is the version that made it through all of them.

Twenty-three players have rated it on BoardGameGeek so far. Their average is 8.1 out of 10.

Before there was anything to buy, 1,240 of you signed up to hear when this campaign launched.

I'm not asking for money to find out whether this game works. Those 140 sessions answered that. I'm asking for money to print it.

**Who I am**

I'm a land surveyor. I work full time and designed this game in the hours around that job. This is my first published game and my first campaign.

That raises a fair question: will a first-time creator actually deliver? My answer is the budget.

**Where your money goes**

The goal is $28,000. A copy costs $49, with US shipping included.

- **Printing:** I have a factory quote of $17,400 for 1,500 copies.
- **Shipping:** getting each copy to a backer costs about $9.
- **At the goal:** that's about 570 backers. Shipping their copies comes to about $5,100, so printing plus shipping is about $22,500. The rest covers platform fees and payment processing.

At the goal, the whole print run and every backer's shipping are paid for. Printing doesn't depend on reaching a stretch goal.

**What it takes**

If about half of the 1,240 people on the launch list back the game, it's funded.

If it falls short, there's no print run. The game stays a prototype that 60 people have played and nobody else can buy.

**What you get**

For $49 you get a finished copy shipped to your door in the US, along with a winter at the edge of the sea for one to four people at your table. You'll be playing a game that was tested 140 times before you ever opened the box.

Thank you for reading, and thank you to everyone who sat down at the table with me over the last three years. You're why this game is ready.

Let's keep the light on.
</document>

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
