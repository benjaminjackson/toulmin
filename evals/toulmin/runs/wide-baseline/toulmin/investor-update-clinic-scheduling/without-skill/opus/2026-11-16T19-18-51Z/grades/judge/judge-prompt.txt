**Subject: [Company] quarterly update: flat revenue, a big loss, and what we're doing about it**

Hi all,

First, an apology: I didn't send updates for the last two quarters. That was a mistake, especially going into a quarter like this one. You'll hear from me every quarter from now on.

**The numbers**
- Monthly recurring revenue: $41,000, flat from three months ago
- Clinics: 9 added, 4 lost
- Cash: $480,000
- Net burn: about $44,000/month, which gives us about 11 months of runway
- Team: 6

**What happened**
Our largest customer left this quarter. They were a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500/month, about 13% of our revenue, and they moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. The 9 new clinics we added made up for that loss. That's why revenue is flat and not down.

I don't think this was a one-off. Records vendors are bundling scheduling, and it's hard to compete with "good enough and already included" for chains that already run everything inside one system. Independent and smaller clinics are a different situation, but I'd be fooling myself (and you) if I treated this as bad luck.

**What's working**
Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a no-show rate of 7%, down from 13% before they started. That's nearly half. For a clinic, a no-show is lost revenue they can measure directly, so this is the clearest return we offer.

**What's next**
Next month we start selling the reminder feature on its own for $150/month. There are two reasons:
1. It's the part of our product with proven, measurable value.
2. It lets us sell to clinics that are happy with their current scheduler, including the ones built into records systems, so we don't have to compete with those schedulers head-on.

Next quarter I'll report on standalone reminder sign-ups, churn and revenue, so you can judge whether this is working.

**Runway**
Eleven months isn't long. [Your plan: e.g., when you intend to raise and how much, the cost changes you're making, or the revenue level that gets you to break-even, and by when.]

**How you can help**
- Introductions to owners or operators of physical therapy clinics, especially independents, for the reminder launch.
- Introductions to anyone who has sold a standalone add-on into a market where the big platform bundles a "good enough" version.
- [Any other specific ask.]

Thanks for your patience over the last six months. I'm happy to get on a call with any of you to go deeper.

Ben
</document>

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
