Hi all,

First, an apology. You haven't heard from me in two quarters, and that was my mistake. From now on you'll get an update every quarter, whether the news is good or bad.

**Headline numbers**
- Monthly recurring revenue: $41,000, the same as three months ago
- Clinics: 9 added, 4 lost
- Cash: $480,000
- Net burn: about $44,000 a month, so about 11 months of runway

**The bad news**
We lost our largest customer this quarter, a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500 a month. They switched to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. The 9 new clinics made up for that revenue, which is why monthly revenue stayed flat instead of falling. Still, we aren't growing, and that isn't where we need to be.

This loss also taught us something. For larger groups, a basic scheduler that comes free with their records system is a real competitor. Scheduling on its own may not be enough to keep them.

**The good news**
Our automatic text reminders are working. Clinics that use them have a 7% no-show rate, compared with 13% before they started. That cuts missed appointments almost in half. Every no-show is a lost visit for the clinic, so this is the clearest value we deliver. It also works no matter which scheduler a clinic uses.

**What's next**
Starting next month, we'll sell the reminder feature on its own for $150 a month. This lets us sell to clinics that are committed to their records system's scheduler, including groups like the chain we just lost, without having to replace that scheduler. It also gives current customers another reason to stay.

Runway is my main concern. In the next update I'll report how the standalone reminders are selling and what that means for our spending and fundraising plans. You'll get that update on time.
