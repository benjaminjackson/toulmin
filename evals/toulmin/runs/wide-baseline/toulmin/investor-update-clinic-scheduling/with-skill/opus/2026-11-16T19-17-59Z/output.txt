Revenue stayed flat because we lost our largest customer. A 12-clinic chain paying $5,500 a month moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. The 9 new clinics made up for that loss, but they didn't grow us.

This is the biggest risk to the business, and I'd rather name it than hide it. Records vendors are putting "good enough" scheduling into their products. Chains that already pay for those systems will be tempted to consolidate. A scheduler on its own will not hold up against that.
