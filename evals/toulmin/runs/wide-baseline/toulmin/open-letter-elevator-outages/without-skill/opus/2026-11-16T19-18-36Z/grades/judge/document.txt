**An Open Letter to [Property Management Company]
From the Tenants' Association of [Building Name]**

[Date]

To the management of [Building Name]:

We are writing for the residents of the 112 apartments in our building. We have one request: an elevator we can depend on.

Our building has nine stories and one elevator. This year that elevator has been out of service on 41 days, roughly one day in every eight. One outage in August lasted 12 days in a row.

For many of our neighbors, these aren't small inconveniences. Twenty-three of our residents are over 75, and six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the elevator stops, some of them can't leave their homes to get groceries, go to medical appointments or get out in an emergency. Others carry children, groceries and laundry up as many as eight flights of stairs.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and gave it a passing certificate. We're glad it meets the safety standards. But passing an inspection isn't the same as being reliable. An elevator can be safe and still be out of service when residents need it.

In June you told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. Five months later, we're being told the same thing. Residents have lost confidence in these estimates.

Seventy-four households, about two-thirds of the building, signed our petition for a second elevator. You've told us it would cost about $400,000 and can't be done in this building. We take that seriously. But we haven't seen the assessment behind it, and our residents deserve to understand the decision.

We are asking you to do the following:

1. **Give us a firm repair date in writing.** Include the supplier's confirmed delivery date for the controller part and a timeline for installation.
2. **Make a plan for outages.** Give notice right away whenever the elevator goes down, and arrange assistance for residents who can't use the stairs.
3. **Report every month.** Post the dates of each outage, its cause and the repair status in the lobby.
4. **Share the second-elevator assessment.** Show us the engineering and cost review that led to your conclusion, or commission an independent one.
5. **Meet with us.** Schedule a meeting with the Tenants' Association within the next 30 days.

We want to work with you on this. Please respond in writing within 14 days to [contact address].

Sincerely,

[Name]
Secretary, on behalf of the [Building Name] Tenants' Association
