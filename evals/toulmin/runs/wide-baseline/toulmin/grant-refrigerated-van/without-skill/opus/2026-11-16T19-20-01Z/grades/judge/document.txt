**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

Every month, our volunteers distribute about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households in our town of roughly 9,000 people. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. We are requesting $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van that will let us accept, transport and store much more fresh food.

**The Need**

The limit on our fresh food is not supply. It is cold transport and storage. Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We had to turn it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or keep it fresh until distribution. That food was available to families in our community, and we could not accept it.

**The Project**

A refrigerated van solves both halves of the problem. It lets us collect produce directly from farms at harvest and keep it at safe temperatures until it reaches families. Four of our 23 regular volunteers have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so it will be in regular use from the day it arrives.

**Expected Results**

Based on last summer's offers alone, the van will allow us to distribute at least 9,000 additional pounds of fresh produce in its first year. That is a 12.5 percent increase over our current 72,000 pounds of fresh food a year. During the three summer harvest months, it raises our fresh food distribution by half. We consider this a floor. Once we can reliably collect cold food, we will approach farms and donors who have not been able to work with us before. We will track the pounds of fresh food we receive and distribute each month and report these figures to the Foundation.

**Our Investment**

The van costs $68,000. We will contribute $18,000 from our reserves, more than a quarter of the total, which leaves our reserves at $11,000.
