Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: what it means for you

Team,

I want you to hear this directly from me. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Every one of the 14 people who work at those two branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher.

Here is why. The way our members reach us has changed. Over the last three years, visits to these two branches fell 44%, while phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. We haven't kept pace. A member who calls us today waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. Every one of us feels that: by the time a member gets through, they're already frustrated. If we do nothing, those waits will keep growing as calls keep climbing.

The new center puts our most experienced people where members now come to us. Nobody knows our members better than the staff at Maple Street and Route 9, and they are exactly the people we want answering.

What this means for the 14 of you:

- Pay: the same as today or higher.
- Flexibility: after 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- Hours: the center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, longer than the branches' 9-to-5, and shifts will rotate. That means some early starts and some later evenings.

I won't pretend this is easier for everyone in every way. For some of you, rotating shifts will be hard to fit around family or other commitments. That is a real cost, and I want to hear about it. [Name/HR] will meet with each of you individually by [date] to go through the role and the schedule and answer your questions.

If the new role isn't right for you, you will receive two weeks of pay for every year you have worked here. Please let us know your decision by [date].

To everyone at our other five branches: this center is built to answer the calls that today sit on hold. Members will get help faster, whichever way they reach us.

I believe this change sets us up for better, more secure jobs, doing the work our members increasingly need from us. I'm grateful to the Maple Street and Route 9 teams for everything they have built in those branches. I'm glad to be bringing that experience with us.

My door is open, and so is my inbox.

[Your name]
Chief Operating Officer
