Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

I am writing to recommend Priya Nair for doctoral study in marine ecology. I have taught biology at Westbrook College for 17 years and supervised about 30 senior theses. Priya is one of the three best students I have taught.

I know her work well. Priya took three courses with me and earned the highest grade in the class in two of them, each with 28 students. I also supervised her senior thesis, which is the main reason I am confident she is ready for doctoral research.

Her thesis compared oyster growth at six sites in our bay over 14 months. She did all of the fieldwork herself, making about 90 trips by kayak in every season and in all kinds of weather. Few undergraduates keep up a field schedule like that for a semester, and she did it for more than a year. She found faster growth at the two sites nearest the salt marsh. That is a clear and interesting result, and it raises real questions about how marsh proximity shapes oyster habitat quality. It comes from a single study period, and a storm cost one site three months of data, so the next step is to repeat the work. I think the finding is promising and well worth that follow-up. I expect a repeated and extended version would be a strong candidate for publication. Priya presented the work as a poster at a regional conference.

Her work outside Westbrook shows the same reliability. Last summer she worked as a paid field assistant at a state shellfish lab. For two weeks she ran the lab's water sampling program on her own. Her supervisor there told me that he would hire her again.

Priya does not yet have a publication. I would ask the committee to weigh what she has already shown: she can plan and sustain a long field study, collect data carefully under hard conditions, recognize a meaningful pattern, and present it to other scientists. Professional researchers have trusted her to work independently. These are the abilities that turn into publications in a doctoral program, and in my experience they are the hardest to teach.

I recommend Priya Nair to your program without reservation. Please contact me if I can provide more information.

Sincerely,

Dr. Anne Caldwell
Department of Biology
Westbrook College
