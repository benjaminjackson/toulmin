# Steady Plate
## Lunches and dinners for type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian

Most people managing type 2 diabetes know what they're supposed to eat. The hard part is doing it every day: counting carbs, planning meals and cooking after a long day, week after week.

Steady Plate takes that work off your plate. Every Sunday, we deliver 10 cooked lunches and dinners to your door. Each one has **45 grams of carbohydrate or less**, so you don't have to count.

### Every menu is planned by a dietitian

I'm Lena Ortiz, a registered dietitian. I plan every Steady Plate menu myself. I don't use a template or an algorithm. I build each week's meals around the needs of adults living with type 2 diabetes.

### What our customers have seen

Fourteen customers shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months with us. **For 10 of them, A1C went down.** For 3 it stayed about the same, and for 1 it went up.

I'm sharing all of those numbers because I can't promise what will happen to your A1C. Everyone's body, medication and routine are different, and these 14 people chose to share their results. Steady Plate doesn't replace your doctor's advice or your treatment plan. It makes it easier to eat the way your care team recommends.

Today, 88 people get our meals each week. 37 of them have been with us for more than six months.

### How it works

- **10 meals a week for $129** (about $12.90 a meal), delivered every Sunday.
- **Change any meal you like.** Each week you can trade any meal for one of 6 other choices.
- **No contract.** Skip a week or cancel whenever you want.
- **Order by Thursday at noon** for that Sunday's delivery.
- **We deliver within 25 miles of downtown.**

### Try one week for $59

You don't need to commit to anything to find out whether Steady Plate works for you. A trial week costs $59. Eat the meals, see how they fit your days, and then decide.
