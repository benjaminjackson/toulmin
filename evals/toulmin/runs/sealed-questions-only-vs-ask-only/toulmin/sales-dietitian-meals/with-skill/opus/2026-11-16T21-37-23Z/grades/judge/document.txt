# Steady Plate

## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, with every menu planned by a registered dietitian

With type 2 diabetes, every meal means thinking about carbs. You read labels, guess at portions, and still have to cook on nights when you're tired. Steady Plate does that part for you. Every Sunday we deliver ten cooked meals to your door. Each one has 45 grams of carbohydrate or less, and Lena Ortiz, RD, plans every menu.

### How it works

- **10 meals a week for $129.** Lunches and dinners, cooked and delivered every Sunday.
- **45 grams of carbohydrate or less in every meal.** You don't have to count carbs or guess.
- **You choose.** Any meal you don't want can be swapped for one of 6 other choices that week. The menu repeats every four weeks, so you'll get to know your favorites.
- **Order by Thursday at noon** to get that Sunday's delivery.
- **No contract.** You can skip a week or cancel any time.

### Planned by a dietitian

Lena Ortiz is a registered dietitian, and she plans each Steady Plate menu herself. She checks every meal against the 45-gram limit before it goes on the menu. Every week you eat meals planned for blood sugar management by someone trained in it.

### What our customers are seeing

We deliver to 88 customers each week, and 37 of them have been with us for more than six months.

Fourteen customers shared their A1C results with us from before they started and after three months. For 10 of them it went down, and the average drop across all 14 was 0.6 points. Three stayed about the same, and one went up. Results vary, and we can't promise any result for you. Our meals work alongside your doctor, your care team and your medication. They don't replace them.
