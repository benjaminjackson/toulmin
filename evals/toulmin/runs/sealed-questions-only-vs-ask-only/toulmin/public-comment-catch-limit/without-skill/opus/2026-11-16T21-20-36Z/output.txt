Commissioners, my name is [NAME]. I've run a charter boat out of [HARBOR] for 22 years. I run about 160 trips a season at $900 a trip.

First, we don't dispute your staff's science. The stock is at 78% of target, and we want it rebuilt as much as anyone, because our living depends on it. What I'm asking is that you rebuild it in a way that leaves charter boats in this harbor when it's done.

I know what a limit cut does to bookings because I've been through one. After the 2016 cut I dropped from 170 trips to 138 the next season. That's 32 trips, close to $29,000 gone in one year. Two years later I was still 15 trips short of where I'd been.

This cut is bigger. It halves the limit, and it lasts three years. Our charter association surveyed 70 past customers. Forty-one said they'd book fewer trips at two fish. Only twelve said they'd book the same.

There are 11 charter boats in our harbor. Two captains have already told me that if bookings fall the way they did in 2016, they'll sell. If you lose a charter boat, it doesn't come back when the stock does.

Charter boats land about 9% of this state's summer flounder. Private boats land about 80%. We're a small part of the catch, but we're the part where every fish is somebody's paycheck.

We're not asking you to do nothing. We would accept a three-fish limit, or a shorter season at four fish. Please ask your staff to look at whether either of those can meet the target before you cut to two.

Thank you.

A few notes:

- **About "out of business":** the comment doesn't say every boat will close, because you can't back that up. What it says instead is all true: the two captains who told you they'd sell, how big this cut is compared with 2016, your survey numbers, and "if you lose a charter boat, it doesn't come back." That last line is your strongest argument, and nobody can dispute it.
- **The $29,000** is 32 trips × $900. The comment doesn't claim the 2016 cut was the same size as this one, since we don't know that. It only says this one halves the limit and lasts three years.
- **Check before you speak:** a three-fish limit or shorter season may not meet the staff's target. That's why the request is for staff to look at those options, not a claim that they work. If anyone has asked staff about this already, use their answer.
- If you learn your break-even number or the harbor's crew count before the hearing, add it after the 2016 paragraph. Either one would make the comment stronger.
