Some notes before you use it:

- **Length:** It's about 290 words, which runs close to two minutes at a normal pace. Time yourself reading it out loud. If you need to cut, drop the private-boat comparison first.
- **The harbor point:** I kept it to what you can stand behind: what's in your books, what two captains told you, and what a lost boat means for the docks. Nothing in it claims boats will close.
- **The association commitment:** You weren't sure you can speak for the association. Before the hearing, confirm they'll back testing the reporting app. If they won't, change that line to "I'll ask our association to test…"
- **"At today's rates":** This is there because your 2016 price may not have been $900. If you know the 2016 price, you can use the actual dollar figure.
- **What I left out:** I didn't mention that 70% of your trips are already booked. A commissioner could take it as a sign that demand is fine. If someone asks, you can answer honestly: those customers booked expecting a 4-fish limit, and you don't yet know how many will keep their trips.
