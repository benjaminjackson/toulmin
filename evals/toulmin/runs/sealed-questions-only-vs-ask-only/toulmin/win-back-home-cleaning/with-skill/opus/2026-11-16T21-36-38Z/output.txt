Some notes:

- **The cleaner promise:** "You'll always know who's coming" is the strongest thing you can say truthfully. It's only true if customers are actually told their backup's name. Please make sure that happens before this goes out.
- **Price after the first visit:** I left out $165 because you weren't sure what returning customers will pay. People will ask, so have an answer ready for replies and calls. If you settle on a price, add one line like "After that, it's your regular rate of $X." It would also make the email stronger.
- **Turnover:** "1 of 18 since July" is only about four months of data. It's true, so I kept it. Just don't round it up to something like "our cleaners stay" in replies.
- **Greeting:** It says "Hi," because you haven't decided on merging names. If you do merge first names, the email will feel more personal.
