**Bring Back the Majestic**

The Majestic on Main Street has been dark since 2022. The nearest cinema is now 38 miles away. When the Majestic closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week.

We can reopen it by Thanksgiving. We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and repair the roof. If we raise it, the building's owner will rent the Majestic to us for $1 a year for ten years.

Some of you remember our last campaign. Three years ago we asked for $120,000, reached $47,000, and returned every dollar. This time is different:

- **The goal is $35,000 lower.**
- **A local bank has pledged $25,000** if we raise the rest by June 30.
- **Three local businesses have already pledged $11,000** in writing.
- **46 volunteers have signed up** to run the box office and the snack bar.

That leaves **$49,000** to raise from our community between May 1 and June 30.

**Your pledge is safe.** This campaign is all-or-nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge is returned in full, just as last time.

**You'll see where the money goes.** If we succeed, we'll post a public spending report at the town library every three months.

**One honest note:** we can't promise new films in their opening week. Release dates are set by the film distributors, not by us. We can promise a screen, a seat and a night out without a 76-mile round trip.

Every pledge, large or small, brings the Majestic closer to opening its doors. Pledge by June 30, and let's turn the lights back on.

Ruth Albers
Chair, volunteer group to reopen the Majestic
