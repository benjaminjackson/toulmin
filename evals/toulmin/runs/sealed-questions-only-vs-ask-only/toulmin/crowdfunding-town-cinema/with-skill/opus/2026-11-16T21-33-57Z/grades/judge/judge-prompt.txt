**Bring the Majestic Back**

Three years ago, we asked you to help reopen the Majestic. Our goal was $120,000. You gave $47,000. It wasn't enough, and we returned every dollar.

We're asking again, for less money and with more already behind us.

**The goal is $85,000.** That pays for a new projector and sound system and repairs the roof. If we raise it, the building's owner will rent the Majestic to us for $1 a year for ten years.

**We're not starting from zero:**

- A local bank has pledged $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30.
- Three local businesses have pledged $11,000 in writing.
- 46 volunteers have signed up to run the box office and the snack bar.

That leaves **$49,000** to come from the people who'll sit in the seats.

When the Majestic closed in 2022, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. Since then, the nearest movie screen has been 38 miles away. That's a long drive for a Saturday matinee, a first date, or a night out with the kids.

**If we fall short, you get your money back.** This campaign is all-or-nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge is returned in full, just like last time.

**You should also know this:** our roof estimate is from last fall, and the contractor has told us it could rise by as much as 15%. We'd rather you hear that from us now than find out later.

**We'll show you where the money goes.** Once the money is in, we'll post a public spending report at the town library every three months, so you can see where every dollar went.

If we reach the goal, we plan to open the doors by Thanksgiving. We can't promise every new release in its opening week, because that's up to the film distributors. We can promise that there will be a working screen on Main Street again, run by your neighbors.

The campaign runs from May 1 to June 30. Please pledge what you can, and tell someone who remembers the Majestic.

Ruth Albers
Chair, on behalf of the volunteers working to reopen the Majestic
