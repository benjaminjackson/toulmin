**Bring Back the Majestic**

The screen at the Majestic on Main Street has been dark since 2022. Our town has 6,500 people, and the nearest cinema is now 38 miles away. Seeing a film on a Friday night means a 76-mile round trip.

When the Majestic closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. The audience never left. Now we have a real chance to reopen it.

**What it takes**

We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and to fix the roof. If we raise it, the building's owner will rent the Majestic to us for $1 a year for ten years.

**Where we stand**

- A local bank has pledged $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30, 2027.
- Three local businesses have already pledged $11,000 in writing.
- 46 volunteers have signed up to run the box office and the snack bar.

The rest has to come from us: the people who live here and want our cinema back.

**Yes, we've tried before**

Three years ago we set out to raise $120,000. We reached $47,000, fell short, and returned every pledge in full.

This time the goal is $35,000 lower. The bank and local businesses are already on board. And the promise is the same: this campaign is all-or-nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, 2027, every pledge comes back to you in full. We've done it before, and you can hold us to it.

**What we're asking**

Pledge what you can, then tell a neighbor, your family group chat, and whoever you used to see at the Saturday matinee. Every dollar from this town also unlocks the bank's $25,000.

Let's turn the lights back on at the Majestic.
