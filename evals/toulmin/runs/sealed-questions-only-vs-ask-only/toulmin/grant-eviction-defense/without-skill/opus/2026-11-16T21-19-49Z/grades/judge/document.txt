**Tenant Defense Project: A Third Eviction Defense Attorney**

Last year, 1,140 eviction cases were filed in our county. The Tenant Defense Project represented the tenant in 163 of them. We turned away about 300 more tenants because no attorney was free to take their case.

Our attorneys are at capacity. Each of our two attorneys carries about 80 open cases, and one of them leaves in June.

The tenants we do represent often keep their homes or gain time to move. In 58% of last year's cases, our client either stayed in the home or secured at least 60 days to relocate.

We request $90,000 over two years ($45,000 per year) to hire a third staff attorney, so that fewer tenants are turned away for lack of an available lawyer.

**Funding plan.** The position costs $78,000 per year, including benefits. The grant and a family fund's two-year pledge of $20,000 per year together cover $65,000 per year, or 83% of the cost. The Tenant Defense Project will pay the remaining $13,000 per year from its general budget. Our core operations also rely on a $60,000 annual county contract that comes up for renewal in October. Because outside funds cover most of the new position's cost, it does not depend on that renewal.

**Reporting.** Each year of the grant, we will report how many tenants we represented, how many we turned away, and our case outcomes, measured the same way as the 58% figure above.
