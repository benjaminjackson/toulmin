Dear David,

I'd like to be considered for promotion to senior paralegal in the January review cycle. I've been a paralegal in the litigation group for five years. The senior role means supervising the other paralegals and making sure the group never misses a deadline, and I already do much of that work.

**My work last year**
I supported four lawyers on 23 cases, two of which went to trial. I billed 1,640 hours, 90 above the group's target of 1,550.

**The deadline tracking sheet**
In 2023 I built the court-deadline tracking sheet our group uses. Two other groups in the firm now use it as well.

I also want you to hear about a mistake I made in that sheet. Last March I entered a filing deadline wrong. Another paralegal caught it, and we filed two hours before the court's cutoff. That was too close. In April I added a second-person check, so a second person now confirms every deadline entered in the sheet. - A short weekly check-in with the paralegals I supervise, to go over workload and upcoming deadlines and catch problems early.
- Completing the state paralegal certification by June 2027.

I understand the promotion comes with a $9,000 raise and responsibility for supervising the group's paralegals. I'm ready for both.

Thank you for considering my request.

Best regards,

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
