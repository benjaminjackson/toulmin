**Subject: Three office days a week from 1 September 2027**

Everyone,

From 1 September 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. This ends fully remote work at the firm. I want to explain why we're doing it, what it means for the people most affected, and what we can and can't offer.

For 15 of you, this changes what you were told when you joined. You were hired as fully remote in 2021 and 2022, and many of you have organised your lives around that. We are going back on that arrangement, and I won't pretend it's a small thing.

**Why we're doing this**

Last year we lost two large hospital projects at the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. That cost us work we wanted and were capable of doing.

We're seeing the same problem inside the firm. Our review records show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. People learn much of what it takes to lead by working next to people who already do it. We haven't been giving our newer colleagues enough of that.

**What this asks of the partners**

The three partners have been coming in one day a week. From September, we'll be in all three days as well. Five of our six project leads already come in three days a week on their own.

**If you live a long way from the office**

Four of you live more than two hours from the office. You can keep working remotely until 31 December 2027. From January 2028, we're asking you to come in one day a week. I know that gives you only four months longer than everyone else.

Anyone who lives more than 30 miles from the office can claim up to $150 a month toward commuting costs.

Apart from those four colleagues, we can't make individual exceptions. I'd rather tell you that now than leave you hoping for one.

**Practical points**

- **Desks:** We don't have a desk for everyone yet. New desks arrive in August, before the change begins.
- **Review:** Six months after the change starts, we'll review the policy with staff.

**Friday**

We'll go through all of this at the all-staff meeting on Friday at 10 a.m. in the main studio. If you have questions before then, or something you'd rather raise privately, please come to me or to HR.

Paul Whitaker
Partner
