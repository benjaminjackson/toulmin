Subject: Moving to three office days from September 1, 2027

Dear all,

Before we meet on Friday, I want you to have the full details in writing. Starting September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. This includes the partners. For the past while we have come in one day a week, and from September we'll be in on all three days.

For some of you this is a big change. Fifteen of you joined us in 2021 and 2022 as fully remote employees. You built your lives around that arrangement, and we agreed to it. I'm not going to pretend this is a small adjustment.

**Why we're doing this**

Last year we made the final interview for two large hospital projects and lost both. When we asked the clients why, they both told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. We do good work, but the way we work together showed in that room and it cost us.

The second reason matters just as much to me. According to our review records, junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. That isn't down to their talent. They have had less time sitting near experienced people, watching how decisions get made and asking questions as they come up. We owe them better than that.

**What this means for you**

- **Office days:** Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, starting September 1, 2027.
- **Commuting support:** if you live more than 30 miles from the office, the firm will pay up to $150 a month toward your commuting costs.
- **Colleagues who live more than two hours away:** the four people in this group can keep working remotely through December 31, 2027. From January 2028 they will come in one day a week. I'll talk to each of you individually.

These four are the only exceptions, and I'm not able to offer others. The decision and the three days are final.

**What happens next**

Six months after the change begins, we'll review how it's working together with all of you, and we'll take that review seriously.

I know some of you will be wondering whether you can make this work and what happens if you can't. I don't have a complete answer to that yet, and I'd rather tell you so than give you a vague one. If you're in that position, please come and talk to me before or after Friday. I'd much rather hear about it early.

We'll go through all of this on Friday, and you can ask me anything.

Paul Whitaker

A few notes on the draft:

- **Mondays and Fridays:** you didn't know, so the email doesn't mention them. Expect someone to ask on Friday, so it's worth deciding before the meeting.
- **Staff who can't make it work:** the email invites them to talk to you but promises nothing. If the partners agree on an answer before Friday, adding it here would help more than anything else in the email.
- **The four long-distance staff:** the email says you'll talk to each of them individually. Ideally do that before the email goes out, so they don't learn the details along with everyone else.
