**Subject: Office days from 1 September 2027**

Everyone,

From 1 September 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. This ends fully remote work here. Before we meet on Friday, I want to explain why we're doing this and what it means for you.

**Why we're making this change**

Two things changed our minds.

Last year we lost two large hospital projects in the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. They weren't questioning anyone's skill. They were watching how we worked together in the room, and they didn't see people who were used to working side by side.

Our review records also show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. That isn't their fault. A lot of what gets someone ready to lead comes from working near people who already do it. You overhear a client call, someone marks up your drawing at your desk, or you get pulled into a conversation. We haven't been giving our newer people enough of that.

The partners haven't helped. We've been coming in one day a week. From September, we will be in on all three days as well.

**What this means for you**

- **Office days:** Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, starting 1 September 2027.
- **Commuting support:** If you live more than 30 miles from the office, the firm will pay up to $150 a month toward your commuting costs. This applies to everyone, not only people hired as remote.
- **Colleagues who live more than two hours away:** You can keep working remotely until the end of 2027. From January 2028, you will come in one day a week.
- **Exceptions:** We are not able to make individual exceptions beyond the arrangement above.

We're telling you almost ten months ahead so you have time to plan.

**To those of you we hired as fully remote**

Fifteen of you joined us in 2021 and 2022 on fully remote terms. This changes what you agreed to when you joined, and I won't pretend it doesn't. For most of you it means coming in three days a week. Some of you will have hard choices to make about how this fits your life. If you want to talk it through, please come to me or to HR directly.

**Friday**

We'll meet on Friday at 10 a.m. in the main studio. The meeting is for your questions, so I'll keep my part short. If you'd rather ask something before then, or in private, come to me or to HR.
