**Subject: Office days from September 1, 2027**

Everyone,

From September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday stay remote. I want to explain why before we meet on Friday.

**Why we're doing this**

Last year we reached the final interview for two large hospital projects and lost both. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. That's the kind of work we most want to win, and we lost it because of how we came across as a team.

The second reason is about people who've joined recently. Our review records show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. Five of our six project leads already come in three days a week by choice. Juniors who work mostly from home get less time alongside them, and we think that's part of the gap.

This applies to the partners too. We've been coming in one day a week, which is less than we're now asking of you. From September, all three of us will be in on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

**For those of you hired as remote**

Fifteen of you joined in 2021 and 2022 as fully remote employees. This is a real change to the job you took on, and I won't pretend otherwise.

- The four of you who live more than two hours from the office can keep working remotely until December 31, 2027. From January 2028, you'll come in one day a week.
- Everyone else will work the three office days from September 1.
- Those four are the only exceptions. I know some of you will have hoped for more, and I'd rather tell you that now than on Friday.

**What we're putting in place**

- Anyone who lives more than 30 miles from the office will get up to $150 a month toward commuting.
- We don't have a desk for everyone yet. New desks arrive in August, before the change starts.
- The policy is decided, but it isn't fixed forever. Six months in, we'll review with all of you how it's working.

**Friday**
