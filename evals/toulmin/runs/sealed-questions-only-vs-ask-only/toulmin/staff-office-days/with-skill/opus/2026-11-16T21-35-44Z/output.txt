Two things to check before sending:

- **Contracts.** Nobody has checked whether the offer letters for the 15 remote hires list remote work as a term. If they do, changing it may need each person's agreement. That would change how the "For those of you hired as remote" section should read. I'd have HR or your employment lawyer check before Friday.

Even if you expect to grant none, it may be safer to replace "Those four are the only exceptions" with something like "Apart from those four, the three days apply to everyone." That way the email doesn't promise to refuse every request.
