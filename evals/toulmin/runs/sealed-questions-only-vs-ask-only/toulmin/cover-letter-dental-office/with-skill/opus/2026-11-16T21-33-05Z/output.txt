Dear Dr. Pierce,

I am applying for the Office Manager position at Brookside Family Dental. For eleven years I was office manager at a veterinary clinic with four veterinarians and fourteen staff, and I would like to bring that experience to a practice here in town.

At the clinic I handled scheduling and billing for the whole practice. In 2014 I led our move from paper records to practice-management software (AVImark). In my last three years there, I reduced invoices unpaid past 90 days from about $38,000 to $9,000.

I left the clinic on my own, giving two weeks' notice, when my mother was diagnosed with an illness. For the next seven years I cared for her full time. That included managing her insurance claims and coordinating her appointments with six specialists, so I know this work from the patient's side as well. For the past four years I have also been the volunteer treasurer at my church, keeping the books in QuickBooks. My mother passed away last spring, and I am ready to return to full-time work.

I have not used Dentrix yet. I have enrolled in the 20-hour Dentrix online training course and will finish it before my first day.

I can start within two weeks of an offer and can work your Tuesday and Thursday evening hours. Dr. Sue Kim, head veterinarian at the clinic, has agreed to be a reference.

I would welcome the chance to talk with you about keeping Brookside running smoothly for your dentists, your staff and your patients.

Sincerely,

Linda Marsh

A few notes:
- **What I left out:** The letter doesn't mention your age, and it doesn't say why the reference isn't from the clinic's owner. If someone asks, just tell them he retired and moved abroad. There's no need to raise it first.
- **Supervising staff:** The letter never says you led people, because you didn't have specific examples. Expect the interview to ask how you would manage a team of 9. It's worth thinking of one or two real examples before then, even small ones.
- **Check before sending:** The letter says you "led" the move to new software. If you shared that work with others, change it to "managed" or "handled." Also make sure "Office Manager" matches the job title in the posting exactly.
