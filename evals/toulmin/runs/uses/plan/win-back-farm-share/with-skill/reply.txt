## The angle

These readers mostly don't need to hear about vegetables. They need a reason to trust you again. At least some of them left because they paid $540 and got roughly 3 weeks' worth less food (6 weeks at half), about $74 per share, with no credit and one short email. So the email has to make this case:

> **Claim:** Give us another season.
> **Grounds:** We know what happened last year and how we handled it. Here's what we've changed since.
> **Link the reader has to accept:** A farm that admits what went wrong and has visibly changed things is worth a second try.

You're the one who has to win back their trust, not the other way round. So the apology comes before the pitch. Don't argue that "CSA members share the risk." It's true, but to these readers it will sound like an excuse.

## Order

1. **Admit it plainly.** On July 9 hail took half the crop. Boxes were half-size for 6 weeks, you didn't offer credit, and you said too little about it. Keep this to one or two sentences and don't defend it.
2. **What you changed, described exactly.** Hail nets over the tomatoes and peppers and a second field on higher ground. Mentioning the $31,000 once is fine because it shows you're serious. Don't let it read as a plea.
3. **What's in it for them.** Same price as last year ($540), and they can now pause up to 2 weeks for credit.
4. **The ask.** Sign up by March 15, 52 shares left.
5. **A personal sign-off** that invites replies, so people can tell you why they left.

## Leave out

- **97 of 260 didn't renew.** That's your problem, not theirs, and it signals desperation.
- **Any claim the changes will stop it happening again.** The nets cover two crops. Higher ground helps with drainage and frost, not hail.
- **Any suggestion that pausing is a fix for the hail.** It covers members' absences, not crop failure. Present it as a separate new benefit.

## Find out first

1. **If disaster cuts the boxes again, what will you do?** Credit, extra weeks, faster updates? This is the real answer to their complaint, and right now the facts don't include one. Only promise something you'll actually honor.
2. **Will you offer lapsed members anything for last year,** like a discount or bonus weeks? If so, think about the 163 who renewed. They got the same half boxes and will hear about it.
3. **Why did the second field go on higher ground?** I need the true reason so the email doesn't imply it protects against hail.
4. **Do you know why people left?** If some moved or left for other reasons, the opening shouldn't assume everyone left over the hail.
5. **When does this go out, and is there an earlier deadline?** Today is November 16 and sign-up closes March 15. Is 52 the number left now, and will it still be accurate when you send?
