- **Your rent in year five:** With 3% increases, your $5,100 offer reaches about $5,740 by year five, which is more than the $5,650 he asked for. I put that figure in because it's your strongest number. If you'd rather not commit to it in writing, take that sentence out.

- **Lower sales:** I mentioned your 6% drop in sales once, because it's the honest reason you can't pay $5,650. You may prefer to leave it out if you think he'll read it as a sign you're struggling.
- **The empty unit:** I mentioned it just once and didn't work out the cost of the vacancy for him. That's so it reads as information and not as pressure.
