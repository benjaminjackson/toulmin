**Project Summary: Tenant Defense Project**

The Tenant Defense Project requests $90,000 over two years ($45,000 per year) to fund a staff attorney position. One of our two attorneys leaves in June. This grant keeps at least two lawyers defending tenants facing eviction in our county.

**Need.** Last year 1,140 eviction cases were filed in our county. Our two attorneys, each carrying about 80 open cases, represented the tenant in 163 of them, about one in seven. We turned away roughly 300 more tenants because no attorney was free.

**Without this grant.** After June we would have one attorney, able to represent roughly half the tenants we serve now. More than 300 tenants a year would be turned away.

**Results.** In the cases we handled last year, 58% ended with the tenant staying in the home or getting at least 60 days to move. We do not yet have a comparable figure for tenants without a lawyer. Beginning in January, we will request court-wide outcome data from the county clerk so that next year we can make that comparison.

**Budget.** The position costs $78,000 a year with benefits. This grant would provide $45,000 a year, a family fund has pledged $20,000 a year for both years, and $13,000 a year comes from our general budget. Our general budget includes a $60,000-a-year county contract that is up for renewal in October, and we cannot guarantee it will be renewed. $65,000 of this position's annual cost does not depend on that contract.

**Accountability.** Every six months, we will report to the Foundation the number of cases opened and how they ended.

Maria Chen, Managing Attorney, Tenant Defense Project
