**Project Summary**

[Clinic Name] requests $90,000 over two years ($45,000 per year) to add a third staff attorney to our eviction defense practice, expanding legal representation for low-income tenants facing eviction in [County].

**The need.** In [year], 1,140 eviction cases were filed in [County]. Our two attorneys represented the tenant in 163 of them, about 14%. **Our results.** In cases where we represented the tenant, 58% ended with the tenant remaining in the home or receiving at least 60 days to move. That time allows families to secure new housing on their own terms rather than under the pressure of an immediate lockout.
