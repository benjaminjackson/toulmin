<document>
**An open letter to the State Employees' Health Plan**
*From 38 independent pharmacies, on what the July 1 contract must fix*

To the leadership of the State Employees' Health Plan:

We are 38 independent pharmacies writing together as the Main Street Pharmacy Alliance. More than one in five prescriptions we fill is for a member of your plan. At my own store, Reilly Drug in Carver, that came to about 3,100 prescriptions last year.

Since January, your plan has paid us less than it costs us to buy and fill the 20 generic drugs we dispense most. On average, we lose $1.80 on each one. These are not rare or specialty drugs. They are the everyday prescriptions your members pick up most often.

Some of us make up part of that loss on brand-name drugs. We can't tell you how much, and we won't pretend otherwise. But no pharmacy can rely on one kind of prescription to keep covering losses on its most common ones, month after month.

Two of our 38 pharmacies have already told me they will stop filling prescriptions for plan members if nothing changes. Nine independent pharmacies in this state have closed in the last two years. We don't know how many of them closed because of payment rates, and we won't claim they all did. What we do know is that every month we are paid below cost moves more of us closer to the same decision.

That matters most outside the cities. In 14 counties, the nearest pharmacy for most people is an independent one, and for most people there the nearest chain pharmacy is more than 20 miles away. When a pharmacy there stops taking your plan, your members can't switch to another store down the street. They have to drive 20 miles or more for a refill.

Your contract with your pharmacy benefit manager comes up for renewal on July 1. We ask one thing of it: that it pay pharmacies at least what each drug costs us, plus a fee for filling it.

We will show you the numbers. We will share our purchase invoices for all 20 drugs with your staff, so you can check the figures yourselves. And we will meet with your staff at any time before May 1, so there is time to write this into the contract.

Your members count on us to be there. We're asking you to make sure we can stay.

Sincerely,

Tom Reilly
Owner, Reilly Drug, Carver
On behalf of the Main Street Pharmacy Alliance and its 38 member pharmacies
</document>
