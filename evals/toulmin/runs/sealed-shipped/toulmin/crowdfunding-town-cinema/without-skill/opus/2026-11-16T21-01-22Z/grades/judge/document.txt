**Let's Reopen Our Cinema**

Since our cinema closed in 2022, seeing a film on a big screen has meant a 76-mile round trip. Before it closed, our neighbors were buying about 180 tickets a week. People here didn't stop going to the movies. We lost the place to go.

We want it back, and this time our plan is built to succeed.

**What we need: $85,000**

That pays for a new projector, a new sound system and a repaired roof. That's what the building needs to open its doors again.

**Why this time is different**

Some of you gave to our campaign three years ago. It aimed for $120,000, reached $47,000, and we refunded every dollar. We learned from it. This time:

- **The goal is smaller.** We're asking for $85,000, not $120,000.
- **A local bank is backing us.** [Bank name] has pledged $25,000 if we raise the remaining $60,000 by June 30.
- **The building is secured.** If we reach our goal, the owner will rent it to us for $1 a year for ten years. Your money goes into the equipment and the roof, not into rent.
- **The people are ready.** 46 volunteers have already signed up to run the box office and the snack bar.

**What your gift does**

Every dollar you give before June 30 brings us closer to unlocking the bank's $25,000. If we raise $60,000, their pledge completes the goal. If we fall short, we lose both the bank's money and the $1 lease.

Our town has 6,500 people. $60,000 works out to less than $10 per resident.

That's the price of one ticket for a Friday night that doesn't involve a 76-mile drive. It's a first date, a kids' matinee or a reason to be downtown after dark.

Give what you can. Then tell your neighbors. Let's turn the lights down, and the projector on, one more time.
