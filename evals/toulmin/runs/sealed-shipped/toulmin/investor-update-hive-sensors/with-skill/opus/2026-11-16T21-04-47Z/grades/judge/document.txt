**Subject: HiveSense half-year update and bridge round**

Hi everyone,

Here's where HiveSense stands, and what we need from you before December 15.

**Growth**

We now have 31 paying beekeepers, up from 12 a year ago. Revenue over the last six months was $212,000. It arrives with the seasons: 70% came in March and April, as beekeepers prepared colonies for pollination.

Every sale earns a margin. A sensor costs us $38 to make and sells for $55. Each hive then pays $4 a month for the app. That means a hive brings in $103 in its first year and $48 a year after that.

**Results in the field**

Our largest customer runs 4,000 hives. He lost 18% of his colonies last winter, compared with 31% the winter before. Across 4,000 hives, that's about 520 more colonies alive. He says the sensors were part of it. He also changed his mite treatment that year, so we don't claim the whole improvement.

**Competition**

In May a large farm equipment company launched a similar sensor at $45. Theirs measures temperature only. Ours also measures sound and hive weight. Three of our beekeepers have asked to test theirs. We haven't yet decided whether to keep our $55 price. At $45, our margin on each sensor would fall from $17 to $7. App revenue would stay the same.
