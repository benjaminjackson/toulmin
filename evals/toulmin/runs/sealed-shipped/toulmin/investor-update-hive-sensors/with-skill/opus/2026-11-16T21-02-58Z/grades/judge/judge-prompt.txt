**Subject: HiveSense half-year update and our bridge round**

Hi all,

HiveSense now has 31 paying commercial beekeepers, up from 12 a year ago. We're raising a $600,000 bridge on a convertible note, and I'd like each of you in it. Here's where we stand, including the parts that aren't comfortable.

**The half year**

Revenue was $212,000. 70% of it came in March and April, as beekeepers got ready for pollination. Each sensor costs us $38 to make and sells for $55, and each hive pays $4 a month for the app.

Our biggest customer runs 4,000 hives. He lost 18% of his colonies this winter, against 31% the winter before. That's about 520 more colonies alive this spring. He says the sensors were part of it. He also changed his mite treatment, so I can't tell you how much of that result is ours.

**Competition**

In May a large farm equipment company launched a similar sensor at $45. Theirs measures temperature only. Ours also measures sound and hive weight. Three of our beekeepers have asked to test theirs. Nobody has switched that I know of. We aren't changing our price.

**Cash**

We spend about $95,000 a month and have $340,000 left. I'm bringing spending down to $75,000 a month by February by not filling two open roles. Even so, without new money we run out around early March, just as the season that brought in 70% of our revenue begins.

**The bridge**

- $600,000 convertible note, 20% discount, $6M cap
- One angel has already committed $250,000
- Closes December 15
- Everyone in the bridge gets a monthly cash report from me

With the full round and spending at $75,000 a month, we have cash to around October 2027 before counting any revenue. That carries us through the whole 2027 season, so we'll have a full season's results in hand.

**The path to break-even**

I won't promise profit in 2027 without a model I can stand behind. Here is what the numbers do show. At $75,000 a month, we cover our costs when revenue reaches about $450,000 per half year, roughly double this half. Last year we went from 12 customers to 31, so doubling is within the pace we've already run.

The test is next March and April. This year those two months brought in about $148,000. If March and April 2027 bring in $300,000 or more, we're on course to break even during 2027. If they fall short, you'll see it in the May cash report.

If you'd like to be part of the bridge, reply to me by December 1 with the amount. There's no minimum.

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
