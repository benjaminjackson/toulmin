**Subject: Working in the office from September 2027**

Dear all,

From September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and fully remote work will end. Before we meet on Friday, I want to explain why we're doing this, what it means for you, and what we'll do to help.

**Why we're making this change**

Last year we lost two large hospital projects at the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. Our experience and design work were strong enough to get us to the final round. What the clients saw in the room was a group of people who didn't work together day to day, and it cost us both projects.

We're also seeing a pattern in our review records. Junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. We can't show that remote work is the cause, and we're not claiming that it is. But architects learn a lot from overhearing a call with a consultant, getting pulled into a review at the next desk, and watching how a colleague handles a difficult client. That only happens when people share a room. We think its absence is part of the problem, and we don't want people's careers to move more slowly while we wait for proof.

The partners share responsibility for this. We have been coming in one day a week. From September, we'll be in all three days as well.

**What this means for you**

- Fifteen of you were hired as fully remote in 2021 and 2022. This changes the terms you joined on, and I know it's a significant change.
- The four colleagues who live more than two hours from the office will keep their current arrangement until the end of 2027. From January 2028 they'll come in one day a week. We can't make any other exceptions to the three days.
- Anyone who lives more than 30 miles from the office can claim up to $150 a month toward commuting costs.
- We don't have a desk for everyone yet. New desks arrive in August, before the change begins.
- After six months, in March 2028, we'll review the policy with you.

I'm telling you now, more than nine months ahead, so you have time to plan. If this change raises questions about whether the job still works for you, please talk to me or to HR.

**Friday's meeting**

We'll meet on Friday at 10 a.m. in the main studio. If you have questions before then, send them to me or to HR.

Paul Whitaker
Partner
