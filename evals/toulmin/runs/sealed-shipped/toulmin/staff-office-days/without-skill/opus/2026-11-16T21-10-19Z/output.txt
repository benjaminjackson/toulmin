**Subject: Moving to three office days a week from September 1**

Hi everyone,

We're sending this before Friday's all-staff meeting so you have time to read it and think about it before we talk.

**What's changing**

From **September 1, 2027**, everyone at the firm will work in the office on **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday**. This ends fully remote work at the firm.

**If you were hired as fully remote**

Fifteen of you joined in 2021 and 2022 on fully remote terms. We know this changes the arrangement you joined under, and we don't take that lightly.

- **The four of you who live more than two hours from the office** can keep working remotely until **December 31, 2027**. From January 2028, you'll come into the office one day a week.
- **For the other eleven**, the three-day pattern starts on September 1, the same as for everyone else.

**Why we're doing this**

There are two reasons.

First, last year we lost two large hospital projects at the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. Clients notice this, and it is costing us work.

Second, our review records show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. Much of what makes someone ready to lead is learned by sitting near more experienced people: overhearing a difficult client call, getting a quick answer at someone's desk, seeing how a problem gets solved. That's much harder to do remotely.

We don't think remote work is the only cause of either problem. But we think it's a large part of both, and we would rather act now than wait for more evidence.

**This applies to us too**

The three of us have been coming in one day a week. That's part of the problem, and it changes as well. From September 1, we'll be in the office all three days.
