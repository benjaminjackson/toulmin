# Steady Plate

## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian

Managing type 2 diabetes means thinking about carbohydrates at every meal. Steady Plate takes ten of those meals off your plate. Each Sunday we deliver 10 cooked lunches and dinners to your door. Every one has 45 grams of carbohydrate or less.

**[Start a trial week: $59]**

### Every menu planned by a dietitian

I'm Lena Ortiz, RD, and I plan every Steady Plate menu myself. Each meal is built to keep carbohydrates in check so your blood sugar has a steadier day. You don't have to count, weigh or plan anything.

### What our customers have seen

Fourteen customers have shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months. Ten saw their A1C go down. Three stayed about the same, and one went up. Across all fourteen, the average drop was 0.6 points.

These results aren't a promise. Fourteen people is a small group, everyone's body is different, and I don't track whether customers changed their medication during that time. The meals don't replace your doctor's advice either. Keep your care team informed, especially if you take medication that lowers blood sugar.

People do stay with us. Of our 88 current customers, 37 have been with us for more than six months.
