Good evening. I've run a charter boat out of this harbor for 22 years. I take out about 160 trips a season.

I'm not here to dispute your staff's numbers. The stock is at 78 percent of its target, and nobody needs it rebuilt more than the people who fish it for a living. I'm asking you to do it with a three-fish limit, or a shorter season, instead of two fish.

Here's why. The last time the limit was cut, in 2016, my bookings fell from 170 trips to 138 the next season. A fifth of my business was gone in one year. Two years later I was back to 155. Today I'm at 160. Ten years on, I still haven't gotten all of it back.
