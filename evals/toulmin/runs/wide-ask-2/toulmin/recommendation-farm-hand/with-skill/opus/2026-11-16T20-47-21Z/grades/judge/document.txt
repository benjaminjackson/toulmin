Dear Admissions Committee,

I am writing to recommend Tomás for your two-year agricultural technology program. I manage a 400-cow dairy farm, and Tomás has worked for me for three years, since he was 19. He already does on our farm the kind of work your program trains people for, and I recommend him without reservation.

Five days a week, Tomás runs our 3 a.m. milking shift on his own. At that hour no one is checking on him. The cows have to be milked on time, the equipment has to run properly, and any problem has to be dealt with or reported before the day crew arrives. I gave him that shift because I trusted him with it, and he has kept that trust.

He has also outgrown the job I hired him for. He learned our herd management software, and he now uses it to flag sick cows for our veterinarian. Last winter he caught nine cases of mastitis early. Catching it early means faster treatment, less lost milk and healthier cows. He managed that by reading the data and also watching the animals in front of him. As I understand it, your program is built to develop exactly that mix of practical stock sense and comfort with technology.

His attendance has been excellent. He has missed two shifts in three years. The one exception came last summer, when his father fell ill in Mexico. Tomás gave me two days' notice and was gone for five weeks. That left us short-handed, and I won't pretend otherwise. But it was a family medical emergency, and when it was over he came back and took up his shift again.

His spoken English is good. His written English is weaker, and he knows it. He is taking a night class to improve it while working a shift that starts at 3 a.m. He learned our software through that same persistence, and I expect him to bring it to his coursework.

Tomás is reliable, he pays attention, and he wants to learn more than the job requires. I believe he will do well in your program and that the industry will be better for having him. I would be glad to speak with you if you have any questions.

Sincerely,

Farm Manager
