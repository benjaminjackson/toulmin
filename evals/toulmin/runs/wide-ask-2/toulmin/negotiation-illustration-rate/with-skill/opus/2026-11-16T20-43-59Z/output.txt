I left out the line saying every other publisher pays at least $12,000, because your own facts don't support it. One publisher paid you $12,500, and your agent's range of $11,000 to $13,000 includes fees below $12,000. Editors know roughly what the market pays, so a claim like that could easily get called out. If it does, it weakens the rest of your case. It would also make your ask of $11,500 look odd, since it's below the number you'd just called the minimum. Stated accurately, your real numbers make a strong case, and the draft uses them that way.

You didn't give me your editor's name, the book's title or your name, so the greeting and sign-off are left plain. The draft is about 190 words.
