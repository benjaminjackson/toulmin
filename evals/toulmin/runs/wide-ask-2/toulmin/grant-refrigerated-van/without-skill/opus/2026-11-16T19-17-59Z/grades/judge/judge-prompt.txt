**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van for Fresh Food Recovery**

[Food Bank Name] is a volunteer-run food bank serving 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month, our 23 regular volunteers distribute roughly 38,000 pounds of food. About 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. These are the foods our families value most and the hardest for us to handle, because we have no way to transport or hold them cold.

**The Need**

That gap cost our community real food last summer. Over three months, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce. We had to turn all of it down because we could not pick it up cold or keep it fresh. That is 50 percent more fresh food than we normally distribute in those months. We had willing donors and families who needed it, and the only thing missing was a cold vehicle.

**The Project**

We request $50,000 toward the $68,000 purchase of a refrigerated van. The van will let us collect produce, dairy and eggs directly from local farms and keep them cold until distribution. Four of our volunteers have committed to driving on a weekly schedule, so the van will be in regular use from the start.

**Expected Results**

In the first year, we expect to:

- Accept the farm donations we have been turning away, about 9,000 pounds during the growing season based on last summer's offers.
- Increase the fresh food we distribute by about 12 percent over the year, and by about 50 percent during the summer months.
- Set up regular cold pickups with local farms, which we expect to increase our fresh food supply further in future years.

We will track the pounds of fresh food received and distributed each month, broken out by source, and share these figures with the foundation.

**Our Investment**

We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. This is a significant commitment for an all-volunteer organization, leaving us with $11,000 in reserve, and it reflects how central cold transport is to serving our families well.

**Our History with the Foundation**

The foundation supported us in 2023 with a $12,000 grant for shelving. We recognize that our final report on that grant was four months late. For this grant, we will submit a short update each quarter with our monthly tracking figures. That way the foundation can follow our progress throughout the year, and the final report will arrive on time.

**Conclusion**

Local farms already want to give fresh food to our neighbors. A refrigerated van turns that offer into fresh food on family tables. With the foundation's support, we can stop saying no.
