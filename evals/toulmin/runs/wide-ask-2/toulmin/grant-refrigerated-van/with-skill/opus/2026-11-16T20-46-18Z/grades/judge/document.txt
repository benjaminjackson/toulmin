**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

Each month, our all-volunteer food bank distributes about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households in a town of about 9,000 people. Only about 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. That is the food families most often can't afford, and the food we most often can't accept.

The problem is not supply. Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down. We had no way to pick it up cold, and produce that rides in a volunteer's hot car spoils before it reaches a family.

**Our request**

We are requesting $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves. That is a large share of what we have, because it will leave us with $11,000. We are making that commitment because we believe this van is the most valuable thing those funds can buy for the families we serve.

**What the van will do**

The van will let us collect donated produce, milk and eggs from farms and other donors and bring them to distribution at safe temperatures. Four of our 23 regular volunteers have already committed to driving it on a weekly schedule, so it will be on the road from the week it arrives.

**What we expect**

Last summer's offers alone would have increased our fresh food by half during those months: about 3,000 additional pounds a month on top of the 6,000 we distribute now. If farms repeat those offers, that is 9,000 more pounds of fresh food a year reaching local households.

We see that figure as a floor, not a ceiling. It counts only the farms that came to us unasked. With a van, we can go to them and to other donors whose fresh surplus we have never been able to take.

We will record the pounds of fresh food the van collects each month and report them against our current baseline of 6,000 pounds a month.

**Our record with the Foundation**

In 2023, the Foundation granted us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late. We regret that, and we will file every report for this grant on schedule.

**If this request is not funded**

Without a refrigerated van, next summer will look like last summer. Farms will offer us fresh food, and we will have to say no while families in our town go without it. With the Foundation's support, we can say yes.
