**A cooperative game about getting a family through the winter.**

The supply boat won't come again until spring. The lamp has to stay lit every night. One to four players share a lighthouse keeper's work and worries: rationing oil and food, mending what the storms break, and keeping everyone at the table going until the ice breaks up. You win or lose together.

**Tested, not guessed at**

Since 2023 I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. I've watched families, game groups and first-time players get through the same winter in very different ways. I've changed every rule that confused people and every turn that dragged. The game you're backing is the one that came out of those sessions.

I also took it to two conventions to see how strangers would react. On the main board game ratings site, the 23 people who have rated it so far gave it an average of 8.1. That's a small number of ratings, and I won't pretend otherwise. But every one of them comes from someone who sat down, played a whole winter, and then chose to rate it.

And 1,240 people have signed up to hear when the campaign launches. If you're one of them, thank you. You're the reason this page exists.
