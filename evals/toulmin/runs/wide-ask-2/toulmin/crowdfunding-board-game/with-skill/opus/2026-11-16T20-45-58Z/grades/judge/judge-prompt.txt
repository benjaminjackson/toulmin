**A winter at the lighthouse. A cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players.**

When the storms come in, the supply boat stops running. Until the thaw, a lighthouse keeper's family has only each other, what's in the stores, and the light they have to keep burning. In this game you and up to three others see that family through the winter together. You win or lose as one household. It also plays solo.

**Tested at the table, not just on paper**

I've been building this game since 2023. In that time it has been through 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. Some of them were friends, and many were strangers who owed me nothing. I took it to two conventions, where people sat down without any introduction and played it. So far, 23 people have rated it on BoardGameGeek, and the average is 8.1 out of 10. I won't call that a verdict. Twenty-three ratings is a small number, and the game hasn't been published yet. What it does show is that people who played it liked it, and so far that has held true each time a new group sat down.
