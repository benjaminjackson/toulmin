Subject: Q3 update: flat revenue, a lost customer, and what we're doing about it

Hi all,

First, I owe you an apology. I didn't send updates for the last two quarters. You should hear how the company is doing on a regular schedule, whether the news is good or bad. From now on you'll get one every quarter.

**The numbers**

- MRR: $41,000, the same as three months ago
- Clinics: 9 added, 4 lost (net +5)
- Cash: $480,000
- Net burn: about $44,000/month, which gives us roughly 11 months of runway (to around October 2027)
- Team: 6

**What happened**

We lost our largest customer: a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500 a month. They moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. Revenue stayed flat because the rest of the business grew by roughly that amount. Without the loss, MRR would have been about $46,500, up 13% for the quarter.

The loss tells us something important. When a clinic's records vendor offers a scheduler that's good enough, being a better scheduler won't always win. We have to be better at something the records vendors don't offer.

**What's working**

Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a no-show rate of 7%, down from 13% before they started. That's about 46% fewer missed appointments. For a PT clinic, every no-show is an empty slot that can't be billed. Of everything we sell, this result is the easiest to measure and the hardest for clinics to give up.

**What we're doing next**

Next month we'll start selling the reminder feature on its own for $150 a month. Clinics that want to cut no-shows won't have to switch schedulers. That includes clinics like the chain we just lost. The product we're selling is the one with proof behind it.

To give you a sense of scale: each 100 standalone reminder customers adds $15,000 in MRR. About 290 would cover our current burn. In next quarter's update I'll report how many we've signed, how long the sale takes, and whether these customers stay.

**Where you can help**

If you know owners or operators of physical therapy clinics, especially groups with several locations, an introduction would help more than anything else right now.

I'm happy to talk through any of this. Just reply or grab time with me.

Thanks for your patience and your support.

Ben

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
