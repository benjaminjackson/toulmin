**Subject:** Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31, and our new member phone and chat center

Team,

I want you to hear this from me directly. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. All 14 people who work at those branches are being offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher. No one is losing their job because of this change.

**Why we're doing this**

Our members have changed how they reach us. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. In the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Our staffing hasn't kept up with that shift, and members feel it. Someone calling us today waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and many of you hear about it from members every day.

**What this means for our 14 colleagues moving to the center**

- Your pay stays the same or goes up.
- The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, which is longer than the branches' 9-to-5 hours, and shifts will rotate. That is a real change to your schedule, and we know it.
- After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- If you decide the new role isn't right for you, you'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

**Why I believe this is better**

For the colleagues who are moving, it means keeping your job, your pay and everything you know about our members, with the option to work from home part of the week. For everyone, it means more of our people answering members where members are actually trying to reach us. Members will get through to us faster, and they'll be less frustrated when they do, whether they then walk into a branch or stay on the phone.

I won't pretend this is easy. Maple Street and Route 9 have served their neighborhoods well, and the people who work there have built real relationships with members. Moving from a counter to a headset, and from 9-to-5 to rotating shifts, is a big change. I'd rather say that plainly than tell you everyone will love it on day one.

If you have questions or concerns, reply to this email or come find me. I want to hear them.

Thank you for everything you do for our members.

Chief Operating Officer
</document>
