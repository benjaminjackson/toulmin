**Subject: Closing Maple Street and Route 9, and opening our member phone and chat center**

Team,

I want you to hear this from me first. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Every one of the 14 people who work at those two branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher.

**Why we're doing this**

Over the last three years, visits to those two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Members are showing us how they want to reach us, and we haven't kept up: a member who calls us today waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give. It's also hard on the colleagues who answer those calls after a member has already waited that long.

This change moves experienced people to where most members now reach us.

**What the center will look like**

The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. That's three more hours a day than our branches, so members can reach us before and after their own working day. To cover those hours, shifts will rotate.

I know rotating shifts are a real change for anyone used to 9 to 5. Some of you have childcare, commutes or other commitments built around those hours, and I won't pretend that part is easy. Here is what comes with the role: after 90 days of training, center staff can work from home two days a week.

**For the 14 of you at Maple Street and Route 9**

Each of you will be offered a role in the center at your current pay or higher. If the new role isn't right for you, you will receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here. Either choice is a fair one, and we will respect whichever you make.

**For everyone**

This is a big change, and I don't think it makes every part of every job easier. What I do believe is that it puts our people where our members are, cuts the time members spend waiting for us, and gives everyone affected a real place in what comes next.

If you have questions or concerns, reply to this email. I would rather hear them now than have you wonder.

Thank you for the care you put into serving our members every day. That's what this change is meant to protect.
