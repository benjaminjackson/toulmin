Good evening, commissioners. I've lived on Alder Road for 19 years, two blocks from the church that's applying tonight. I'm also a member of that church, so you should know that up front. But I'm speaking to you as a neighbor, because neighbors are the ones who will live with this project, traffic and all.

Your own housing report says this county is short about 2,100 affordable homes. Right now, 412 households are on the housing authority's waiting list, and the average wait is three years.

This project is 64 apartments, every one of them for households earning under 60 percent of the area median income. That's enough homes for about one in six of the households on that waiting list. I want to talk about traffic directly, because I drive Alder Road every day and I've read the study. It estimates 310 more car trips a day on Alder, and it says the intersection at Alder and 4th will drop from a C to a D at rush hour. I'm not going to tell you that's nothing.
