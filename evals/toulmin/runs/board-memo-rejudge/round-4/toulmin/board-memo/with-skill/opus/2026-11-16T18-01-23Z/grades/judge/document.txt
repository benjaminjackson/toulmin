**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Replacing display ads with reader membership from January 2027

**Decision requested**
I'm asking the board to approve two things: ending display advertising on 31 December 2026, and launching a paid reader membership program on 1 January 2027.

**Ad revenue is falling fast**
Display ads brought in $131,000 two years ago and $84,000 last year. That's a drop of $47,000, or 36%, in a single year. If the decline continues, ads will bring in between $37,000 (the same dollar drop) and $54,000 (the same percentage drop) in 2027. Keeping ads doesn't keep $84,000. It keeps a shrinking share of it.

**Readers have already shown they will pay**
In our six-week spring pilot, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. Our only promotion was two newsletter mentions. That's about 35 sign-ups a week and 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers. It's also $20,350 a year in revenue, nearly a quarter of last year's ad income, for almost no effort. [X] of those 212 members are still paying today.

**What it takes to replace ads**
To match last year's $84,000, we need 875 members at $8 a month, about 6% of our newsletter list. That means adding 663 members over twelve months, or about 13 a week. That's roughly a third of the pace the pilot reached with no real promotion. We'll have a proper launch campaign, ad-free reading as a member benefit, and a full year to work. With those, I expect membership to replace our ad revenue within a year. [Newsroom name], a newsroom our size in [state], now covers 40% of its budget from membership, so the model can carry well beyond what ads now bring us.

**Risks, and how the board will know**
Two things could slow us down. First, the pilot drew our most committed readers, so later sign-ups may come more slowly. Second, some members will cancel. The 13-a-week target leaves room for both, but the board shouldn't take that on faith, so I propose a checkpoint. By 30 June 2027 we should have at least 545 paying members, worth about $52,000 a year. If we fall short, I'll bring the board a revised budget and options at the July meeting, including local business sponsorship.

**Our ad sales role**
[Name] has sold our advertising part-time for 11 years and knows our local business community better than anyone. I propose offering them [a role in sponsorship and membership outreach]. I'll bring a staffing proposal to the board at [meeting].

**If we don't make this change**
We keep an ad business likely to earn $37,000–$54,000 next year and less after that. And we go into 2027 without the membership program the pilot showed our readers will pay for.
