What I have:

**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Approval to end display advertising and launch reader membership in January 2027

**Request**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising on [December 31, 2026] and launching a reader membership program in January 2027. Our goal is for membership to fully replace our ad revenue on an annual basis within 12 months.

**Why now**
Display ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 over two years, a 36% drop. If the decline continues, ads will bring in roughly $65,000–$70,000 in 2027. We are spending effort to protect a shrinking line of revenue.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week pilot this spring, 212 readers joined at $8 a month, worth $20,352 a year. We promoted it with only two newsletter mentions. That is 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers. [X of the 212 are still paying today.] A newsroom of our size in [state] reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget.

**The target**
Replacing $84,000 takes about 875 members at $8 a month, or 6% of our newsletter list. That means adding about 660 members in 12 months, roughly 13 a week. The pilot averaged 35 a week. We expect growth to slow after our most loyal readers join, and some members will cancel. To leave room for both, our working target is **1,000 members by December 2027**.

**Risks and how we'll manage them**
- **Year-one revenue gap.** Ad revenue stops in January, but membership revenue builds gradually. Even if we hit our target, total 2027 membership revenue will be below $84,000. We will cover the gap with [reserves of $X / bridge funding from Y].
- **Slower growth than planned.** Our checkpoint is [500] members by June 30. If we are below [400], I will come back to the board with options, including a limited sponsorship program.
- **Our colleague.** Our part-time ad sales lead has been with us for 11 years. [Describe plan: transition into a membership/business role, or notice and severance terms.] I have [discussed this with them directly].

**What I'm asking the board to approve**
1. End display ad sales effective [date].
2. Launch membership in January with a marketing budget of [$X].
3. Use [$X from reserves / other source] to cover the 2027 revenue gap.
4. Review progress against the June checkpoint at the [July] board meeting.

I would especially welcome input from board members with sales and advertising experience as we build the membership pitch and sign-up process.

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
