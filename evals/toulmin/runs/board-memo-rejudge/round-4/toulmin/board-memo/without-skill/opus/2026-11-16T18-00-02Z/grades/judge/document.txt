**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Approval to replace display advertising with reader membership, effective January 2027

**Request**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising on [January 1, 2027] and making reader membership our main source of earned revenue. Our goal is to replace current ad revenue within 12 months, with a board review at six months.

**Why now**
Display ad revenue fell from $131,000 to $84,000 over two years. That's a 36% drop, about 20% a year. If the trend continues, ads will bring in roughly $67,000 in 2027. We are putting staff time into defending a line that keeps shrinking.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week pilot this spring, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. We promoted it only twice, in the newsletter. That's 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers and about $20,000 a year in recurring revenue. A newsroom of our size in [state] reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget.

**What success requires**
- Replacing $84,000 takes about 875 members at $8 a month: four times the pilot, or about 6% of our newsletter list.
- Replacing the projected $67,000 takes about 700 members (5%).
- To get there, we will [run a launch campaign in January, add membership asks on the site and in every newsletter, and hold a year-end drive].

**Risks and how we'll manage them**
- **First-year revenue gap.** Ad revenue stops at once, but members sign up gradually. If we grow steadily to 875, membership will bring in about $52,000 in 2027. That's $15,000–$32,000 less than ads would have earned. We will cover the gap with [reserves / bridge funding / other].
- **Slower growth.** Pilot members were our most loyal readers, and later sign-ups may come more slowly. Some members will also cancel.
- **Six-month checkpoint.** I will report member numbers to the board every month. If we have fewer than 550 members by June 30, I will bring options to the board, including sponsorships or bringing back some advertising.
- **Staffing.** [Name], our part-time ad sales lead, has been with us for 11 years. I propose [moving their role to sponsorships and member development / other plan].

**Proposed motion**
The board approves ending display advertising effective [date] and launching a reader membership program, with monthly progress reports and a formal review at the June 2027 board meeting.
