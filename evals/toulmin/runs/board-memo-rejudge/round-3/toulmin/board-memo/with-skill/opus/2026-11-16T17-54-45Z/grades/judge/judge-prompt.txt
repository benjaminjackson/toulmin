<document>
**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** Executive Director
**Date:** 16 November 2026
**Re:** Replacing display ads with reader membership from January 2027

**Decision requested**
Approve ending display advertising on 1 January 2027 and launching a reader membership program in its place.

**Doing nothing is a slow decline**
Display ads brought in $84,000 last year, down from $131,000 two years earlier. That is a 36% fall, or about 20% a year. If the trend holds, ads will bring in roughly $67,000 in 2027 and less after that.

**What the pilot showed**
In six weeks this spring, 212 readers joined at $8 a month. Our only promotion was two mentions in the newsletter. That is about 150 new members a month, or 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers. Those members are worth $20,352 a year.

**What it takes to replace ads**
To match last year's $84,000 we need 875 members at $96 a year, or 6% of our newsletter list. To match what ads are on track to earn in 2027 we need about 700. Growing from 212 to 875 by December 2027 means adding about 55 members a month. That is roughly a third of what the pilot achieved with almost no effort. A newsroom of similar size in another state now gets 40% of its budget from membership, so the model works at our scale.

**My commitment and your checkpoint**
Membership will replace our ad revenue within a year: 875 paying members, worth $84,000 a year, by December 2027. To get there we need at least 540 members by 30 June 2027. If we are below that, I will bring the board a revised plan within 30 days.

**Risks you should weigh**
- *Early joiners are the keenest readers.* Later members will be harder to win, and some will cancel. The plan allows for this because it needs only a third of the pilot's pace.
- *2027 will bring in less cash than a full year of ads.* Members join gradually, so steady growth to 875 brings in about $52,000 from membership in 2027. That is about $15,000 less than ads are likely to earn next year and $32,000 less than they earned last year. This is the cost of switching, and it is concentrated in year one. From 2028, membership income starts the year at the full rate, while ad income would keep falling.

**Our ad sales lead**
Our ad sales lead has sold for us for 11 years and knows our readers and community better than anyone. I intend to offer them a role running member sales and renewals. I will speak with them before any public announcement.

**Recommendation**
Ads are shrinking by about a fifth each year. Membership has already shown it can grow faster than this plan needs. I ask the board to approve the switch from January 2027, with progress reviewed at the 30 June checkpoint.
</document>

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.
