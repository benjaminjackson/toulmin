**Request**
I'm asking the board to approve ending display advertising and launching a reader membership program on January 1.

**Why change now**
Display ads brought in $84,000 last year, down from $131,000 two years ago. That's a 36% drop. If it keeps falling at the same rate, ads will bring in about $67,000 next year, and they'll keep shrinking. We would be spending staff time on a source of income we can't control and that is going away.

**What the pilot showed**
This spring we ran a 6-week membership test at $8 a month. **212 readers signed up**, and our only promotion was two mentions in the newsletter. That's 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers, reached with almost no effort. [Add: number of pilot members still paying today.] A peer newsroom of our size in another state reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget.

Members will join over the course of the year, not all at once. So first-year membership income will be lower than the annual figure.
