**Bring the Majestic Back**

The one screen at the Majestic on Main Street has been dark since 2022. Today, seeing a movie in a theater means a 76-mile round trip.

We can change that by Thanksgiving.

**The deal**

The building's owner has agreed to rent the Majestic to us for $1 a year for ten years. The condition is that we get it ready to open. That takes $85,000 for a new projector, a new sound system and a repaired roof.

A local bank has pledged $25,000 of that. The bank's condition is that the town raises the other $60,000 by June 30.

**Why this time is different**

Some of you backed our campaign three years ago. It reached $47,000 of a $120,000 goal, and we returned every dollar, as we said we would. This campaign works the same way. It's all-or-nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge comes back to you in full.

Three things have changed. The goal is $35,000 lower. A bank has already committed a quarter of it. And there's a ten-year lease waiting for us.

Last time, this town pledged $47,000. This time we need $60,000 from you, which is $13,000 more than you gave before.

**Who will run it**

Forty-six of your neighbors have already signed up to run the box office and the snack bar. In its last days, the Majestic was still selling about 180 tickets a week, so the audience was there right up to the end.

**What we're asking**

Pledge what you can by June 30. If you gave last time, please give again. If you didn't, this is the time. Then tell one person who misses Friday nights on Main Street.

If we reach the goal, the projector goes in, the roof gets fixed, and the lights come back on this Thanksgiving.

Thank you,

Ruth Albers
Chair, the volunteer group working to reopen the Majestic
