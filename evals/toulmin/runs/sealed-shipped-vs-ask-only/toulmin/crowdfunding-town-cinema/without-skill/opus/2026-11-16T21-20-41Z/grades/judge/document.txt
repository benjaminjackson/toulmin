**Bring Back the Majestic**

The Majestic on Main Street closed in 2022. Since then, seeing a film on a big screen has meant a 76-mile round trip, because the nearest cinema is 38 miles away.

We're a group of volunteers working to reopen it. We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and to fix the roof.

**You may remember our last attempt.**
Three years ago we asked for $120,000. We reached $47,000, fell short and refunded every pledge in full. This campaign is built differently:

- **A smaller target:** $85,000, not $120,000.
- **A lease ready to sign:** Once we raise the money, the building's owner will rent us the Majestic for $1 a year for ten years.
- **A bank behind us:** A local bank has pledged $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30, 2027.
- **A head start:** Three local businesses have already pledged $11,000 in writing.
- **People ready to work:** 46 volunteers have signed up to run the box office and the snack bar.

That leaves **$49,000** to raise from the community. Last time, our community pledged $47,000. We're asking you to do it again and go a little further.

**Your money is safe either way.**
The campaign is all-or-nothing. If we don't reach the full $85,000 by June 30, every pledge will be returned in full, as it was last time. If we do reach it, we'll post a public spending report at the town library every three months, so you can see where every dollar went.

**The audience is here.**
When the Majestic closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. That's an audience that has been waiting for a screen.

The campaign runs from May 1 to June 30, 2027. Pledge what you can, tell your neighbours, and help us turn the lights back on at the Majestic.
