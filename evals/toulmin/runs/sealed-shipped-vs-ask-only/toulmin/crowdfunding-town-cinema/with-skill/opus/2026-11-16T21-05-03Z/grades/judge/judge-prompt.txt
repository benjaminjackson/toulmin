**Bring Back the Majestic**

Three years ago, this town pledged $47,000 to reopen the Majestic. It wasn't enough to reach our $120,000 goal, so we returned every dollar. We're asking again because the goal is smaller now, and most of the money has already been pledged.

We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and fix the roof. A local bank has pledged $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30. Three local businesses have pledged $11,000 in writing. That leaves $49,000, just $2,000 more than you pledged last time.

The rest is ready. If we reach the goal, the building's owner will rent the Majestic to us for $1 a year for ten years. 46 volunteers have signed up to run the box office and the snack bar. With rent at $1 a year, what we take in at the door goes into the films and the building.

People will come. When the Majestic closed in 2022, it was selling about 180 tickets a week. Since then, the nearest movie screen has been 38 miles away. For a night out, a first date or a kid's first film, that's a long drive.

There are two things you should know. The roof estimate is from last fall, and the contractor says it could rise by as much as 15%. Also, we can't promise new releases in their opening week, because that's up to the distributors. What we can promise is a public report at the town library every three months showing where every dollar went.

This campaign is all-or-nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge is returned in full. If we do, we plan to reopen the Majestic by Thanksgiving.

If we don't reach it, the screen on Main Street stays dark and the nearest movie stays 38 miles away. If we do, it's ours for the next ten years.

Please pledge by June 30.

Ruth Albers
Chair, on behalf of the volunteers working to reopen the Majestic
</document>

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
