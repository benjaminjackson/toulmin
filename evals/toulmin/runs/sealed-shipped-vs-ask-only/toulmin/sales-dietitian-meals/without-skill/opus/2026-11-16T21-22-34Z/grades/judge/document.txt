# Steady Plate

## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian and delivered every Sunday

Managing type 2 diabetes means thinking about every meal: counting carbs, reading labels, and wondering whether tonight's dinner will push your numbers up. Steady Plate does that planning for you. Ten cooked meals arrive at your door each Sunday, and every one of them is built to fit a diabetes-friendly day.

### What you get

- **10 cooked lunches and dinners a week**, delivered every Sunday within 25 miles of downtown.
- **45 grams of carbohydrate or less in every meal.** You don't have to count anything.
- **Every menu planned by me, Lena Ortiz, RD.** I'm a registered dietitian, and I design each week's meals myself.
- **Choice built in.** Each week you can swap any meal for one of 6 other options. The menu runs on a 4-week cycle, so you won't eat the same week twice in a row.

### What our customers have seen

Fourteen customers shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months with us. **Ten of them saw their A1C go down, by 0.6 points on average.** Three stayed about the same, and one went up.

That's a small group, and everyone's body, medication and routine are different, so I can't promise your A1C will drop. Steady Plate doesn't replace your doctor's advice or your care plan. It makes following that plan easier at mealtimes.

People also tend to stay. We have 88 active customers, and 37 of them have been with us for more than six months.
