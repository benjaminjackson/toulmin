## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian

I'm Lena Ortiz, a registered dietitian, and I plan every menu myself. Before your first delivery, we'll have a 15-minute phone call so I can learn about your routine and what you like to eat.

### What our customers' numbers show

Fourteen customers have shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months. Ten saw their A1C go down, three stayed about the same, and one went up. Among them, the average drop was 0.6 points.
