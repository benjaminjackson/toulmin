# Steady Plate

## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian

Knowing what to eat with type 2 diabetes is one job. Cooking it twice a day, every day, is another. Steady Plate does the second one for you.

Every Sunday we deliver 10 cooked lunches and dinners to your door. Every meal has 45 grams of carbohydrate or less, and I plan every menu myself.

— Lena Ortiz, RD

## What customers have seen

Fourteen customers shared their A1C results with me, once from before they started and again three months later. For 10 of them, A1C went down, by an average of 0.6 points. Three stayed about the same, and one went up.

That's a small group. I don't track whether anyone changed their medication during that time, so I can't tell you how much of the change came from the meals. I can't promise your A1C will drop, and Steady Plate doesn't replace your doctor's advice.

I can promise you'll know what's on your plate: cooked meals, each with 45 grams of carbohydrate or less, planned by a registered dietitian.

Right now, 88 people get our meals each week. Thirty-seven of them have been with us for more than six months.

## How it works

**Talk to me first.** Before your first delivery, you get a 15-minute phone call with me. Ask me anything about the meals before you start.

**Make the week yours.** You can swap any meal for one of 6 other choices. The menu runs on a four-week cycle.

**Get it on Sunday.** We deliver within 25 miles of downtown. Orders close Thursday at noon for that Sunday's delivery.

**Stay only as long as it helps.** There's no contract. Skip a week or cancel at any time.
