# Steady Plate

## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian

You know what you're supposed to eat. The hard part is cooking it twice a day, every day. Steady Plate does the cooking for you. Every Sunday we deliver 10 cooked lunches and dinners within 25 miles of downtown, and each meal has 45 grams of carbohydrate or less.

### I plan every menu myself

I'm Lena Ortiz, a registered dietitian. I plan every Steady Plate menu around one rule: no meal goes over 45 grams of carbohydrate. You always know what you're getting.

### What customers' numbers show

Some customers have shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months. Of the 14 who did, 10 saw their A1C go down, 3 stayed about the same and 1 went up. The average drop was 0.6 points.

Here's what that does and doesn't mean. Fourteen people is a small group. They chose to share their results, and I don't track whether anyone changed their medication during that time. So I can't promise your A1C will go down. What I can promise is that a dietitian plans every meal with blood sugar in mind. These meals don't replace your doctor's advice, so keep working with your care team.

### People stay

We have 88 active customers, and 37 of them have been with us for more than six months.

### You choose what you eat

Each week you can swap any meal for one of 6 other choices. The menu repeats every four weeks, so you won't get bored.

### What it costs

- **Trial week:** $59.
- **Full plan:** 10 meals for $129 a week, about $12.90 a meal, delivered every Sunday.
- **No contract:** skip a week or cancel any time.

Orders close Thursday at noon for that Sunday's delivery.

### Talk to me first if you'd like

Not sure this fits the way you eat or your schedule? I'll do a 15-minute phone call with anyone before they start, so you can ask me anything.

### Try a week

Without a plan, next week looks like this one: you decide, shop and cook for your diabetes on your own, twice a day. For $59 you can see what a week of planned meals is like instead.
