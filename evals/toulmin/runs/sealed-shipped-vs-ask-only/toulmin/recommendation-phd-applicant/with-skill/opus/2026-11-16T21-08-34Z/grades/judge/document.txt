Dear Admissions Committee,

I am writing to recommend Priya Nair for doctoral study in marine ecology. I have taught biology at Westbrook College for 17 years and have supervised about 30 senior theses. Priya is one of the three best students I have taught in that time.

Priya took three of my courses. In two of them she earned the top grade in a class of 28.

Her senior thesis shows best what she would bring to a PhD program. She tracked oyster growth at six sites in our bay for 14 months and did all of the fieldwork herself. That came to about 90 trips by kayak, more than six a month for over a year, in every season. She found faster growth at the two sites nearest the salt marsh. This result comes from a single study that has not yet been repeated, and a storm cost one site three months of data, so I would not call it finished. I do believe it can become a publishable paper with a second season of data. That is the kind of work a doctoral program would let her carry forward. She has no publications yet, and this thesis is the natural place for her first. She presented the study as a poster at a regional conference.

Her work outside my classroom confirms what I saw in it. Last summer she was a paid field assistant at a state shellfish lab, where she ran the lab's water sampling on her own for two weeks. Her supervisor there told me he would hire her again.

A PhD in marine ecology takes years of fieldwork, much of it done alone, in bad weather, with no quick result to keep a student going. Priya has already kept up a 14-month field study on her own, and a working lab trusted her to run its sampling unsupervised. I recommend her without reservation.

Sincerely,

Dr. Anne Caldwell
Westbrook College
