Dear Admissions Committee,

I am writing in strong support of Priya's application to your PhD program in marine ecology. I have taught biology at Westbrook College for 17 years and supervised about 30 senior theses. Priya is one of the three best students I have taught in that time.

Priya took three courses with me and earned the top grade in two of them, in classes of 28 students. Her grades are not what sets her apart, though. What sets her apart is what she did once the work moved outside the classroom.

For her senior thesis, Priya compared oyster growth at six sites in our bay over 14 months. She did all the fieldwork herself, about 90 trips by kayak through every season, including the winter months when most students would have found a reason to stay ashore. She found faster growth at the two sites nearest the salt marsh. This is an interesting result, and it fits a question the field is actively working on. It also comes from a single season, and one site lost three months of data after a storm. In my judgment, the work is publishable with a second season of data, and Priya has the persistence to collect it. She presented the project as a poster at a regional conference, where she held her own in discussion with working scientists.

Last summer Priya worked as a paid field assistant at a state shellfish lab. For two weeks she ran the lab's water sampling program on her own. Her supervisor told me he would hire her again. That matches what I have seen myself. Once Priya has been shown how something should be done, she can be trusted to do it carefully, and without anyone watching.

Most students who come to me interested in field ecology like the idea of it. Priya has already done the work, and done it in difficult conditions over a long period. She has kept going on a project after the novelty wore off, which is what a PhD will ask of her. She is ready for graduate research, and I recommend her without reservation.

Please feel free to contact me if I can tell you more about her.

Sincerely,

Dr. Anne Caldwell
