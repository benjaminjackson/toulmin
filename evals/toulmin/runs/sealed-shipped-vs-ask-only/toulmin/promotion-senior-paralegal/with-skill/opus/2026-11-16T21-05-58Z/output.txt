Subject: Request for promotion to Senior Paralegal

Dear David,

I'm writing to ask to be considered for promotion to senior paralegal in the January review cycle.

I've been a paralegal in the litigation group for five years. Last year I supported four lawyers on 23 cases, two of which went to trial, and billed 1,640 hours against the group's target of 1,550.

Much of the senior role is supervising other paralegals and keeping the group's systems reliable. I'm already doing a good part of both.

Training. I trained all three paralegals the group has hired in the last two years. Two are still with us. One left after eight months.

Systems. In 2023 I built the group's tracking sheet for court deadlines. Two other groups in the firm now use it.

That sheet is also where I made a serious mistake. Last March I entered a filing deadline wrong. Another paralegal caught it, and we filed two hours before the court's cutoff. That was too close, and I didn't treat it as a one-off. In April I added a second-person check to the sheet. Since then it has had no wrong or missed deadlines across about 600 entries. When something goes wrong, I change the process so it doesn't depend on one person getting it right, including me. That is the approach I would bring to supervising the group's paralegals.

I know the senior role carries $9,000 more a year and responsibility for the other paralegals. I'm asking for both. The training and the deadline system will need someone responsible for them either way, and I'd like to take that on formally.

If the timing isn't right this cycle, I'd value hearing what you would want to see from me before the next one.

I'd be glad to talk before the review if that would help.

Thank you for considering this.

Best regards,
Jenna Morales
</final_document>

Some notes on what's in the email and what's left out:

- **The March error** comes right after the tracker, along with your fix and the 600 entries with no errors since. Those numbers are the best answer you have to it. I didn't say the check caught any particular error, because you don't know whether it did.
- **The paralegal who left** is mentioned once, next to the fact that the other two are still with the group. I didn't give a reason they left because you didn't give one.
- **Whether there's an opening:** since you don't know, the email asks to be considered in the January cycle rather than asking for a specific open position.
- **Worth doing before you send:** if one of your four lawyers would support you, ask them before December 15. A line like "[Lawyer] has offered to speak to my work" would be the strongest addition you could make. I left it out because you haven't confirmed it yet.
