Subject: Request for promotion to senior paralegal

Dear David,

I'm asking to be considered for promotion to senior paralegal in the January review cycle. I've spent five years in the litigation group. Over the last two, I've done much of the training and process work that the senior role involves, and I'd like to take it on formally, along with supervising the group's paralegals.

In 2023 I built the group's tracking sheet for court deadlines. Two other groups in the firm have since adopted it.

I made one serious mistake in that sheet. Last March I entered a filing deadline incorrectly. Another paralegal caught it, and we filed two hours before the court's cutoff. That was too close. In April I added a second-person check to every entry. Since then the sheet has logged about 600 deadlines, and none has been wrong or missed. I think that response is part of the case for this promotion. A senior paralegal is responsible for the systems the rest of the team relies on. As senior paralegal, I would supervise the group's paralegals and keep building on the training and deadline-control work I already do. I understand the role carries a $9,000 increase, and I believe the work described above has earned it.

Thank you for considering it.

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
