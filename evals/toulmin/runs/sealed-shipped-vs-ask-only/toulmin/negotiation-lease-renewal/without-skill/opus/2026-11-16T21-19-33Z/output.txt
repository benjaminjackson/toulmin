Here's the reply. It's about 250 words, and it works as either an email or a printed letter.

<final_document>
Dear Frank,

Thank you for letting me know your plans for the new lease. I've thought carefully about $5,650 a month, and I have to be honest: I can't pay that and keep Pho Lan open.

What I can offer is $5,100 a month on a new five-year lease, with 3% increases each year after the first. By the fifth year that comes to about $5,740 a month, so over the term you would get close to your number from a tenant you already know.

We've been in your building for ten years, over two leases. In 2019 we put about $70,000 into the kitchen, including the new hood system, which stays with the building.

I know two payments were late in early 2024. Our walk-in cooler failed and we had to close for nine days. We've paid on time every month since, and I'm grateful that you waived the late fees.

I've also seen that the unit two doors down has been for lease for 14 months at $28 a square foot. I mention it only because steady rent from a long-term tenant seems worth something right now.

Since the lease ends in August, could you give me your answer by June 1? That leaves me time to plan for the restaurant and the staff. I'm happy to meet in person if that's easier.

Thank you for ten good years. I hope we can agree on the next five.

Best regards,
Pho Lan
</final_document>

A few things to know before you send it:

- **The fifth-year figure:** With 3% raises each year, your rent goes $5,100, then $5,253, $5,411, $5,573 and finally $5,740. So in the last year you'd be paying more than his $5,650. That helps your case with him, but make sure you can afford it by then.
- **The empty unit:** The line about the empty unit is meant to stay soft. It doesn't say or suggest that you'll move, since you haven't decided.
- **The sign-off:** It's signed only "Pho Lan," as you asked. You could add your own name above it so it reads as coming from you personally.
