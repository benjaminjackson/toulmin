Subject: Lease renewal for Pho Lan

Dear Frank,

Thank you for sending the renewal terms. We've been in your building for ten years and two leases, and we'd like to stay for five more. I want to make you an offer that works for both of us.

I know two payments were late in early 2024, when our cooler failed and we were closed for nine days. Thank you for waiving the fees. We've paid on time every month since.

I've thought hard about $5,650, and I can't pay it and keep the restaurant open. Here is what I can do:

- $5,100 a month for the first year, going up 3% each year after that, to about $5,740 in year five
- A 5-year lease, signed within two weeks of agreeing on terms
- Rent paid by automatic bank transfer on the 1st of every month

There are a few things I hope you'll weigh. In 2019 we put about $70,000 of our own money into the kitchen, including the hood system, which stays with the building. The unit two doors down has been listed at $28 a square foot for 14 months, and your figure comes to about $29 for our space. I also understand a coffee chain has asked about the space. What we offer is a tenant who has been here ten years and will stay five more, with rent that arrives on the 1st without anyone having to ask.

Could you let me know what you think before June 1? I'd be glad to meet and talk it through.

Best regards,
Pho Lan
</final_document>

A few notes:
- **Signature:** It's signed "Pho Lan" as you asked. You could add your own name above it so it reads as coming from you personally.
- **Year five:** I added the year-five figure (about $5,740) because it shows Frank your offer keeps rising over the lease.
- **What I left out:** The letter doesn't offer a personal guarantee, and it doesn't say whether you'd look at other spaces. You weren't sure about either, so it stays quiet on both.
- **Check your lease first:** Before you send this, see whether your current lease has a renewal option or a notice deadline. If it has one, it could change your timing or give you more leverage.
