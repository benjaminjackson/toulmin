Subject: Three office days a week from September 1, 2027

Everyone,

From September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays. That includes the three partners. This email explains why we're making the change, what it means for those of you it affects most, and what help we're offering. We'll talk it through together at Friday's all-staff meeting.

**Why we're doing this**

Last year we reached the final interview for two large hospital projects and lost both. Both clients gave us the same reason: our team seemed not to know each other. A client hiring for a hospital is choosing a team to work with for years, and they could tell we didn't yet work as one.

We're seeing the same thing inside the firm. Our review records show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point of leading a project. That isn't their fault. People learn to lead projects by working next to people who already do it: hearing how a client call is handled, getting pulled into a markup, asking a quick question instead of saving it for a meeting. Working remotely has meant much less of that.

**The partners too**

For the past few years the partners have come in one day a week. That's been part of the problem, so we're changing it. We'll be in all three days, the same as everyone else.

**If you were hired as fully remote**

Fifteen of you joined in 2021 and 2022 as fully remote. You took the job on those terms, and this changes them. I know that's a real cost to some of you, and I won't pretend it isn't.

Four of you live more than two hours from the office. You can keep working remotely until the end of 2027. From January 2028, we'll ask you to come in one day a week.

Apart from those four, we aren't able to make individual exceptions. I would rather tell you that plainly now than leave you guessing.

**Help with commuting**

From September 1, we'll pay up to $150 a month toward commuting costs for anyone who lives more than 30 miles from the office.

**What's fixed and what's open**

The three days are fixed. Six months after we start, we'll review with you how the policy is working.

If you have questions or concerns, please come to me or to HR before Friday. If this is going to be hard for you, I would rather hear it now.

Paul Whitaker
