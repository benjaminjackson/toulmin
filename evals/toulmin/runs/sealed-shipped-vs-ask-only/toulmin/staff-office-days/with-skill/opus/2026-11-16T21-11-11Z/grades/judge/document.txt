**Subject: Working in the studio three days a week from 1 September 2027**

Everyone,

From 1 September 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. This means fully remote working is ending. I want to explain why, set out what it means for you, and give you time to think it over before we meet on Friday at 10 a.m. in the main studio.

**Why we're doing this**

Last year we lost two large hospital projects at the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. Our work was strong enough to get us to the final round, and we lost there because of how we came across as a team.

Our review records also show that junior staff who have joined since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point of leading a project. We can't prove that remote working is the whole cause. But a lot of how architects learn happens in the room: overhearing a client call, getting pulled into a review, watching a more experienced colleague work through a detail. That is hard to get through a screen.

The partners haven't set the right example. We've been coming in one day a week. From September, all three of us will be in on all three days. Five of our six project leads already come in three days a week on their own.

**What this means for you**

- From 1 September 2027, everyone works in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.
- If you live more than 30 miles from the office, the firm will pay up to $150 a month toward your commuting costs.
- Fifteen of you were hired as fully remote in 2021 and 2022. For you, this changes the terms you joined on, and I know that is a lot to ask. The four of you who live more than two hours away will stay fully remote until 31 December 2027. From January 2028, you'll come in one day a week. Apart from these four, the policy applies to everyone.
- We don't have enough desks for everyone today. New desks arrive in August, before the start date.
- We haven't yet decided how Mondays and Fridays will work. We'll confirm that before September.
- Six months after the start, we'll review the policy together with you.

We haven't yet settled how the office days will be organised, and we'd like to hear your ideas on Friday.

If you have questions before the meeting, please come to me or to HR.

Paul Whitaker
On behalf of the partners
