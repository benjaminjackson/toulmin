Good evening, Commissioners. My name is [NAME]. I've run a charter boat out of [HARBOR] for 22 years. I'm one of 11 charter boats there.

I'm not here to tell you the stock is fine. Your staff says flounder are at 78% of target, and I want them around for the next 22 years too. I'm asking you to get there without cutting the limit to two.

Charter boats land about 9% of this state's summer flounder. Private boats land about 80%. For a private angler, two fish means a smaller day. For us, it decides whether the customer books at all.

So I'm asking for either of two things: a three-fish limit, or a shorter season. Either one protects the stock without putting charter boats through three years like the one I had after 2016.
