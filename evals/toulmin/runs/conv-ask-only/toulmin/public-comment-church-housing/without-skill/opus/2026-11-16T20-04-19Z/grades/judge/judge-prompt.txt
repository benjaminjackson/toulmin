Good evening, Commissioners. My name is [NAME], and I've lived on Alder Road for 19 years, two blocks from [CHURCH NAME]. I'm here to support the 64 affordable apartments planned for the church's back lot.

Right now, 412 households are on the county housing authority's waiting list, and the average wait is three years. That's three years of families paying rent they can't afford, doubling up with relatives, or moving away from their jobs, their kids' schools, and their churches.

But every one of these 64 apartments would rent to a household earning under 60 percent of the area median income. These are the people on that waiting list.
