**Subject: Closing Maple Street and Route 9, and opening our member phone and chat center**

Team,

I want you to hear this from me directly. On March 31, we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Every one of the 14 people who work at those branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center at the main office on Elm Avenue.

**Why we're making this change**

Our members have changed how they reach us. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%, and phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Today, a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and we can't fix it by keeping staff in branches that fewer members visit.

The center will be open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. That's three more hours a day than our branches are open. Members will be able to reach a person before and after their own workday, and the people answering will be colleagues who already know our members.

**For staff at Maple Street and Route 9**

- **Pay:** No one will earn less than they do today. Pay in the center starts at $1.50 an hour above current teller pay.
- **Working from home:** After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **Shifts:** Shifts will rotate across the 8-to-8 schedule. I can't promise anyone a 9-to-5 schedule. When shifts are chosen, the people who have worked here longest choose first.
- **Timeline:** Written offers will go out by February 14. Your decision is due March 1.
- **If you decide not to join:** You'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.
- **If you join and it isn't right for you:** If you leave within your first 6 months in the center, you'll still receive the same severance.
- **Talking it through:** Anyone who wants a one-on-one meeting with HR can have one. Please contact [HR contact] to set one up.

I know this is a big change, especially for people who have built their days around a branch schedule and the members they see there. Different hours won't suit everyone, and I won't pretend otherwise. I do believe this center will serve our members better. For the people who join it, it means higher pay, two days a week at home, and a real role in fixing one of the biggest problems our members have with us.

We'll share the center's opening date and the training schedule as soon as they're set. Until then, please bring your questions to me or to HR.

Thank you for everything you do for our members.

[Your name]
Chief Operating Officer
