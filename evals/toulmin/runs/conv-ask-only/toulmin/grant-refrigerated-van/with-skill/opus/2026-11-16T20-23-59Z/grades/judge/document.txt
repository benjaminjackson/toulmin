[Food Bank Name] is asking the [County] Community Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will cover the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves.

**Who we serve**

Every month our volunteers hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households in our town of about 9,000 people. Only about 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs.

**The problem**

Fresh food is available to us, but we have no way to collect it. Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We had to turn all of it down because we had no way to pick it up and keep it cold.

**What the van will do**

A refrigerated van will let us collect perishable donations directly from farms and keep them cold on the way back. Two of the farms that offered produce last summer have written letters saying they will give again once we can pick up cold. Those letters are attached.

**What we expect in the first year**

Our goal is to bring in the roughly 9,000 pounds of produce we had to refuse last year. That would raise the fresh food we hand out from about 72,000 pounds a year to about 81,000, an increase of about one-eighth. We based this goal on last season's offers. The farms have not promised a specific amount, and harvests vary. Whatever we bring in, we will report it to you honestly. If we fall short, we will explain why.

**Who will run it**

We are entirely volunteer-run, with 23 regular volunteers. Four of them have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule.

**Cost and sustainability**

The van costs $68,000. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000, and the remaining $18,000 will come from our reserves. We estimate insurance and fuel at about $4,500 a year. [How this will be paid.]

**Our record with the Foundation**

In 2023 the Foundation granted us $12,000 for shelving. Our final report on that grant was four months late. Our treasurer at the time was in the hospital for most of that period, and we should have had someone ready to step in.

For this grant, our treasurer, Dave Lin, will be responsible for the final report and will submit it within 30 days of the end of the grant year. We will also send the Foundation our fresh food totals every quarter, so you can follow the van's results throughout the year.

**Why this matters**

Local farms are already offering us fresh food. With this van, we will be able to say yes when a farm calls.
