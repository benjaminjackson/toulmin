I couldn't make "every other publisher pays at least $12,000" true with only one quote, so the email leans on what you can prove. Another publisher paid you $12,500, and your agent says $11,000–$13,000 is normal. The email adds that $11,500 is still below what you've been paid elsewhere. That makes your ask sound modest without claiming anything you can't support.

<final_document>
Subject: The Night Garden – fee

Hi Nadia,

Thank you for offering me The Night Garden. I'd love to illustrate it, and after six books together I'm glad you thought of me.

Before I say yes, I need to talk about the fee. I've been paid $9,000 per book since our first project four years ago, and our books have done well since then. Two of them have sold more than 20,000 copies, and one was picked for a state reading list.

My fees outside our work together have gone up with that record. Last year another publisher paid me $12,500 for a 32-page book of the same scope. My agent tells me $11,000 to $13,000 is the standard range for illustrators with my sales history. Each of these books takes me about four months, and at $9,000 I can no longer set aside that much time.

For The Night Garden I'm asking $11,500. That's still below what I've been paid elsewhere, which shows how much I value working with you and the team.

I'd really like to make this work. Could you take this to whoever needs to approve it, or let me know a good time to talk it through?

Best,
[Your name]
</final_document>

Some notes:
- **Tone:** It's warm but businesslike. That works for a managing editor you've worked with for four years.
- **Your $11,000 minimum** isn't in the email. The line "at $9,000 I can no longer set aside that much time" tells Nadia the old fee won't work, but you still have room to negotiate. If she offers $11,000, you can accept it.
- **Sales:** You don't know if she's seen the numbers, so the email states them as plain facts. If you find the titles before sending, name them in the second paragraph. That makes the point land harder.
- **Your agent:** If they're handling this deal, check with them before sending. They may want to send it, or they may not want their range quoted.
