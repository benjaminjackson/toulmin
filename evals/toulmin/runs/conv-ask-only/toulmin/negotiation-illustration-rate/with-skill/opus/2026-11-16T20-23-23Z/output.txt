- **I didn't use "every other publisher pays at least $12,000."** You've only seen one other publisher's fee, so the sentence would be false. Nadia is a managing editor and probably knows what other publishers pay, so she'd likely see through it. The strongest true version is "the going rate for this work is now well above $9,000." Both your $12,500 fee and the bottom of your agent's range back that up, and the two numbers that follow it are specific and can be checked.
- **I left out your $11,000 floor and the gap in your schedule.** If she counters, you can come down to $11,000. If you'd like to push harder, you could add "I'm not able to take it on at $9,000." It's true, but once you've said it you have to turn the book down if she refuses.
