**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

We ask the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will pay the remaining $18,000 from our reserves. The van will let us collect fresh produce that local farms offered us last summer and that we had to turn down.

**Our last grant report**

The Foundation granted us $12,000 for shelving in 2023. Our final report on that grant was four months late. For this grant, I will be personally responsible for reporting, and we commit to:

- a one-page report of pounds collected within 30 days of the end of each quarter;
- a final report within 30 days of the grant's first anniversary;
- telling your program officer before the due date if any report will be late.

**The need**

We are a volunteer-run food bank in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households, roughly 62 pounds per household. Only about 6,000 pounds of that, around one pound in six, is fresh produce, milk and eggs. That comes to about 10 pounds per household per month.

More fresh food is available to us. Last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of produce over three months. We turned it all down because we had no way to pick it up cold or store it. Without a refrigerated van, we will have to turn down offers like that again next summer.

**What the van will change**

Last summer's offers came to about 3,000 pounds a month. If we had been able to collect them, the fresh food we handed out in those months would have risen from about 6,000 to about 9,000 pounds. That is half again as much, or about 5 more pounds of fresh food per household each month.

Our first-year target is to collect at least 9,000 pounds of farm produce, the amount we turned down last summer, by September 30, 2027. We will report the pounds collected each quarter. If we fall short of the target, our report will say by how much, why, and what we are changing.

**Who will run it**

We have 23 regular volunteers. Four of them have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule.

**The funding**

The van costs $68,000. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000, about 74% of the cost. We will cover the other $18,000, more than a quarter of the cost, from our own reserves. For an organization our size that is a real commitment, because it leaves us $11,000 in reserve. We are making it because farms have already offered us this food, and a refrigerated van is the only way we can accept it.
