**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

Last summer, local farms offered us about 9,000 pounds of fresh produce over three months. We turned all of it down because we had no refrigerated vehicle to collect it. A refrigerated van would let us say yes.

Before we make our request, there is something we owe you. The Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving in 2023, and our final report on that grant was four months late. That was our failure. For this grant we will submit every report by its due date. We will also send you a short update each quarter showing how many pounds of fresh food the van has brought in, so you can see results as they come rather than waiting for a final report.

**Our request**

We ask the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will pay the remaining $18,000 from our reserves. That is more than half of what we hold, and it leaves us $11,000. We are willing to commit that much because we know the food is there.

**Who we serve**

We are a volunteer-run food bank in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. Only about 6,000 pounds of that, roughly 16%, is fresh produce, milk and eggs. Fresh food is the hardest for us to get because it has to stay cold from pickup to distribution.

**What the van changes**

The fresh food is already being offered to us. Last summer the farms had it and we couldn't collect it. With the van, our first goal is to take every pound we were turning away. During the summer harvest months, that means about 3,000 more pounds of fresh food each month. Our fresh distribution in those months would rise by half, from about 6,000 to about 9,000 pounds. For each household, that is about five more pounds of fresh food a month.

**Our target, which you can hold us to:** at least 9,000 pounds of donated farm produce collected by the van between June and August 2027. Our quarterly updates will report the actual figure. If we fall short, the update will say so, explain why, and set out what we are changing.

**Who will run it**

We have 23 regular volunteers. Four of them have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so the route doesn't depend on any one person. The farms that called us last summer are the first pickups we will schedule.

**If we cannot buy the van**

Without refrigerated transport, we expect to turn down next summer's offers just as we did last summer. That would be thousands of pounds of fresh food grown a few miles from the families who need it, left unused because we couldn't carry it home cold. With the Foundation's help, that food will reach 610 households instead.

Thank you for considering our request, and for your support in 2023.
