**Project Narrative: Refrigerated Van**

Over three months last summer, local farms offered us roughly 9,000 pounds of fresh produce. We had to turn all of it down because we had no way to pick it up cold or store it. We are asking the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van so that next summer we can say yes.

First, something your records will show. In 2023 the Foundation gave us $12,000 for shelving, and our final report on that grant was four months late. That was our responsibility, and we fell short. For this grant, we will send you a short update at the end of each month from June through September 2027 showing the pounds of farm produce collected. We will send our final report by October 31, 2027.

**Who we serve**

We are a volunteer-run food bank in a town of about 9,000 people. Each month we hand out about 38,000 pounds of food to 610 households. Only about 6,000 pounds of that is fresh produce, milk and eggs. That is roughly one pound in six, or about 10 pounds per household per month.

**What the van changes**

The produce we turned down came to about 3,000 pounds a month across those three summer months. If we had been able to accept it, the fresh food we hand out in those months would have risen by half, from about 6,000 pounds to about 9,000. For each household, that is about 15 pounds of fresh food a month instead of 10. Across a full year, those 9,000 pounds would add about one-eighth to the fresh food we provide now.

**Our first-year goal**

By September 30, 2027, we will collect at least 9,000 pounds of farm produce that we cannot accept today. We will report the actual total to you by October 31, whether or not we reach the goal. If we fall short, we will tell you why.

**Who will run it**

We have 23 regular volunteers. Four of them have committed to driving the van on a weekly schedule, so the van does not depend on any one person.

**Our share of the cost**

We will pay the remaining $18,000 from our own reserves, which will leave us with $11,000. That is most of what we have saved. We are willing to spend it because otherwise we will have to turn down the same food again. Your $50,000 would cover about three-quarters of the van's cost.

**If the van is not funded**

Without a refrigerated vehicle, nothing changes next summer. When local farms offer fresh produce, we will have to refuse it again, and 610 households will go without it.

**Our request**

We ask the Foundation for $50,000 toward a $68,000 refrigerated van. We will report on it monthly during the 2027 growing season and send our final report by October 31, 2027.
