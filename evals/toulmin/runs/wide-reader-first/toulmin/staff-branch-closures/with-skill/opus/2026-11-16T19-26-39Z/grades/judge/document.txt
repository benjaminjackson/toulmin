**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: a new role for every team member**

Team,

I want you to hear this from me first. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Every one of the 14 people who work at those two branches will be offered a job in our new member phone and chat center, at the same pay or higher. Our other five branches are not changing.

**Why we're doing this**

Our members have changed how they reach us. Over the last three years, visits to Maple Street and Route 9 fell 44%. In the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Today a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give. We can fix it by putting experienced people where members are actually trying to reach us.

**What changes for the Maple Street and Route 9 teams**

- **Pay:** the same as now or higher.
- **Hours:** the center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. Branches were open 9 to 5, so some of your shifts will start earlier or end later than you're used to. I know this affects childcare, commutes and second jobs. Please tell us about your situation before schedules are set.
- **Working from home:** after 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **If you decide not to take the role:** you'll receive 2 weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

**What happens next**

Your manager and someone from HR will meet with each of you one-on-one by [date]. You'll have until [date] to tell us your decision. Until then, please bring any questions to [name/contact].

**What this means for everyone else**

When members wait 9 minutes on hold, many of them give up and walk into your branch, often frustrated. A center staffed 12 hours a day is how we bring that wait down. Our goal is [target] minutes by [date]. I'll report where we stand in an email to all staff on that date, whether we've hit it or not.

I won't pretend this is easy. Many of you at Maple Street and Route 9 have spent years with members who come to you by name. You'll still be serving those members, and many more, through a different door. That knowledge is exactly why we want you in this role.

If you'd like to talk with me directly, my door is open.

[Your name]
Chief Operating Officer
