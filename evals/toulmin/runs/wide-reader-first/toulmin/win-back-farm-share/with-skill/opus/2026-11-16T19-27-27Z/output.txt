A few things to check before you send it:

- **Fill in the placeholders:** the first name, your sign-up link and your name.
- **Consider offering a goodwill credit.** These members probably left mainly because they never got anything back for the short boxes. A full share is $540 for 22 weeks, which works out to about $24.55 a week. Half of that for 6 weeks is about $74 each person didn't get. A credit for returning members, even part of that amount, would fix the problem itself rather than only apologizing for it. I left it out because you didn't mention offering one. If you do, put it right after the apology.
- **I didn't overstate what the changes protect.** The nets only cover tomatoes and peppers, and I didn't say the new field protects against hail, because nothing you told me says it does. If the higher ground does help, for example with drainage or flooding, add one line saying how.
