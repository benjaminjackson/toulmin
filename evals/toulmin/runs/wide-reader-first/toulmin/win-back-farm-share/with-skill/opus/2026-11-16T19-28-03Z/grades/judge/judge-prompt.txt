You are the reader described between the <reader> tags. Read the document between the <document> tags as that reader would receive it.

The brief between the <brief> tags is what the writer was asked to do and shows the facts the writer had. You, as the reader, have not seen the brief. Use it only to check what the document does with those facts.

<reader>
A household that paid $540 for last year's vegetable share and cancelled after a July hailstorm left the boxes thin for six weeks with no refund or credit. They felt the farm went quiet when things went wrong. They decide whether to sign up for next season.
</reader>

<brief>
Can you write an email to people who didn't renew their farm share? I run a 30-acre vegetable farm and sell 22-week shares that people pick up every Thursday.

Facts:
- Last year 260 households had a share. 97 didn't renew.
- On July 9 a hailstorm destroyed about half our crops. For the next 6 weeks the boxes had about half the usual amount. We didn't offer refunds or credit, and I sent one short email about it.
- A full share is $540 this year, the same as last year.
- This year we've added a second field on higher ground and put up 2 hail nets over our tomatoes and peppers. That cost $31,000.
- Members can now pause up to 2 weeks and get credit for them.
- Sign-up closes March 15. 52 shares are left.

Under 200 words.
</brief>



<document>
Subject: What we got wrong last summer, and what's changed

Hi [first name],

You didn't renew your share this year, and I think I understand why. After the July 9 hailstorm, your box held about half the usual amount for six weeks. That's roughly three weeks of produce you paid for and didn't get. We offered no refund or credit, and I sent one short email about it. You deserved better on both counts. I'm sorry.

Here's what's different this year:

- **Hail nets** now cover our tomatoes and peppers.
- **A second field** on higher ground means our crops aren't all growing in one place. The nets and the new field cost us $31,000.
- **Pause with credit:** you can pause up to 2 weeks and get credit for them.
- **Same price:** a full share is still $540 for 22 weeks of Thursday pickups.

No farm can control the weather. We spent this winter making the farm harder to knock out.

Sign-up closes **March 15**, and **52 shares** are left. If you'd like yours back, you can sign up here: [sign-up link]

Thanks for being with us last season,

[Your name]
</document>

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
