**Keep the light burning. Keep each other going.**

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You are a lighthouse keeper's family, and your job is to get through one winter together. The light has to stay lit, and everyone has to make it to spring. You win together or not at all, and it works just as well solo on a quiet night as it does with four people around the table.

**$49, shipping included in the US.** That's one pledge level and one copy of the game, delivered to your door.

**Who's making it**

I'm a land surveyor and I still work full time. This is the first game I've published. I'm telling you that up front because it's the first thing I'd want to know as a backer. It's also why the rest of this page gives you numbers instead of promises.

**How it's been tested**

Since 2023 the game has been played 140 times by about 60 different people. I took it to two conventions and watched strangers sit down with it. On BoardGameGeek, 23 players have rated it, with an average of 8.1 out of 10.

That's a small group, and I won't pretend this is a hit yet. What it does show is that the game has been played often, by people who didn't owe me anything, and that they liked it. Since then, 1,240 people have signed up to hear about the launch. If you're one of them, thank you.

**Where your $49 goes**

I have a factory quote for 1,500 copies at $17,400. That's $11.60 a copy. Shipping each copy to a backer costs about $9. So about $20.60 of every pledge goes to making your game and getting it to you.

The goal is **$28,000**, which is about 572 backers. It's set so that it pays for the whole print run even if we only just reach it. At the goal, printing and shipping come to about $22,550. The remaining $5,450 or so covers platform and payment fees, with a cushion for costs that go up. I set the goal so that funding at the minimum still means your copy gets made.

**What I'll commit to**

On the 1st of every month, from the end of the campaign until your copy ships, I'll post an update. It will cover where production stands, what the factory has sent me, and anything that has gone wrong. When something slips, you'll hear it from me in that update, not find out from a quiet page.

**Why I made it**

I spend my working days measuring coastlines and property lines. I wanted to make a game about the people who keep watch at the edge of the map: a family, a light, and a long dark season they have to get through by relying on each other. Cooperative games are at their best when the table is talking, planning and occasionally groaning together, and that's what playtesters kept telling me happened at their tables.

**Back it at $49 today.**

If you're not ready to pledge, click Follow so you hear how the campaign goes. Either way, thank you for reading this far.

2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.

7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.
