**Is it any good?**

I've been building this game since 2023. In that time I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. That means about 60 strangers, friends and repeat players telling me what dragged, what broke and what made them lean over the table. I took it to two conventions. On the main board game ratings site, the 23 people who have rated it give it an average of 8.1.

Twenty-three is not a big number, and I won't pretend it is. But those 23 people sat down with a prototype, played it through, and came away rating it that highly. Everything on this page is about turning that prototype into a box on your shelf.
