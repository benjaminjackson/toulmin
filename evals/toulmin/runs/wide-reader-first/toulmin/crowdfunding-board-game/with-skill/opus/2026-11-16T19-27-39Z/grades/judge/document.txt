**Keep the light burning. Keep each other going.**

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You are a lighthouse keeper's family, and your job is to get through one winter together. The light has to stay lit, and everyone has to make it to spring. You win together or not at all, and it works just as well solo on a quiet night as it does with four people around the table.

**Why I made it**

I spend my working days measuring coastlines and property lines. I wanted to make a game about the people who keep watch at the edge of the map: a family, a light, and a long dark season they have to get through by relying on each other. Cooperative games are at their best when the table is talking, planning and occasionally groaning together, and that's what playtesters kept telling me happened at their tables.
