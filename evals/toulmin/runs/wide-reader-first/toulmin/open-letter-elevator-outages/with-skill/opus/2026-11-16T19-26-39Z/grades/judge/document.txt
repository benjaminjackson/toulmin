**An open letter to [Management Company] from the [Building] Tenants' Association**

Our building's only elevator has been out of service on 41 days this year. That is about one day in every eight. We are asking you for three answers in writing by December 1.

Our building has nine stories, 112 apartments and one elevator. When the elevator stops, many of our neighbours are stuck. Twenty-three residents are over 75. Six use wheelchairs or walkers. For someone on an upper floor who uses a walker, an outage can mean being unable to leave home. That means no appointments, no shopping and no way out without help. In August, the elevator was out for 12 days in a row.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and gave it a passing certificate. We are glad it is safe when it runs. Our problem is how often it does not run.

In June, you told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. Five months later, you are telling us the same thing: six to eight weeks.

Seventy-four of our 112 households, two in every three, signed a petition asking for a second elevator. You have told us it would cost about $400,000 and cannot be done in this building. We are not asking you to change that answer today. We are asking you to show us the reasons behind it.

**What we are asking for, in writing, by December 1:**

1. **The part.** The date the controller part was ordered, the delivery date the supplier has confirmed, and what you will do if that date is missed.

2. **A plan for outages.** A written plan for residents who cannot use the stairs, in place before the next outage. It should say how residents will be told about an outage and how they will get in and out of the building while it lasts.

3. **The second elevator.** The engineering assessment behind your statement that a second elevator cannot be built here, shared with the association. If it truly cannot be done, tell us what you will do instead to keep the one elevator we have running.

We would welcome a meeting with you before December 1 to go through these answers together. When you reply, we will post your answer in the lobby next to this letter, so every resident can read it.

We want the same thing you should want: an elevator our neighbours can rely on.

Sincerely,

[Name]
Secretary, on behalf of the [Building] Tenants' Association
