**An Open Letter to [Management Company] from the Tenants of [Building Name]**

So far this year, our elevator has been out of service on 41 days. That is roughly one day in every eight.

We are writing to ask for three things. We would like a written reply within 14 days:

1. **A firm installation date** for the new controller part, with confirmation from the supplier that it has been ordered.
2. **A plan for residents who cannot use the stairs**, covering every day the elevator is out of service until the repair is done.
3. **Your reasons in writing** for saying that a second elevator cannot be built in this building.

**Why this matters**

Our building has 9 stories, 112 apartments and one elevator. When the elevator stops, everyone who lives above the ground floor has to use the stairs. Twenty-three of our neighbours are over 75. Six use wheelchairs or walkers. For them, a day without the elevator can mean a day when they cannot leave home. In August, the elevator was out for 12 days in a row.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and gave it a passing certificate. We are not saying the elevator is unsafe when it runs. Our concern is how often it does not run.

**The repair**

In June, you told us that a new controller part was on order and would arrive in 6 to 8 weeks. You have now told us the same thing again. We are not asking you to make a part arrive sooner than it can. We are asking for a date we can rely on, and for a plan to help the residents who are hit hardest until then.

**The second elevator**

Seventy-four of our 112 households signed a petition asking for a second elevator. That is two households in every three. You have told us it would cost about $400,000 and cannot be done in this building. We are not asking you to reverse that decision today. We are asking you to set out the reasons in writing, so that the families who signed can read them for themselves, and to tell us what you can do instead.

**What happens next**

We want to solve this with you, not against you. When we receive your reply, we will post it in the lobby next to this letter, so that every resident can read it.

Sincerely,

[Name]
Secretary, on behalf of the [Association Name] Tenants' Association
