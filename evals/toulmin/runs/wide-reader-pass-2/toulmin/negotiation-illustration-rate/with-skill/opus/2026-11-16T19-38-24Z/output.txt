I left out the line saying every other publisher pays at least $12,000, and I'd recommend you don't use it. Your own facts don't support it:

- **What you know:** one publisher paid you $12,500. Your agent puts the normal range at $11,000 to $13,000.
- **What the line says:** every publisher pays at least $12,000. Your agent's own range starts $1,000 below that.
- **It works against your ask.** If everyone pays $12,000 or more, the publisher will wonder why you're asking for only $11,500. It also gives them an easy reply: "Not every publisher does." Then the argument becomes about that line instead of your fee.

Your real numbers make a stronger case. You have a concrete $12,500 fee, and your agent's range puts $11,500 near its low end. So you come across as fair and loyal, not as bluffing. The draft below is about 210 words.

One way to make it stronger: if you'd turn the book down at $9,000, or have other work competing for those four months, adding that would show what a "no" costs them. I didn't include it because you didn't say it's true.
