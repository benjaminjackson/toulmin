**An Open Letter to [Management Company] from the Tenants of [Building Name]**

To [Management Company]:

Our building has nine floors, 112 apartments and one elevator. This year that elevator has been out of service on 41 days, which is more than one day in every eight. In August it was out for 12 days in a row.

For most of us an elevator outage is a hardship. For some of our neighbours it is far worse. Twenty-three residents of this building are over 75. Six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the elevator stops, a resident who uses a wheelchair and lives above the ground floor cannot get to a doctor's appointment or go out to buy food. They cannot leave home at all unless someone carries them.

In June you told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. Five months later you are telling us the same thing: six to eight weeks.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and issued a passing certificate. We do not question it. But a certificate says the elevator is safe to ride when it is running. It does not say the elevator will be running when our residents need it. This year, on 41 days, it was not.

Seventy-four of our 112 households, two in every three, signed a petition asking for a second elevator. You have told us it would cost about $400,000 and cannot be done in this building. If that is so, we ask you to show us why. Whatever the answer on a second elevator, this building needs one elevator that works.

We ask you for three things:

1. **A firm installation date for the controller part, in writing**, together with the supplier's confirmation of the order.
2. **A plan, made together with the association, for residents who cannot use the stairs** for whenever the elevator is out of service.
3. **A written explanation of why a second elevator is not possible**, and a meeting with the association to discuss what can be done instead.

We have posted this letter in our lobby and shared it with [TV station], because neighbours who cannot take the stairs deserve to see this settled in public. We would much rather be able to report that it has been.

Sincerely,

The [Building Name] Tenants' Association
[Name], Secretary
