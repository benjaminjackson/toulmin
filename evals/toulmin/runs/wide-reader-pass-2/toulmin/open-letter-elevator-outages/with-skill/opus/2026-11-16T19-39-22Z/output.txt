**An Open Letter to [Property Management Company] from the Tenants of [Building Name]**

Our building has nine stories, 112 apartments and one elevator.

This year, that elevator has been out of service on 41 days. That is about one day in every eight. In August it was out for 12 days in a row.

For most of us, that means climbing stairs. For some of our neighbours it means much more. Twenty-three residents of this building are over 75. Six use wheelchairs or walkers. When the elevator stops, those who live on the upper floors cannot get down to the street. They miss appointments. They depend on others for groceries and medicine. In August, some of them waited out twelve days.

In June, you told us a new controller part was on order and would arrive in six to eight weeks. It is now November. You are telling us the same thing.

We know the city inspected the elevator in September and issued a passing certificate. We do not doubt that it is safe when it runs. But the certificate does not tell us how often it runs, and that is the problem.

Seventy-four of our 112 households signed a petition asking for a second elevator. That is two households in every three. You have told us a second elevator would cost about $400,000 and cannot be built in this building. If that is so, residents deserve to see the reasons.

Whatever the answer on a second elevator, the one we have must work. We ask you to:

1. **Give us a confirmed delivery and installation date for the controller part, in writing.** Tell us what you will do if that date slips again.
2. **Put a plan in place, before the next breakdown, for residents who cannot use the stairs.**
3. **Share the assessment behind your conclusion that a second elevator cannot be built here.**

If nothing changes, next year will look like this one. At this year's pace, that is roughly seven weeks without an elevator in a nine-story building. Many of us have lived here for years and want to keep living here. We want to work with you to fix this. But we cannot accept another "six to eight weeks."

We look forward to your written reply.

The [Building Name] Tenants' Association
[Name], Secretary
