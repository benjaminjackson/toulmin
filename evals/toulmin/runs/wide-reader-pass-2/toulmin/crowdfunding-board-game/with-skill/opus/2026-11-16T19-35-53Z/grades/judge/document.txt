**A cooperative game about getting a family through the winter**

You and up to three others play as a lighthouse keeper's family. Or play alone. Nobody wins at someone else's expense. You share the work, you share the decisions, and everyone at the table has the same goal: get the family through to the end of winter, together.

I've spent three years making that goal worth fighting for. Now I need your help to print it.

**Tested at the table, 140 times**

Since 2023 I've run 140 playtest sessions with about 60 different people. That's 140 times I've watched people play it and then gone back to fix whatever didn't work.

I've taken it to two conventions. On BoardGameGeek, the 23 people who have rated it so far give it an average of **8.1 out of 10**.

And **1,240 people** signed up to hear the day this campaign went live. If you're one of them, thank you. You're a big part of why I believe this can work.

No game is a hit before it's in a box. But this one has been played hard, rated well and asked for, and it's ready to be made.
