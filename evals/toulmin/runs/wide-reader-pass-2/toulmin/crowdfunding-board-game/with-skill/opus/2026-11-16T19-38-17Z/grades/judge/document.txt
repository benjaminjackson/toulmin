**See a lighthouse keeper's family through the winter.**

This is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. Nobody at the table plays against anyone else. You work together to get one family through one long winter, and you win or lose together. If you play alone, it plays solo.

**Tested hard before you pay a cent**

I've run 140 playtest sessions since 2023, with about 60 different people. That's 140 chances to find what didn't work, what dragged and what confused people, and all of it happened before anyone was asked for money.

I've also taken the game to two conventions. On the main board game ratings site, 23 people have rated it, with an average of 8.1 out of 10. That's an early score from a small group, and I'm telling you so because I'd rather you trust the number than be dazzled by it. Still, it's the number I hoped for.
