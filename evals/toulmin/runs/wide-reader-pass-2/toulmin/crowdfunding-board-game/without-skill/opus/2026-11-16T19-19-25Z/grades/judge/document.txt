**[Game Title]: Keep the Light Burning Until Spring**

The last supply boat has gone. The sea is freezing at the edges. Between now and the spring thaw, your family has to keep the lamp lit, the larder full and each other going.

[Game Title] is a cooperative board game for 1 to 4 players. You play a lighthouse keeper's family getting through one long winter. Everyone wins together or loses together. Every choice is a trade-off: oil for the lamp or fuel for the stove, mending the roof or watching the water. Storms come in, supplies run out, and the ships out in the dark rely on you.

It's a game about the hard things that happen in a small, close household. It works well as a quiet solo evening and just as well as a tense four-player night around the kitchen table.
