Subject: Quarterly update: flat revenue, our biggest customer left, and what we launch next month

Hi all,

First, I owe you an apology. This is the first update you've had from me in six months. I should have written after each of the last two quarters, and I didn't. This one covers where we stand now, including the bad news.

**The numbers**
- Monthly recurring revenue: $41,000, the same as three months ago
- Clinics: 9 added, 4 lost (net +5)
- Lost our largest customer, a 12-clinic chain paying $5,500/month
- Cash: $480,000. Burn: about $44,000/month, which gives us about 11 months of runway
- Next month: we start selling our text reminders on their own for $150/month

**The loss**
The chain moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. That's the biggest competitive threat we face: a records system that comes with a scheduler that's good enough. I'd rather tell you that plainly than leave you to work it out from the numbers.

**What the flat number hides**
Losing $5,500 a month from one customer and still finishing level means the rest of the business grew. Leaving the chain out, monthly revenue went from $35,500 to $41,000 this quarter, up about 15%. Nine new clinics signed on.

**What's working**
Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a no-show rate of 7%, against 13% before they started. That nearly halves missed appointments. An empty slot is revenue a clinic can't get back, so this is the result our customers feel most directly.

**Why we're selling reminders on their own**
The reminders are the part of the product with the clearest, most measurable result. Selling them separately at $150/month means a clinic can buy them without giving up the scheduler it already has, including one built into its records system. That gives us a product to offer the kind of customer we just lost, instead of having to win them away from software they already have.

**Runway**
At our current burn we have about 11 months of cash. The reminder launch is the main thing I'll be watching, and it will be the center of the next update.

If you'd like to go through any of this in more detail, reply here and I'll set up a call.

Thanks for your patience, and for your support.

Ben
