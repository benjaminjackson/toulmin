Subject: Quarterly update: MRR flat at $41K, largest customer lost, reminders launching standalone

Hi all,

First: you haven't had an update from me in two quarters. That was a mistake. You should hear news from me on schedule, especially when some of it is bad, and this quarter's is mixed.

**The numbers**
- MRR: $41,000, the same as three months ago
- Clinics: 9 added, 4 lost
- Cash: $480,000
- Net burn: about $44,000/month, so just under 11 months of runway
- Team: 6

**What happened**
We lost our largest customer, a 12-clinic chain that paid $5,500 a month. They moved to the scheduler built into their electronic records system. That one account was about 13% of our revenue.

Apart from the chain, the business grew. Not counting that account, MRR rose $5,500 this quarter, with new clinics more than covering the three other customers we lost. So the flat number hides real sales. It also shows a risk I take seriously: when a records system offers a scheduler that's good enough, a whole chain can leave at once.

**What's working**
Clinics that use our automatic text reminders have a 7% no-show rate, down from 13% before they started using them. That's almost half of their missed appointments gone. For a physical therapy clinic, a no-show is a slot it can't bill, so this is the clearest value we deliver.

**What's next**
Next month we start selling the reminder feature on its own for $150 a month. It's aimed at clinics like the one we just lost: clinics that are happy scheduling in their records system but still lose appointments to no-shows. Until now, those clinics had to switch schedulers to get our reminders. Once this launches, they won't have to.

**Where that leaves us**
Revenue is flat, we have just under 11 months of cash at our current burn, and our strongest result is a feature we're about to sell separately. The next few months will show whether that product sells on its own. I'll send you the first numbers in next quarter's update, and I'll send it on time.
