Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: what it means for you

To all staff,

I'm writing with news that affects all of us, and most of all our 14 colleagues at the Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Both branches will close on March 31. Everyone who works at them is being offered a job in our new member phone and chat center.

If you work at Maple Street or Route 9, here is what this means for you:

- **Your job:** A role in the phone and chat center is open to every one of you.
- **Your pay:** The same as you earn now, or higher.
- **Your hours:** The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. That's a change from the 9-to-5 branch day, and for some of you it will be a hard one.
- **Working from home:** After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **If you decide not to move:** You'll receive two weeks of pay for every year you've worked here.

**Why we're making this change**

Over the last three years, visits to these two branches fell 44%. Over the same three years, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we haven't kept up. Right now a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That isn't the service we want to give, and the colleagues who pick up those calls are the ones who hear about it.

**What I believe this means for our work**

I believe this change will make work better for many of us, though not in every way for every person. For those moving to the center, it means the same or better pay, two days a week at home once you're trained, and work in the place where members now need us most. For everyone who serves members, it means a team built to answer calls and chats, so members reach us sooner. The longer hours and rotating shifts are a real cost, and I won't pretend they aren't.

This is the right decision for our members and for the credit union, and I know it's a big change for the people it touches most. The skills you've built at Maple Street and Route 9 are exactly what members need from us on the phone and in chat.

**What happens next**

If you work at Maple Street or Route 9, please bring any question to your manager or reply to me directly. Nothing about your own situation is too small to ask.

Thank you for everything you do for our members.
