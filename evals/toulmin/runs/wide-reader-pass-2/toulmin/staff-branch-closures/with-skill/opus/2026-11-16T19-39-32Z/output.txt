I recommend one change: don't say the move will make **everyone's** job better. Your facts don't support that, and the staff you most need to keep can see why.

**What the facts support:** All 14 people keep a job, at the same pay or higher. They get two days a week at home once training is done. Their work moves to where members now come to you: branch visits are down 44% and phone and chat contacts are up 60%.

**What they don't support:** The center runs 8 to 8 with rotating shifts. The branches were 9 to 5. For anyone with childcare or a second job, that's a real cost, and they'll know it the moment they read the hours. A line saying "this is better for everyone" would make them doubt the rest of the email. Nothing in your facts says how the move helps staff at the other five branches either.

**Subject: Maple Street and Route 9 branches closing March 31: what it means for our team**

All,

I want you to hear this from me directly. On March 31 we will close our Maple Street and Route 9 branches. Nobody who works at those branches will lose their job. All 14 of them are being offered a role in our new member phone and chat center.

If you work at Maple Street or Route 9, here is what this means for you:

- **Pay:** Your new role pays the same as your current one or more.
- **Hours:** The center is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, and shifts will rotate. That's a real change from the 9-to-5 schedule you work now. I know it will matter more to some of you than to others, and I want to hear from you about it.
- **Working from home:** After 90 days of training, you can work from home two days a week.
- **If you decide not to take the role:** You will receive two weeks of pay for every year you have worked here. For example, someone with eight years here would receive 16 weeks of pay.

**Why we're doing this**

Over the last three years, visits to these two branches fell 44%. Over the same period, phone and chat contacts across the credit union rose 60%. Our members have changed how they reach us, and we haven't kept up. Today a member who calls us waits an average of 9 minutes on hold. That's too long, and fixing it is the main job of the new center.

The longer hours are part of that fix. Members who work 9 to 5 themselves will be able to reach a real person before or after their own workday.

I believe this is a good move for the people taking these roles. You keep a secure job at the same pay or better. You get a schedule with time at home built in. And you'll be doing the work our members most need from us now. The people at Maple Street and Route 9 know our members and our products as well as anyone here. I want that experience on the other end of the phone when a member calls.

I also know this isn't a small change. Some of you chose branch work because you like being face to face with members, or because 9 to 5 fits your life. Those are fair reasons, and you're free to weigh this offer against them. Whatever you decide, you'll be treated with respect.

If you have questions, reply to this email or come and find me. I'd rather hear your concerns now than have you carry them alone.

Thank you, all of you, for the work you do for our members.
