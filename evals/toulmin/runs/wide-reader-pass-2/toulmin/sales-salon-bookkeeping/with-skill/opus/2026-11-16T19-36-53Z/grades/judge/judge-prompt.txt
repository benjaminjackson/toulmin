<brief>
Write the main text for my sales page. I'm a bookkeeper and I work only with hair and nail salons. I do monthly books, payroll for up to 10 staff, and sales tax filing.

What's true:
- I have 31 salon clients. 2 left last year, both because they sold their salons.
- I charge $199 a month. Until January it was $149, and I raised it for everyone, including existing clients.
- I close each month's books by the 10th of the next month. I missed that date 3 times last year.

- I'm one person. I don't answer the phone on Sundays or Mondays.
- The first call is free and takes 20 minutes.

Keep it to about 350 words, with a button at the end to book the call.
</brief>



<document>
# Bookkeeping for hair and nail salons. Only salons.

I keep the books for 31 hair and nail salons. Salons are the only clients I take on.

## What you get every month

- **Monthly books**, closed by the 10th of the following month
- **Payroll** for up to 10 staff
- **Sales tax filing**

## Clean books cost less at tax time

Last tax season, 9 of my clients told me their accountant charged them less because their books came in clean. One owner saved $800.

## Salons stay

Two clients left last year. Both had sold their salons.

## What you should know before you hire me

I'm one person, not a firm. That means you deal with me every month: the same person, who already knows your salon.

It also means two things I'd rather you hear from me than find out later. My deadline is to close your books by the 10th, and last year I missed it three times. And I don't answer the phone on Sundays or Mondays.

## Price

**$199 a month** for monthly books, payroll and sales tax.

Every client pays the same. When I raised the price from $149 in January, I raised it for everyone, including clients who'd been with me for years. You won't get a low starting rate that jumps later.

Payroll is included for up to 10 staff. If your team is bigger, tell me on the call.

## Start with a 20-minute call

The first call is free and takes 20 minutes. You'll tell me about your salon, I'll tell you how I work, and we'll both know whether it's a fit.

**[Book your free 20-minute call]**
</document>

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
