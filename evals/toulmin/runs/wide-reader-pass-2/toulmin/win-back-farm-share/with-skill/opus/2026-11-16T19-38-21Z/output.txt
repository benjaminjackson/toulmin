I wrote the email for readers who feel let down. It opens with a plain apology and admits the shortfall. Next it lists what has changed, then the price and the deadline. Here are some choices I made, with my reasoning:

- **The loss is put in numbers.** Half a box for six weeks comes to about three weeks of produce they paid for. Naming that shows you understand what they lost. You can remove the phrase if you'd prefer.
- **The changes aren't oversold.** The hail nets cover only tomatoes and peppers, so the email doesn't say next season is safe from storms. A reader who got burned once will trust a careful claim more.
- **It doesn't offer credit for future crop losses or a returning-member discount.** You didn't give me either. They're the two things most likely to win these people back. A line like "If a storm cuts your boxes again, we'll credit you" answers the main reason they left. If you can commit to that, add it as a fourth bullet.
