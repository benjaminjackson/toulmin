I wrote the letter, but it doesn't promise to cut their new-hire training time in half. I'd recommend keeping that line out, for three reasons:

- **You can't back it up yet.** You don't know how long their training takes now, or which parts are fixed by their written procedures and by regulators. Nothing in your record shows you shortening a training program either. Your 12-hour new-teacher training is a good fact, but you haven't said it used to be longer.
- **It could backfire with this reader.** In a regulated plant, "half the training time" can sound like "half the training." That's exactly the worry a hiring manager will already have about someone from outside the industry.
- **You have stronger evidence.** You wrote a safety program, and roughly seven years of labs with no injury beyond first aid have followed. That record answers the cutting-corners worry instead of raising it.
