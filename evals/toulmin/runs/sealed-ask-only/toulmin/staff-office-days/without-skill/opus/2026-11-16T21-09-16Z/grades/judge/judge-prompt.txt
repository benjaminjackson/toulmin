**Subject: Moving to three office days from September 1**

Dear all,

From September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. This ends fully remote work at the firm. We'd rather you hear it in writing before Friday's all-staff meeting, so you have time to think about it and bring your questions.

**Why we're making this change**

Two things have pushed us here.

First, we're losing work. Last year we lost two large hospital projects at the final interview. Both clients gave us the same feedback: our team seemed not to know each other. In a shortlist interview, clients are judging whether we can work together as a team, and for those two projects we didn't show them that.

Second, our people are developing more slowly. Our review records show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. We think much of that comes from what people pick up by sitting near more experienced colleagues: overhearing a client call, getting a drawing marked up at the desk, being pulled into a problem as it happens. This is hard to replace remotely, and our junior staff are the ones losing out.

**What this means for you**

- **Most of you:** From September 1, you'll be in the office Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.
- **Colleagues hired as fully remote:** We know 15 of you joined in 2021 and 2022 on fully remote terms, and that this is a real change to what you signed up for. We aren't taking that lightly.
- **The four colleagues who live more than two hours away:** You can keep working remotely until the end of 2027. From then on, we'll ask you to come in one day a week. We'll talk with each of you individually.

**What's changing for us**

The partners have been coming in one day a week. That changes too. From September 1, all three of us will be in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. We can't ask you to be here for each other if we aren't.

**Friday**

We'll go through this at Friday's all-staff meeting and take questions. If you'd rather raise something privately, any of the three of us is happy to talk.

Thank you. We know this asks a lot of some of you, and we think it's the right thing for the firm and for the people in it.

[Partner name], [Partner name] and [Partner name]
</document>
