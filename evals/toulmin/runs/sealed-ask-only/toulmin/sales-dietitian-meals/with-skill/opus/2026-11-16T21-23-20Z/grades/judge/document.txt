# Steady Plate

## Cooked lunches and dinners for adults with type 2 diabetes, planned by a registered dietitian

When you have type 2 diabetes, every meal means counting carbs. Steady Plate takes lunch and dinner off your list. Every Sunday we deliver 10 cooked meals to homes within 25 miles of downtown. Each meal has 45 grams of carbohydrate or less.

### Planned by a dietitian

I'm Lena Ortiz, a registered dietitian, and I plan every menu myself. Before your first delivery, we'll have a 15-minute phone call so I can learn about your routine and what you like to eat.

### What our customers' numbers show

Fourteen customers have shared their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months. Ten saw their A1C go down, three stayed about the same, and one went up. Among them, the average drop was 0.6 points.

That's a small group, and these customers chose to share their results. I also don't track whether anyone changed their medication during that time. So I can't promise your A1C will go down. What I can promise is that every meal stays within a carb limit I set, so lunch and dinner are two fewer things to work out. Steady Plate doesn't replace your care team, so keep working with your doctor as usual.

### How it works

- **10 meals a week for $129.** That's about $12.90 a meal, delivered every Sunday.
- **Choose what you eat.** The menu repeats every four weeks, and each week you can swap any meal for one of six other options.
- **Order by Thursday at noon** for that Sunday's delivery.
- **No contract.** Skip a week or cancel any time.

### People stay

Today, 88 people get their meals from Steady Plate. Of those, 37 have been with us for more than six months.
