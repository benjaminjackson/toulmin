**Lunch and dinner, planned by a dietitian for life with type 2 diabetes**

Managing type 2 diabetes means thinking about food all day: what to eat, how many carbs it has and what it will do to your numbers. Our goal is to take a big part of that off your plate.

Every Sunday we deliver 10 fully cooked lunches and dinners to your door. Each meal has **45 grams of carbohydrate or less**, and every menu is planned by me, a registered dietitian. I don't hand that job to someone else. You heat the meal, eat it, and get on with your day.

**What you get**

- Delivery every Sunday in our metro area

**What our customers have seen**

We currently cook for 88 people, and 37 of them have been with us for more than six months. People stay because the meals make their week easier and they like the food.

Some customers have chosen to share their A1C results with me from before they started and after three months. So far 14 people have done this:

- **10** saw their A1C go down, with an average drop of 0.6 points
- **3** stayed about the same
- **1** went up

This is a small group of people who volunteered their results. It is not a clinical study, and I don't track other changes they may have made, such as changes to their medication. Your results will depend on many things, including what else you eat, your activity and your treatment plan. Our meals can be a solid, consistent part of that plan.
