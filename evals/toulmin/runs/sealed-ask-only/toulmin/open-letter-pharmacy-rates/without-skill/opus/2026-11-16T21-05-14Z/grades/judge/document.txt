**An Open Letter to the [State] State Employees' Health Plan**
**From 38 Independent Pharmacies Across [State]**

To [Plan Name] and its Board of Trustees:

We are 38 independent pharmacies in [State]. About one in every five prescriptions we fill (22%) goes to a member of your plan: a state employee, a retiree, or someone in their family. We are writing because since January, the plan has paid us less to fill those prescriptions than it costs us to fill them.

Take the 20 generic drugs we fill most often. For those drugs, the plan's payment now comes in an average of $1.80 below what we pay to buy and dispense each prescription. That is not a thin margin. It is a loss, and our pharmacies absorb it on every one of those prescriptions.

My own pharmacy filled about 3,100 prescriptions for plan members last year. Every pharmacy signing this letter can tell you the same thing: a small business cannot keep losing money on a fifth of its work.

Nine independent pharmacies in [State] have closed in the past two years. We won't claim to know why each one closed. But we know what the numbers look like from behind the counter, and we know what a closure means where we work. In 14 counties, the nearest pharmacy for most residents is an independent one. When that pharmacy closes, state workers and retirees there don't switch to the store down the street, because there isn't one. They drive to the next town, wait for the mail, or go without their medicine.

The plan can fix this. Its contract with its pharmacy benefit manager is up for renewal on July 1. We ask the plan to:

1. **Require in the new contract** that pharmacies be paid no less than what it costs to buy and dispense each drug.
2. **Require the pharmacy benefit manager to disclose** how it sets what it pays pharmacies, and what it charges the plan for the same prescriptions.
3. **Meet with us before the contract is signed.** We will bring our invoices.

We are not asking for a windfall. We want to keep filling prescriptions for your members, many of whom we have known for years. We are asking to be paid what it costs to fill them.

Sincerely,

[Name]
Owner, [Pharmacy Name], [Town]
On behalf of 38 independent pharmacies in [State]
