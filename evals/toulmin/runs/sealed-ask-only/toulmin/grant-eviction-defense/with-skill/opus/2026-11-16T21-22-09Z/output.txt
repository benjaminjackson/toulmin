A few things to check:

- **Third attorney or replacement:** You don't yet know if you'll rehire for the departing attorney's spot. So the summary says plainly that the grant at least keeps you at two attorneys, and makes three possible. Reviewers will likely work out the staffing math either way. Saying it up front protects your credibility. Once you know you'll rehire, change the last paragraph to say the grant creates a third position.
- **The 58% figure:** I left it as your result and didn't compare it to tenants without a lawyer, since you don't have that number. If you find county or court data on unrepresented tenants, adding it would make the summary much stronger.
- **Placeholders and dates:** Replace [County] with your county's name. I wrote "October 2027" for the county renewal and assumed the June departure is June 2027. Correct either one if I've got it wrong.
