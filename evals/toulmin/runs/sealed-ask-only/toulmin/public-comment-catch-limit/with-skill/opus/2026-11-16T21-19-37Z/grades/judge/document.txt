Here's why. I've been through this before. When the limit was cut in 2016, my bookings fell from 170 trips to 138 the next season. Recovery took years, and a two-fish limit for three years is a deeper cut held longer.

I can tell you every one of us would be at risk, and that charter boats land about nine percent of the state's summer flounder. Private boats land about eighty. A cut that weighs this heavily on us can't do much for the stock on its own.

About seventy percent of my trips are already booked. Those are real families and real paychecks.

We'll help you get this right. I'll hand your staff my trip logs for every season since 2016, and next season our association will test a catch-reporting app with your staff. Let's rebuild this stock with better data and a three-fish limit or a shorter season, not by cutting to two.
