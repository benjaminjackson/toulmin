**Bring the Majestic Back**

The Majestic on Main Street has been dark since 2022. The nearest cinema is now 38 miles away, so a night at the movies means a 76-mile round trip.

We're the volunteers working to reopen it. We need to raise $60,000 by June 30.

**What your pledge pays for**

Reopening will cost $85,000. That buys a new projector and a new sound system and fixes the roof. A local bank has pledged $25,000 if we raise the other $60,000 by June 30. If we do, the building's owner will rent us the Majestic for $1 a year for ten years.

**We've tried before**

Three years ago we asked for $120,000. We raised $47,000, fell short and refunded every dollar. Many of you gave then, and we haven't forgotten that.

This time is different:

- **The goal is $35,000 lower.**
- **The bank's pledge covers nearly a third of the cost.**
- **Rent is $1 a year for ten years.**
- **46 volunteers have already signed up** to run the box office and the snack bar.

**The town still wants its cinema**

When the Majestic closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. People here didn't stop going to the movies. The town lost its cinema.

**All or nothing**

The campaign runs from May 1 to June 30. If pledges and the bank's $25,000 don't reach the full $85,000, every pledge is returned in full. Your money only goes into the project if we can finish it.

Pledge what you can, then tell a neighbor. We need $60,000, and we hope to see you in the seats.

Ruth Albers
Chair, on behalf of the Majestic volunteers
