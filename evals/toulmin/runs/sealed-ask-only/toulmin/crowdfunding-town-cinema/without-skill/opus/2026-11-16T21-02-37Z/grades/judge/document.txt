**Bring the Lights Back On at [Cinema Name]**

The screen at [Cinema Name] has been dark since 2022. The nearest cinema is now 38 miles away, which makes a night at the movies a 76-mile round trip. For many families, teenagers and older neighbors, that means no movies at all.

We can change that, and this time we have a real path to the finish line.

**The plan**

We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and fix the roof. If we raise it, the building's owner will rent us the cinema for $1 a year for ten years. That means no mortgage and no rent squeeze, just a cinema run by and for this town.

**Why this time is different**

Many of you gave three years ago, when we set out to raise $120,000. We reached $47,000. That wasn't enough, so we refunded every dollar.

We learned from that. This plan costs about 30% less, and we aren't starting from zero. [Bank Name] has pledged $25,000 if we raise the remaining $60,000 by June 30.

Last time, this town gave $47,000. This time we need $60,000, and every gift helps unlock the bank's $25,000.

**The people are ready**

46 volunteers have already signed up to run the box office and the snack bar. When the cinema closed, it was still selling about 180 tickets a week. The audience is here and so are the volunteers. What's missing is the equipment and a roof that keeps the rain out.

**What we're asking**

Help us raise $60,000 by June 30. Give what you can, share this page and tell your neighbors. [If we fall short, every pledge will be refunded in full.]

Then save a seat. We'll see you at the movies.
