**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** November 2026
**Re:** Replacing display advertising with reader membership from January 2027

**Decision requested**
Approve ending display advertising on January 1, 2027, and launching a paid reader membership program. The board would formally review it at its June 2027 meeting.

**Why now**
Display ads brought in $131,000 in 2024 and $84,000 in 2025, a 36% drop in one year. If the decline continues at that rate, ads will bring in about $35,000 in 2027. Keeping ads will not keep us at $84,000, because that revenue is already shrinking.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week pilot this spring, 212 readers joined at $8 a month, worth $20,350 a year. We did no marketing beyond two newsletter mentions. That is 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers, at about 35 sign-ups a week.

**The target**
To match 2025 ad revenue in full, we need 875 members at $8 a month, about 6% of our newsletter list. Getting there by December 2027 means adding about 13 members a week. That is roughly a third of the pace the pilot reached without any promotion. If membership grows steadily toward that number, it will bring in about $52,000 during 2027. That is less than ads earned in 2025, but more than ads are on course to earn next year. From 2028 onward, membership at that level brings in the full $84,000 a year.

A newsroom of our size in another state reports that membership now covers 40% of its budget. That does not prove our numbers, but it shows the model can work for an organization like ours.

**Risks and how we will manage them**
- *The pilot drew our most committed readers, so later sign-ups will be slower.* The target allows for this: it needs a third of the pilot's pace, not all of it.
- *Members cancel.* I will report sign-ups and cancellations to the board monthly. The first report will include how many pilot members are still paying.
- *Cash flow early in the year.* In January, pilot members will bring in about $1,700 a month. In 2025, ads averaged about $7,000 a month. I will bring a cash-flow plan for the first half of 2027 to the December meeting.
- *Checkpoint.* We are on track if we have 550 members by June 30, 2027. If we have fewer than 450, I will bring the board options at that meeting, including returning to ad or sponsorship sales.

**Our ad sales colleague**
[Name] has sold our advertising part-time for 11 years. I will speak with them before the board discusses this memo. I propose offering them a role in the membership program.

**Recommendation**
Ad revenue is falling whether or not we act. Membership has already shown it can grow without marketing, and it reaches the target with a third of the pilot's pace. I recommend the board approve the move from January 1, 2027, and review it in June.
