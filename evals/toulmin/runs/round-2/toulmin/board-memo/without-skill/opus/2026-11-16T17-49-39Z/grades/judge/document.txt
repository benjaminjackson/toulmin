**MEMO**

**To:** Board of Directors
**From:** [Name], Executive Director
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Proposal to replace display advertising with reader membership from January 2027

**Decision requested**
Approve ending display advertising and launching a reader membership program on January 1, 2027.

**Why now**
Display ad revenue fell from $131,000 two years ago to $84,000 last year, a 36% drop, or about 20% a year. If that trend continues, ads will bring in about $67,000 in 2027. We'd be relying on a shrinking source and spending staff time and page space on it.

**What the pilot showed**
In a six-week spring pilot, 212 readers joined at $8 a month, about $20,000 a year. Our only promotion was two newsletter mentions. That's 1.5% of our 14,000 newsletter subscribers joining with almost no effort. [X] of them are still paying today.

A newsroom of our size in [state] now gets 40% of its budget from membership, so this model works for outlets like ours.

**What it takes to replace ad revenue**
| Target | Members needed at $8/mo | Share of newsletter list |
|---|---|---|
| Match projected 2027 ads (~$67,000) | ~700 | 5% |
| Match last year's ads ($84,000) | ~875 | 6.25% |

These figures are before payment fees and member cancellations, so the real targets are somewhat higher. Our goal is to replace ad revenue within 12 months. That requires roughly four times the pilot's conversion rate. We think this is achievable with a real marketing effort, but it isn't guaranteed, and we're asking you to approve it with that understanding.

**How we'll manage the risk**
- **Funded launch:** a sustained membership campaign across the newsletter, the site and events, with a budget of [$X].
- **Quarterly checkpoints:** we'll report member numbers, revenue and cancellations to the board each quarter against these targets: [Q1: X / Q2: X / Q3: X / Q4: X members].
- **Bridge funding:** [describe reserves or other funds that cover any 2027 shortfall, e.g., "$X from reserves, covering up to X months"].
- **Trigger for review:** if we're below [X]% of target at the six-month checkpoint, I'll bring options back to the board, including sponsorships or reintroducing limited advertising.
