**YES ON MEASURE 4: Library Levy | Ridgeview Door Script**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm ___, a neighbor volunteering with Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**WHY IT MATTERS**
"Measure 4 keeps the Ridgeview library open. Without it, our branch closes this July. It also brings back Sunday hours at all five branches in the city. And people are using the libraries: visits are up 22% since 2021."

**WHAT IT COSTS** *(homeowners)*
"It's 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value, for six years. For a typical Ridgeview home, that's about $10 a month, or roughly $2.35 a week."
*(Renters: "The levy is paid by property owners. It isn't a separate bill to renters.")*

**THE ASK**
"Can we count on you to vote yes on Measure 4 in March?"
- **Yes:** "Thank you! Do you know how you'll vote? Could we put a sign in your yard?"
- **Unsure:** "What's your biggest question about it?"
- **No:** "Thanks for your time. Have a good day." *(Don't argue. Mark and move on.)*

---

**IF YOU HEAR…**

**"It's a blank check."**
"It's actually very specific: $2.3 million a year, for six years, and it ends unless voters renew it. It pays for Sunday hours at all five branches and keeps Ridgeview open."

**"I'm on a fixed income."**
"I understand. For most homes here it's about $10 a month. [IF APPLICABLE: Seniors may qualify for ______. The county assessor can tell you more.]"

**"Didn't this fail last time?"**
"It lost by just four points. That's why we're talking to every neighbor this time. Every vote counts."

*Be friendly, be brief, and stick to the facts on this card.*
