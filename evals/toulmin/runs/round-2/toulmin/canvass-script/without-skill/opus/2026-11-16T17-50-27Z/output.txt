**YES ON MEASURE 4: Door Script (Ridgeview)**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm ___, a volunteer with Yes on Measure 4. Do you have a minute to talk about the library measure on the March ballot?"

**ASK**
"Have you heard about Measure 4? What do you think of it?"
*Listen first. Then:*

**WHY**
"Measure 4 keeps the Ridgeview branch open. Without it, our branch closes this July. It also brings back Sunday hours at all five branches. Library visits are up 22% since 2021, so people are using them."

**COST**
"It's 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value, for six years, and then it ends. For a typical Ridgeview home that's about $10 a month, around 33 cents a day."
*[Other precincts: "about $6.50 a month for a typical home in the city, around 21 cents a day."]*

**ID**
"Can we count on your yes vote in March?"
*Mark 1 (strong yes) to 5 (strong no). Thank them, leave the flyer, remind them of the election date.*

---

**IF THEY SAY…**

**"It's a blank check."**
"It's actually very specific: a set rate, for six years only, and it pays for Sunday hours and keeping the Ridgeview branch open. After six years it expires unless voters approve it again."

**"I'm on a fixed income."**
"That's a fair concern. For a typical home here it's about $10 a month, and it keeps a library open right here in the neighborhood."

**"I rent."**
"Renters vote on this too, and it keeps your branch open and open on Sundays."

**"How much would I pay?"**
"Take your assessed value, divide by 1,000, and multiply by 19 cents. That's your yearly cost."

*Stay friendly, keep it short, and don't argue. Just mark them and move on.*
