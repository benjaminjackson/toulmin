**YES ON MEASURE 4: Library Levy | Door Script**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm ___. I'm a neighbor volunteering for Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**ASK**
"Do you or anyone in your household use the library?"
*(Listen. Build on what they say: kids, books, computers, programs.)*

**WHAT IT DOES**
"Measure 4 restores Sunday hours at all five branches and keeps the Ridgeview branch open. Without it, Ridgeview closes in July. Library visits are up 22% since 2021, so people are using them more than ever."

**WHAT IT COSTS** *(say this plainly, don't round it down)*
"It's 19 cents per $1,000 of assessed value, for six years, and then it ends.
- **Ridgeview:** For a typical home here, that's about $10 a month.
- **Elsewhere in the city:** For a typical home, that's about $6.50 a month."

**ASK FOR THE VOTE**
"Can we count on your yes vote on Measure 4 in March?"
*Mark: YES / LEANING YES / UNDECIDED / NO*

---

**IF YOU HEAR…**

**"It's a blank check."**
"I understand the concern. It's a fixed rate, it ends after six years, and it pays for specific things: Sunday hours at all five branches and keeping Ridgeview open."

**"I'm on a fixed income."**
"That's a real concern, and I won't tell you it's nothing. For a typical Ridgeview home it's about $10 a month. What you get for it is keeping this branch open."

**"I rent."**
"Your vote counts the same as anyone's, and the branches serve everyone."

**A firm NO:** "Thanks for your time. Have a good day." Mark it and move on. Don't argue.

**Stick to the facts on this card. If you don't know an answer, say so and offer to have someone follow up.**
