**YES ON MEASURE 4: Library Levy | Door Script (Ridgeview)**

**OPEN**
"Hi, I'm ___. I'm a volunteer with Yes on Measure 4, the library levy on the March ballot. Do you have a minute?"

**THE CASE**
"If Measure 4 doesn't pass, the Ridgeview library closes in July. Measure 4 keeps it open. It also brings back Sunday hours at all five branches in the city. People are using our libraries more than ever: visits are up 22% since 2021.

For a typical Ridgeview home, it costs about $10 a month, or $122 a year. The rate is fixed, and it ends after six years.

Can we count on your yes vote on Measure 4 in March?"

**IF THEY SAY…**

*"It's a blank check."*
"I can see why that worries people. But the rate is fixed at 19 cents per $1,000, it ends after six years, and the measure spells out what the money pays for: keeping this branch open and bringing back Sunday hours."

*"I'm on a fixed income."*
"I hear you, and that's a fair concern. Your amount is 19 cents for every $1,000 of assessed value. On a $500,000 home, that's $95 a year. Without it, the closest library to you closes this summer."

*"I rent."*
"You can still vote on it, and the library is for everyone in the city."

*Undecided:* "That's fair. Can I leave this with you? The vote is in March."

**CLOSE**
"Thanks for your time. Remember Measure 4 in March!"

*Outside Ridgeview, say: "For a typical home in the city, about $6.50 a month, or $78 a year."*
