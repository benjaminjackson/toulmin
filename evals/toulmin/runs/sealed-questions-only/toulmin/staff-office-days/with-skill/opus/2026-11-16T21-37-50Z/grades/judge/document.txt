Subject: Returning to the office three days a week from September 1, 2027

To everyone,

Starting September 1, 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. That includes all three partners. We'll talk it through together at Friday's meeting, but I wanted you to have the details in writing first.

**Why we're doing this**

Two things in our own records persuaded us.

Last year we lost two large hospital projects in the final interview. Both clients gave us the same reason: our team seemed not to know each other. These were projects we had worked hard for and could have won, and we lost them on how we came across as a team.

Our review records also show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. That's not on them. Much of how architects learn happens by sitting near more experienced people: overhearing a call with a client, getting quick feedback over a drawing, watching how a problem gets solved. We haven't been giving our newer people enough of that.

The partners haven't set a good example either. We've been coming in one day a week. That changes too: from September, all three of us will be in on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

**To the 15 of you hired as fully remote**

You joined us in 2021 and 2022 on the understanding that you would work remotely, and this changes that. I know that's a real cost, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

Four of you live more than two hours from the office. You can keep working fully remotely until December 31, 2027. From January 2028 you'll come in one day a week.

For the rest of you, the three-day schedule applies from September 1. If this creates a serious problem for you, please talk to me or HR directly and soon. I would rather hear it now than in August.

**Support with commuting**

The firm will pay up to $150 a month toward commuting costs for anyone who lives more than 30 miles from the office.

**Desks**

Right now we don't have a desk for everyone. New desks arrive in August, before the policy starts.

**Exceptions and review**

Apart from the four long-distance colleagues above, there are no exceptions to the three-day schedule. Six months after it starts, we'll review the policy with you and listen to what is and isn't working.

**Friday**

We'll meet at 10 a.m. on Friday in the main studio. Please bring your questions. If you'd like to raise something before then, or privately, you can come to me or to HR.

Paul Whitaker
Partner
