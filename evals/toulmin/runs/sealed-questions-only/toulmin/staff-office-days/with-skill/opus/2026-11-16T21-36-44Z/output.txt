**Subject: Working in the studio Tuesday to Thursday from 1 September 2027**

Hi everyone,

Most of you will be affected by this change, so I'm writing before Friday's all-staff meeting to give you time to read it and think about it before we talk.

From 1 September 2027, everyone at the firm will work in the studio on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays. This means we're ending fully remote work. Fifteen of you joined us as fully remote employees in 2021 and 2022. For you, this is a real change from what you signed up for, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

**Why we're doing this**

Last year we reached the final interview for two large hospital projects and lost both. When we asked the clients why, they both said the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. Projects like these keep a firm our size busy for years.

We see the same problem in our own review records. Junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to get to the point of leading a project. We believe part of the reason is that much of what prepares someone to lead is learned by sitting near people who already do it, and that's hard when we're rarely in the same room.

**What the partners will do**

Until now the partners have been in the studio one day a week, which means we haven't set the example. From 1 September, all three of us will be in Tuesday to Thursday too. Five of our six project leads already come in on those days by choice, so the people you'd most want to learn from will be there.

**What this means for you**

- **Commuting.** If you live more than 30 miles from the office, we'll pay up to $150 a month toward your commute.
- **If you live more than two hours away.** Four colleagues are in this position. You keep your current arrangement until 31 December 2027. From January 2028, you'll come in one day a week.
- **Desks.** We don't have a desk for everyone yet. New desks arrive in August, before the change begins.
- **Exceptions.** Apart from those four, we can't offer individual exceptions to the three days. I know that will be hard for some of you, and I'd rather tell you clearly now than leave it uncertain.
- **Review.** Six months in, around March 2028, we'll review how this is working, together with you.

**Questions**

We'll talk it through on Friday at 10 a.m. in the main studio. If you'd like to ask something beforehand, or in private, come to me or to HR.

Paul Whitaker
Partner
