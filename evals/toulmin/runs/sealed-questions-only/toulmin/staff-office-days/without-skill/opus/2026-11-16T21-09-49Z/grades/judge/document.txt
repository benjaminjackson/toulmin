**Subject: Coming back to the office three days a week, from September 1**

Dear all,

Before Friday's all-staff meeting, we want you to hear this decision directly from us so you have time to think about it before we meet.

**What's changing**

From September 1, everyone will work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Mondays and Fridays stay flexible.

This applies to the three partners too. For the past while we have come in only one day a week. That will change, and we'll be in all three days.

**Why we're doing this**

There are two reasons, and both come from our own experience.

First, last year we lost two large hospital projects in the final interview. Both clients told us the same thing: our team seemed not to know each other. That was hard to hear. Clients are hiring a team, and they can tell whether that team actually works together.

Second, our review records show that junior staff hired since 2022 are taking about 30% longer to reach the point where they lead a project. That isn't about anyone's ability or effort. Most of what makes someone ready to lead is learned by being around it: overhearing a difficult client call, sitting in on a design review, asking a quick question at someone's desk. We haven't been giving our newer people enough of that, and it's our responsibility to fix it.

**If you were hired as fully remote**

We know this is hardest for the 15 of you who joined in 2021 and 2022 as fully remote staff. You took those roles on terms we offered, and we're now changing them. We don't take that lightly.

The four of you who live more than two hours from the office can keep working remotely until the end of 2027. From then on, we'll ask you to come in once a week.

For the rest of you, we'd like to talk with each of you individually before September about what this means for you.

**Friday**

We'll go through all of this at Friday's meeting and answer your questions there. If you'd rather raise something privately, any of the three of us is glad to talk with you beforehand.

Thank you. We think this will make us a better firm, for our clients and for each other.
