A few things to know before you send it:

- **The price after the $99 cleaning isn't in the email** because you didn't know it. Since price was the top reason people left, many readers will want to know before they reply. If you can add a line like "after that, your regular price is $X," the email gets stronger. Even an honest "$165 every other week for a 3-bedroom" beats leaving them to guess.
- **I didn't say the backup is someone they've already met.** You didn't know that. If it is true, add it, because it's the best answer to "a stranger in my house."
- **"1 of 18 has left since July" covers about four months.** That's true and worth saying, but it isn't a full year. I didn't claim that turnover is solved.
- **I didn't link your pay to cleaners staying.** You paid $21 last year too, when 7 left, so the email presents pay and insurance as what the higher price buys, not as the reason cleaners stay.
- **Personalisation:** if you can merge in first names, use "Hi [First name]," instead of "Hi,".
