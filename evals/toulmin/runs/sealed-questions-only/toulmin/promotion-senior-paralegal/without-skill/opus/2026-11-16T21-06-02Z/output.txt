Subject: Request for Promotion to Senior Paralegal

Dear [Managing Partner's name],

I'm writing to ask you to consider me for promotion to Senior Paralegal in the litigation group. I've been a paralegal here for five years. Over the last two, I've taken on more of the work the senior role involves, and I'd like the chance to do it formally.

**Workload.** I support four lawyers. Last year I worked on 23 cases, two of which went to trial. I billed 1,640 hours against the group's target of 1,550.

**Training.** I trained all three paralegals the group has hired in the last two years. Supervising the paralegal team is central to the senior role, and helping new paralegals get up to speed is a large part of that work.

**Systems.** In 2023 I built the group's tracking sheet for court deadlines. Two other groups in the firm have since started using it.

That sheet is also where I made my most serious mistake. Last March I entered a filing deadline incorrectly. Another paralegal caught it, and we filed two hours before the court's cutoff. Afterward I added a second-person check to the sheet, so no deadline now depends on a single person's entry. That experience affects how I would supervise: I would build checks into the process instead of expecting anyone to be perfect.

As Senior Paralegal, I would:

- Supervise and support the group's paralegals, building on the training I've already done
- Keep improving the group's deadline and case-management systems, and help other groups adopt them
- Continue carrying a full caseload for the lawyers I support

I understand the role comes with supervisory responsibility and a higher salary, and I'm ready for both. I'd welcome the chance to talk about this at a time that suits you.

Thank you for considering my request.

Best regards,

[Your name]

A few notes:

- **The deadline mistake:** I included it because your managing partner probably already knows about it. When you bring it up yourself and point to the fix, it reads as accountability. If you'd rather leave it out, delete that paragraph and the letter still works. The tracking sheet's adoption by other groups can stand on its own.
- **Salary:** I mentioned the higher salary without giving the $9,000 figure. This keeps the focus on the role. If you want to be more direct about pay, you could name the amount.
- **Supervision:** I didn't say you already supervise anyone, because training isn't the same thing. If you've informally overseen the newer paralegals' work, adding a concrete example would make the letter stronger.
