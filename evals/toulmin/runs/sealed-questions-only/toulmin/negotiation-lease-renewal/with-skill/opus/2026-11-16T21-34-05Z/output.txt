I wrote $5,100 as a proposal, not as your final number, so you can still go a little higher later if you decide to.

I left out the coffee chain on purpose. You don't know anything about them, and a coffee shop probably wouldn't need your hood, so arguing about them would weaken the letter. The hood is described as being worth something to any restaurant tenant instead.

The letter also points out that your offer passes his number by year five. With 3% increases, $5,100 grows to about $5,740.

Subject: Lease renewal for Pho Lan

Dear Frank,

Thank you for sending the renewal terms. Pho Lan has been in your building for ten years, and we'd like to stay for at least five more.

I'd like to propose $5,100 a month for a new 5-year lease, with 3% increases each year after the first. That would bring the rent to about $5,740 by year five, which is above the $5,650 you asked for. I want to be honest with you: at $5,650 from the start, the numbers don't work for the restaurant.

A few things I hope you'll consider:

- The unit two doors down has been listed for 14 months at $28 a square foot. Your number for our space works out to about $29.
- In 2019 we put about $70,000 into the kitchen, including a hood system that stays with the building. A new restaurant tenant would pay around $40,000 to install one.
- I know we paid late twice in early 2024. Our walk-in cooler failed and we had to close for nine days. I'm grateful you waived the late fees, and we've paid on time every month since.

To make this easy for you, I can:

- sign the new lease within two weeks of agreeing on terms
- pay rent by automatic bank transfer on the 1st of each month

Could you let me know your answer before June 1? I'm happy to meet in person if that's easier.

Thank you,

Pho Lan
</final_document>
