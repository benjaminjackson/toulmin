Good evening. I'm Captain Ray Dunn. I've run the Sea Lark out of [harbor name] for 22 years, about 160 trips a season.

I'm not here to argue with your staff's numbers. The stock is at 78 percent of target, and it needs to rebuild. I'm asking you to rebuild it in a way that doesn't hit hardest the boats that take the smallest share.

Charter boats land about 9 percent of the state's summer flounder. Private boats land about 80 percent. But our customers pay for a day of fishing, and what they can take home is a big part of what they're paying for.

I've been through this before. After the 2016 cut, my bookings fell from 170 trips to 138 the next season. That's 32 trips, close to 29,000 dollars, on one boat. Two years later I was back to 155, still short of where I started.

This time our charter association asked 70 past customers. Forty-one said they'd book fewer trips at two fish. Only 12 said they'd book the same.

There are 11 charter boats in our harbor. Two other captains have told me that if bookings drop like they did in 2016, they'll sell. I can't tell you how many boats would close. I can tell you some of us are that close to the edge.

So here's what I'm asking. Set the limit at three fish, or shorten the season instead of cutting to two.

And here's what I'll offer. My trip logs from 2016 to today, open to your staff. And our association is ready to test a catch-reporting app with your staff next season, so you'll have better charter data than you have now.

Thank you.

Some notes:

- **"Out of business":** I didn't use that phrase. Instead the speech says two captains told you they'd sell, that you can't say how many boats would close, and that some of you are "that close to the edge." Everything there is true. Admitting what you don't know should also make the rest easier to believe.
- **The association:** you told me they can commit to testing the app, but you're not sure you speak for them. Check with them before the hearing. If you can't, change that line to "our association has told me it's willing to..."
- **The $29,000:** that's 32 lost trips × $900. It's money from bookings, not profit, so the speech calls it bookings.
- **I left out that 70% of next season is already booked.** It could sound like demand is strong no matter what the limit is. If the commission asks, you can honestly say those customers booked expecting four fish.
- **For your Q&A:** if you can work out how many trips a season you need to break even, that's the number most likely to change minds. It's also the honest way to back up "close to the edge."
