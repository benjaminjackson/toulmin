Good evening. I'm Captain Ray Dunn. I've run the Sea Lark out of [harbor name] for 22 years, about 160 trips a season.

I'm not here to argue with your staff's numbers. The stock is at 78 percent of target, and it needs to rebuild. I'm asking you to rebuild it in a way that doesn't hit hardest the boats that take the smallest share.

Charter boats land about 9 percent of the state's summer flounder. Private boats land about 80 percent. But our customers pay for a day of fishing, and what they can take home is a big part of what they're paying for.
