I've written this to fit two minutes. It doesn't say every boat will close, because you can't back that up and a commissioner who knows the 2016 recovery would catch it. It argues from what you do know: your own losses, the survey, and the two captains who say they'd sell.

<final_document>
Good evening, Commissioners. I'm Captain Ray Dunn. I've run the Sea Lark out of [harbor name] for 22 years, about 160 trips a season.

I'm not here to argue with your staff's science. If the stock is at 78% of target, it needs help. I'm asking you to get it there without breaking the charter boats that bring people to this coast.

Here's what the last cut did. In 2016 the limit came down. The next season my bookings fell from 170 trips to 138. That's 32 trips, close to $29,000 at today's rate, gone from one boat in one year. It took two years to get back to 155, and I've never gotten back to 170.

I expect the same this time. Our charter association asked 70 past customers. Forty-one said they'd book fewer trips at two fish. Only 12 said they'd book the same. Two captains in my harbor have told me that if they lose bookings like they did in 2016, they'll sell. I can't tell you how many of our 11 boats would close. I can tell you it won't be none.

Charter boats land about 9% of this state's summer flounder. Private boats land about 80%. Our share of what this cut saves is small. Our share of what it costs is not.

So we're asking for three fish, or a shorter season, instead of two. And we'll help you find out whether it works. I'll give your staff my trip logs from 2016 on. Our association will test a catch-reporting app with your staff next season.

Let us help fix this without carrying more of the cost than our share. Thank you.
</final_document>

Notes:

- **Length:** It's about 290 words, which takes roughly two minutes at a steady pace. Time yourself reading it out loud. If you run long, cut the sentence "I'm asking you to get it there without breaking…"
- **The $29,000 figure** is 32 trips at today's $900 rate. If you charged less in 2016, keep the "at today's rate" wording so nobody can say you inflated it.
- **I left out the 70% of trips already booked.** A commissioner could hear it as "he's doing fine." If you're asked, the answer that helps you is that those customers booked expecting four fish, and you don't yet know how many will cancel.
- **Be ready for questions about the survey.** Nobody has accounted for the other 17 answers, so if you're asked, say you'll get the full results from the association. Don't guess. Before the hearing, check whether you can say how the 70 customers were chosen.
- **If other captains come,** have them stand when you mention the 11 boats.
