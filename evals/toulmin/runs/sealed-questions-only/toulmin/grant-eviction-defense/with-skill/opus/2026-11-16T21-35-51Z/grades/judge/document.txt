**Project Summary: Tenant Defense Project**

The Tenant Defense Project requests $90,000 over two years ($45,000 a year) to hire a third staff attorney to represent tenants facing eviction.

**Need.** Last year 1,140 eviction cases were filed in our county. With two attorneys, we represented the tenant in 163 of them, about one in seven. We turned away roughly 300 more tenants because no attorney was free to take their case. Each of our attorneys carries about 80 open cases.

**Results.** In the cases we handled, 58% ended with the tenant staying in the home or getting at least 60 days to move. We do not yet have a comparable figure for tenants without a lawyer in our county. Starting in January, we will request court-wide outcome data from the county clerk so that our results can be measured against that baseline.

**Project.** The new attorney will take cases from tenants we now have to turn away. One of our current attorneys leaves in June and has agreed to spend her last month training the new hire.

**Budget.** The position costs $78,000 a year with benefits, or $156,000 over two years. This grant would cover $90,000. A family fund has pledged $20,000 a year for both years, and we will cover the remaining $13,000 a year from our general budget. Separately, our $60,000 annual county contract is up for renewal in October, and renewal is not yet certain.

**Accountability.** Every six months we will report to the foundation the number of cases opened and how each one ended.
