**Project Summary: Tenant Defense Project**

Last year, 1,140 eviction cases were filed in [County]. The Tenant Defense Project represented the tenant in 163 of them. We turned away about 300 more tenants because no attorney was available. Each of our two attorneys carries about 80 open cases.

In June, one of those two attorneys is leaving. Unless we hire, we will drop to one attorney and be able to represent about half as many tenants. We request $90,000 over two years ($45,000 per year) toward a full-time staff attorney so that tenants facing eviction in [County] can still get a lawyer.

**Outcomes.** In cases where we represented the tenant, 58% ended with the tenant staying in the home or getting at least 60 days to move. We do not yet have a comparable figure for tenants without a lawyer in our county. Starting in January, we will request court-wide outcome data from the county clerk so we can measure the difference representation makes.

**Plan.** The departing attorney has agreed to spend her last month training the new hire. Based on current caseloads, the new attorney will carry about 80 cases. Every six months we will report to the Foundation on cases opened and how they ended.

**Budget.** The position costs $78,000 per year with benefits. Of that, the Foundation's award would cover $45,000, a family fund has pledged $20,000 per year for both years, and the remaining $13,000 comes from our general budget. Our general budget includes a $60,000 county contract that is up for renewal in October.

Maria Chen, Managing Attorney
