[Clinic Name] requests $90,000 over two years ($45,000 per year) to hire a third staff attorney to represent low-income tenants facing eviction in [County].

**The need.** Last year, 1,140 eviction cases were filed in [County]. Our two attorneys represented the tenant in 163 of them, about 14 percent.
