**What you need to fill in:**

1. **The $33,000 a year gap.** The foundation will see right away that its maximum award doesn't cover the salary. You need to name where the rest comes from, or say the position will be part-time. The award is per year, so you can't make up the difference by asking for more in total.
2. **The attorney leaving in June.** If that position isn't refilled, this grant gets you back to two attorneys, not three. Calling it a "third attorney" would then be inaccurate, and the foundation would likely find out. If you can't yet say how you'll refill it, the project should be described honestly as keeping your current capacity, which is still a fundable request.
3. **The county contract.** It's now November, so if the renewal was due last month, give the result. A funder will notice an "up for renewal" date that has already passed.
