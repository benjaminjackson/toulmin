**Bring Back Our Cinema**

Since 2022, the only cinema in our town has stood dark. If you want to see a film on a big screen now, you have to drive 38 miles each way. For families, teenagers and older neighbours who no longer drive at night, that usually means not going at all.

We think the town can do better, and we have a plan to make it happen.

**The plan**

The building's owner has agreed to rent us the cinema for **$1 a year for 10 years**. To reopen, we need **$85,000** to replace the projector and sound system and repair the roof.

A local bank has pledged **$25,000** if we raise the remaining **$60,000 by June 30**. Every dollar you give helps unlock that pledge.

**Why we think it will work**

When the cinema closed, it was still selling about **180 tickets a week**. People in this town want to go to the movies. They just need a cinema close by.

**46 volunteers** have already signed up to run the box office and snack bar. Because volunteers do that work and the rent is $1 a year, the money we raise goes into the building, not overhead.

**About last time**

Some of you backed our campaign three years ago. It aimed for $120,000 and reached $47,000. When it fell short, we refunded every contributor.

This campaign is different. The goal is smaller and more focused, a bank is matching our effort, a 10-year lease is ready, and the volunteers are already lined up. We learned from that campaign, and we kept faith with everyone who supported it.

**What we're asking**

Please give what you can before June 30 and share this page with anyone who has ever bought popcorn here. Helping us reach $60,000 secures the bank's $25,000 and reopens the cinema for at least the next ten years.

Let's turn the lights back on, together.
