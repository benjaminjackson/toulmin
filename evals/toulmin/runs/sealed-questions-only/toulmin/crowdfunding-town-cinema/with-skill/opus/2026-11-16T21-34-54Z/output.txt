**Bring Back the Majestic**

The Majestic on Main Street closed in 2022. Right up to the end it was selling about 180 tickets a week. Since then, the nearest cinema has been 38 miles away.

We want to reopen it, and this time we have a plan that can get there.

**What we need**

We need $85,000 to replace the projector and sound system and to fix the roof.

**Why this time is different**

Three years ago we asked for $120,000. You pledged $47,000. It wasn't enough, so we gave every dollar back.

Since then:

- The building's owner has agreed to rent us the Majestic for $1 a year for ten years once we raise the money.
- 46 volunteers have signed up to run the box office and the snack bar.
- A local bank has pledged $25,000 if we raise the rest by June 30.
- Three local businesses have pledged $11,000 in writing.

That leaves $49,000 to raise from the public. Last time, this town pledged $47,000.

**If we fall short**

This is all-or-nothing. If we don't reach $85,000 by June 30, every pledge is returned in full, just like last time.

**If we succeed**

We plan to reopen by Thanksgiving. Every three months we'll post a public report at the town library showing how every dollar was spent.

**How you can help**

Pledge what you can by June 30. Then tell a neighbor, especially one who remembers a Friday night at the Majestic.

We're close enough this time to do it.

Ruth Albers
Chair, Majestic volunteer group

A few things to look at before it goes out:

- **The $49,000 figure** is $85,000 minus the bank's $25,000 and the businesses' $11,000. I put it next to last time's $47,000 because that comparison is the strongest point you have. Please check that the businesses' $11,000 counts toward the total and isn't on top of it.
- **The roof estimate could go up by as much as 15%.** I didn't put this in the pitch because I don't know what you'd do if it happens. If you have a plan, like a contingency or a cap, one line saying so would protect you later. The quarterly library reports partly cover this already.
- **Things I left out because you don't know them yet:** why the cinema closed, a break-even budget, rewards, tax status and the bank's name. A pitch this short works without them. But the biggest question backers will have is "won't it just close again?", and a simple running-costs estimate would answer it.
- **Films:** the pitch makes no promise about new releases or what will play.
- **Your title:** "Majestic volunteer group" is a placeholder. Swap in your group's real name if it has one.
