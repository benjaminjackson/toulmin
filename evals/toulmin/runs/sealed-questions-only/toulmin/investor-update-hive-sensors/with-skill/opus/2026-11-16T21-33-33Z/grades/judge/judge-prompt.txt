**Subject: HiveSense half-year update, and a bridge round we'd like you in**

Hi all,

Here's where HiveSense stands at the half year, and what we're asking of you.

**In short:** We now have 31 paying beekeepers, up from 12 a year ago. We're raising a $600,000 bridge on a convertible note and want our current investors in it. If you'd like to join, please reply by December 1.

**What went well**

- **Customers:** 31 commercial beekeepers are paying us, compared with 12 a year ago.
- **Revenue:** $212,000 for the half.
- **Unit economics:** Each sensor costs us $38 to make and sells for $55. On top of that, the app brings in $4 a month for every hive, for as long as the hive stays connected.
- **Our largest customer:** He runs 4,000 hives. Last winter he lost 18% of his colonies, against 31% the winter before. He also changed his mite treatment in that time, so we can't claim the whole improvement. He does count our sensors as part of it.

**What you should know**

- **Our revenue is seasonal.** 70% of the half's revenue came in March and April, when beekeepers get ready for pollination. Summer and autumn are thin, and most of next year's revenue will come in spring.
- **Cash.** We spend about $95,000 a month and have $340,000 in the bank. Without new money, we run out around the end of March 2027. That is the middle of the season that brings in most of our revenue.
- **Competition.** In May, a large farm equipment company launched a hive sensor at $45. Theirs measures only temperature. Ours measures temperature, sound and hive weight. No customer has left us or asked for a lower price. Three of our beekeepers have asked to test the competing sensor, and I'll tell you what they find.

**What we're doing about it**

We're bringing monthly spending down to $75,000 by February by leaving two open roles unfilled.

With that cut and the $600,000 bridge, our cash lasts to about November 2027, and that's before counting any revenue. It carries us through the whole 2027 spring season and leaves time afterwards to show what that season produced.

I'm not going to promise you profit next year, because I don't yet have a break-even plan I'd stand behind. What I can promise is this: everyone who joins the bridge will get a cash report every month, showing what's in the bank, what we spent and what came in. You'll see how we're doing as it happens, not twice a year.

Score the document on the seven items below, each from 0 to 10, and give each score one sentence of reason.

1. agree: would this reader, including the one who resists, come to agree or act as the writer wants?
2. honest: no claim is stated more strongly than the facts in the brief carry, and no fact or number is invented.
3. works_the_facts: figures are derived from what the brief gave (rates, totals, the pace a target needs against the pace already reached, what happens if nothing changes), and each is put in its most favourable true form.
4. right_comparison: the alternative to what is proposed is described as the facts show it, including any trend.
5. qualification: any condition or hedge names something that could turn out either way and that the reader can check, with a date, a number and a consequence if it is missed. Give a score of null when the document needs no qualification.
6. objections: the resisting reader's objection is met, and no objection is raised that the facts in the brief already answer.
7. fit: the document has the form and length the brief asked for.

Use the whole scale from 0 to 10. A 5 is an ordinary competent draft.
